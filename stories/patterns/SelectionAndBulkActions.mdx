## implementation notes

- the selection is the app's: `DataTable` takes `selectable`, `selected` (a set of row keys) and `onSelectionChange`, and hands the next set to it. it doesn't prune `selected` when `rows` change; keep, drop or clear the selection yourself when a filter, a search or a tab changes the list.
- `isRowSelectable` gives a row a disabled checkbox and keeps it out of every way of selecting; `rowLabel` names each row's checkbox ("select S04E07").
- `onRowPress` makes a click outside a row's controls, and `enter`, open the row; without it, a click on the row toggles its checkbox.
- rows carry `aria-selected`, and the table `aria-multiselectable`.
- `SelectionToolbar` takes `selectedCount` and `onClearSelection`; `primaryAction(n)` returns the emphasized action for exactly that selection (label and handler), or `null` while nothing is selected; `actions` are never emphasized (`overflow: true` puts one in the "more actions" menu); `selectAll` offers "select all n"; `formatSelected` words the count. it announces each change of the count politely, never its first state.
- a group's action is `groupAction(group)` on the table, next to `groupBy` and `groupHeader`.
- the toast comes from `toast()`, shown by the one `<Toaster />` near the app's root.
