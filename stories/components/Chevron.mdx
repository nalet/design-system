## implementation notes

- `Chevron` renders an inline `<svg>` with one path, `M5 3 L11 8 L5 13`, in a 16-unit square.
- The path is stroked in `currentColor`, with rounded caps and join.
- Its colour, `--cloud-blue`, comes from its own class.
- The `color` prop can override it, but the brand says not to.
- `size` is the edge length in px, 16 by default.
- The stroke is `strokeWidth` px. Without it, the stroke is 14% of `size`, but never thinner than 1.5px, so the mark keeps its weight from 14px to 128px.
- It renders `role="img"` with `aria-label="chevron"`.
- Next to a title it is decoration, so pass `aria-hidden`, as every use inside the library does.
- Other `<svg>` attributes and its `ref` reach the `<svg>`.
