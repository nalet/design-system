# multi select

A facet that keeps several values: a trigger that shows the facet's name and what is chosen — `state`, `2 chosen` — and a panel of checkboxes. It is a disclosure, not a menu: `tab` moves through the checkboxes, and `esc` or a press outside closes it.

<Example label="a multi select, live">
  <Playground client:visible />
</Example>

## when to use

- In a <C slug="filter-bar" />, for a facet that can keep several values at once: `state` — `missing` and `on its way`.
- For a handful of options that fit a small panel, each with a count of what it would keep, when you know it.

## when not to use

- **For one value at a time** — a kind, a time range. Use a <C slug="select" />; the filter bar takes those as `select` facets.
- **For a setting, or a form that submits.** Use <C slug="checkbox">checkboxes</C> in a <C slug="field" />. A form sends none of the multi select's choices.
- **For many options.** The panel has no search and no maximum height. Let people type in the <C slug="filter-bar">filter bar</C>'s search instead.
- **To run commands.** Use an <C slug="action-menu" />.

## anatomy

1. **Trigger** — a button 34px tall (`md`, the default) or 28px (`sm`), with 0 × 12px padding (8px at `sm`), a 1px `--border-control` frame on `--bg-2`, square corners, JetBrains Mono 13px (12px at `sm`). It holds:
   - The facet's **label**, in `--fg-muted`, lowercase;
   - The **summary**: the empty text (`any`) while nothing is chosen, the option's label when one is, `2 chosen` when several are. It turns `--cloud-blue` once something is chosen, and ends in an ellipsis when it is long;
   - A 12px **chevron** in `--cloud-blue`, which turns over while the panel is open.
2. **Panel** — 4px under the trigger, lined up with its left edge: `--surface` fill, a 1px `--border-2` frame, a shadow, 8px of padding above and below. At least as wide as the trigger, at most 320px (or the viewport minus 16px).
3. **Options** — a group named by the facet's label, with one <C slug="checkbox" /> per option, 8px × 12px padding each, `--surface-2` on hover. An option's label is a filter value, in JetBrains Mono 13px, as the trigger shows it. An option's count, when you give one, follows its label in `--fg-dim`, mono 12px, tabular figures.
4. **Clear** — `clear` and the facet's label (`clear state`), at the bottom under a 1px `--border` rule, only while something is chosen.

## variants and states

| state | the trigger shows |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing chosen | The label, then the empty text, `any` |
| One chosen | The label, then that option's label in `--cloud-blue` |
| Several chosen | The label, then `2 chosen` in `--cloud-blue` |
| Open | The chevron turned over, and the panel under it |
| Disabled | 50% opacity. It can't be focused or opened |

## behaviour

- A disclosure: the trigger shows and hides a panel of checkboxes. Opening it doesn't move focus.
- A click on the trigger opens or closes the panel; a press anywhere outside closes it, and so does focus leaving the multi select.
- Every change applies at once. There is no apply button.
- The arrow keys do nothing special: each checkbox is its own tab stop.

The keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-inputs-multi-select--docs).

## accessibility

- The trigger's name is its text: the facet's label, then the summary — "state 2 chosen". Write the label so the two read as a phrase.
- The checkboxes are announced as one group, named by the facet's label. Each is named by its option's label and count.
- A closed panel is not read and not focusable.
- Focus is never moved for you, except back to the trigger on `esc`.
- The trigger and `clear` show a 2px `--focus-ring` outline; each checkbox shows its own.

Today:

- `clear` closes the panel and moves focus back to the trigger: the button itself goes once nothing is chosen.
- `clear` is fixed English — "clear" and the label — and can't be translated.
- The panel always opens below, from the trigger's left edge, with no flip near the viewport's edges, and it has no maximum height.

## content

- **Label** — the facet's name, a noun: `state`, `aired`, `kind`.
- **Options** — the values in the list's words, in an order that means something (the states in their usual order, not sorted by count): `missing`, `on its way`, `needs attention`.
- **Counts** — how many items each option would keep, when you know it.
- **Empty text** — what no choice means: `any`, the default, or `all states`.
