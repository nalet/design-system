# box

A box is the simplest building block of a layout: a square frame that puts space around its content. It can also give that content a background and a thin border.

<Example label="a box with padding, a background and a border">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- To put space around a part of the page in the fixed steps of the space scale, instead of writing your own <Term id="css">CSS</Term>.
- To set a part of the page on another shade of the <Term id="canvas">canvas</Term>, such as a darker, sunken area inside a panel.
- To frame something with a <Term id="hairline">hairline</Term>, when the header and footer of a card would be too much.

## when to use something else

- **For a group of content with a title.** Use a <C slug="card" />: the header, the line below it, the footer and the usual padding come with it.
- **For the space between things that sit side by side or one above another.** Use a <C slug="stack" /> and its gap. Padding or margins on each of them drift off the scale.
- **For a line between sections.** Use a <C slug="divider" />.
- **For something people click.** A box isn't a control: it can't take <Term id="focus">focus</Term>, and the keyboard can't use it. An action is a <C slug="button" />; an entry on the <Term id="home">home page</Term> is a <C slug="tile" />.

## anatomy

1. **Frame.** Square and never rounded, like every frame (see [shape](/design-system/styles/shape/#square-frames)). It has no padding, background or border unless you ask for them.
2. **Padding** (optional). Space inside the frame: on every side, on the left and right, or on the top and bottom. Each is a step of the [space scale](/design-system/foundations/layout/#the-scale).
3. **Background** (optional). One of the four shades of the canvas.
4. **Border** (optional). A hairline on every side.

## variants and states

Padding comes in the steps of the scale, and there is no default: a box has no padding until you give it some.

</Example>

The left and right can take a different step from the top and bottom.

  <AsymmetricPadding />
</Example>

The background is one of the four shades of the canvas. Without one, the box is transparent, and whatever is behind it shows through.

Boxes nest: a darker, sunken area can sit inside a lighter panel.

## behaviour

- A box takes no focus and has no action. <Kbd>tab</Kbd> passes over it, and moves through what is inside it, in order.

The full keyboard map is on the box's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-box--docs).

## accessibility

- A box adds no structure for <Term id="assistive-technology">assistive technology</Term>: it has no role and no name.
- <Term id="landmark">Landmarks</Term> and other named regions are up to you. A region that people should be able to find by its name needs a role, and that name.
- The border is a decorative hairline, faint on purpose: it has less <Term id="contrast">contrast</Term> than the edge of a control needs (see [shape](/design-system/styles/shape/#lines)). So a box's border must never be the only thing that marks a control or a selected item.
- A background changes nothing else. The text inside keeps its own colour, and every text colour has enough contrast on all four backgrounds.

## content

A box has no text of its own. Keep what is inside it about one thing. If it needs a title, it is a <C slug="card" />.

## specs

| setting | in code | what it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Padding on every side | `p` | 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 or 64; the steps from 4 to 64 resolve to `--s-1` … `--s-8`. No default. |
| Padding on the left and right | `px` | The same steps, applied after `p`. |
| Padding on the top and bottom | `py` | The same steps, applied after `p`: `p={16} px={24}` gives 16px on the top and bottom and 24px on the left and right. |
| Background | `bg="bg"`, `bg="bg-2"`, `bg="surface"` or `bg="surface-2"` | `--bg`, `--bg-2`, `--surface` or `--surface-2`. Without `bg`, the box is transparent. A sunken area is a `bg="bg-2"` box inside a `bg="surface"` one. |
| Border | `border` | A 1px `--border` hairline on every side. |

- **Frame:** a `div` with `box-sizing: border-box` and `border-radius: 0`, never rounded. No padding, background or border unless they are set.
- **Contrast:** the `--border` hairline sits below 3:1 by design. Every text colour meets 4.5:1 on all four backgrounds.
- **Role and name:** none. For a landmark or a named region, pass a `role` with an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- **Focus:** none. `tab` skips the box and moves through what is inside it, in order.
