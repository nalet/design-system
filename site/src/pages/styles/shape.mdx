# shape

Every frame is square, every line is a pixel wide, and surfaces lie flat on the canvas. The only rounded shapes are the icons.

## square frames

`border-radius` is 0 on every card, panel, tile, button, input, menu, toast, tooltip and dialog — the components set it themselves, and the Tailwind preset's default radius is 0 too. No rounded corners, no pills, no circles behind an icon or a count. A square frame reads like a terminal pane: it lines up with its neighbours and with the grid of the text.

## lines

| line | token | where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hairline, 1px | `--border` | Cards, tables, dividers, the edge of a region |
| Heavier hairline, 1px | `--border-2` | The edge of what floats: menus, tooltips, toasts, dialogs; a default button |
| Control boundary, 1px | `--border-control` | Inputs, selects, textareas, checkboxes, the switch track: it meets 3:1, so the control is visible |
| State border, 1px | `--state-*-border` | The frame of a message strip or a status chip, around its tint |

`--border` and `--border-2` are decoration and sit outside the contrast policy; a control is never identified by them alone — see [colour](/design-system/styles/colour/#contrast).

## rules

A thicker line on one edge marks what is current or what something is about:

- **2px `--cloud-blue` on the leading edge** — a selected table row, the current tile, a focused menu item (in `--focus-ring`).
- **2px `--cloud-blue` under the label** — the current tab.
- **3px in a state's colour on the leading edge** — a message strip, a toast, a highlighted table row.
- **3px `--cloud-blue` on the leading edge** — the selection toolbar, while something is selected.

A rule always comes with words: the row also has its checked box, the strip its message, the tab its label.

## surfaces

- Surfaces are flat. A card, a panel or a tile is a fill (`--surface`) and a hairline, never a shadow.
- What floats over the page is set apart by a heavier hairline. The two menus that float over content — the <C slug="action-menu" /> and the <C slug="multi-select" /> panel — also cast one soft shadow (`0 8px 24px` of black at 45%), so they read as above the rows they cover.
- A <C slug="modal" /> sits on a backdrop of the canvas at 72%, which dims the page behind it; it casts no shadow.
- Depth comes from the canvas steps — `--bg`, `--bg-2`, `--surface`, `--surface-2` — not from elevation.

## the one rounded shape

Icons are the only rounded shape. Lucide draws them with round caps and joins, and the app icon carries its rounded corners in the PNG. Everything around an icon stays square: no `border-radius`, no circle or pill behind it. The tile's 28px icon box is square and unfilled.
