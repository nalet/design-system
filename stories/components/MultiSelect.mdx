## implementation notes

- It is controlled: pass `value` and `onChange`.
- Every change calls `onChange` at once, with the new list of values in the order they were chosen.
- `clear` calls `onChange([])`.
- Values in `value` that aren't in `options` stay in `value`, but the summary doesn't count them.
- The trigger is a `<button type="button">` with `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` pointing at the panel.
- The trigger has no `aria-haspopup`, because it opens a group of checkboxes, not a menu or a listbox.
- The options are a `fieldset`, named by the facet's label in a visually hidden `legend`, so a screen reader announces them as one group.
- Each option is a native [checkbox](?path=/docs/components-inputs-checkbox--docs).
- The checkboxes have no `name`, so a form sends none of them.
- A closed panel is `hidden`, so it is neither read nor focusable.
- Today: the panel isn't rendered into `document.body`, so an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` cuts it off. It is positioned under the trigger, at `z-index: 120`.
- The options sit in a part of their own (`.nc-multiselect__list`) that scrolls once they are taller than 320px (`20rem`), with `overscroll-behavior: contain`, so scrolling them doesn't scroll the page. The foot is outside it.
- When the panel opens, and on any scroll or resize while it is open, it measures itself: it opens above the trigger when it wouldn't fit below and there is more room above, and where neither side holds it, the options get a smaller largest height, so the panel keeps 8px from the viewport's top and bottom edges.
- `action` takes a `label` and an `onSelect`. The action is a `<button type="button">` in the foot, before `clear`, with a plus icon before its label. Pressing it closes the panel and focuses the trigger, then calls `onSelect`, so a modal it opens returns focus to the trigger when it closes.
- `emptyText` replaces “any”. “any”, “2 chosen” and “clear …” come from the page's language, and counts are written in it: a `<Language>` around the page, or the page's own `lang`, sets it. [Content and voice](https://nalet.github.io/design-system/foundations/content-voice/) has the rules.
- Inside `data-nc-screen="touch"`, the trigger is drawn as at `size="lg"` with a touch area of at least 48 × 48, and the options, the action and `clear` are at least 48px tall.
- Inside `data-nc-screen="tv"`, the trigger is drawn as at `size="lg"`, the rows are at least 40px tall with 14px labels, and the focused trigger or row shows the TV focus. While the panel is open, `↓` and `↑` move through the trigger, the checkboxes, the action and `clear`, and `enter` on a checkbox checks or unchecks it.
- `MultiSelect` passes no other attributes through.
- `className` goes on the wrapper around the trigger and the panel.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `enter` / `space` on the trigger | Opens or closes the panel. Focus stays on the trigger. |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | Native: from the trigger into the checkboxes, then the action, then `clear`, then out. When focus leaves the multi select, the panel closes. |
| `space` on a checkbox | Native: checks or unchecks it. |
| `esc` | Closes the panel and puts focus back on the trigger. The key goes no further, so a modal around it stays open. |
| `enter` / `space` on the action | Closes the panel, puts focus back on the trigger, then runs the action. |
| `↓` / `↑`, on a TV only | While the panel is open: to the next / previous of the trigger, the checkboxes, the action and `clear`, stopping at the ends. |
| `enter` on a checkbox, on a TV only | Checks or unchecks it, as `space` does. |
