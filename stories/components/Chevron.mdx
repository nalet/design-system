## implementation notes

- `Chevron` renders an inline `<svg>`: one path, `M5 3 L11 8 L5 13`, in a 16-unit square, stroked in `currentColor` with rounded caps and join.
- its colour comes from its own class, `--cloud-blue`. the `color` prop overrides it; the brand says don't.
- `size` is the edge length in px (16 by default). the stroke is `strokeWidth` px, or 14% of `size` with a 1.5px floor.
- it renders `role="img"` with `aria-label="chevron"`. next to a title it is decoration: pass `aria-hidden`, as every use inside the library does.
- other `<svg>` attributes and its `ref` reach the `<svg>`.
