# tooltip

a small square label with a short, optional detail about what is under the pointer or the keyboard focus. the bubble shows while its trigger is hovered or has keyboard focus.

{/* the example frames clip what overflows them: a trigger in the middle leaves its bubble room on both sides. */}

  <div style={{ margin: '0 auto' }}>
    <Playground />
  </div>

## when to use

- to give the detail behind a compact value or term: the window behind `142 ms`, the full time behind `3 min ago`, what an abbreviation stands for.
- for information people can do without. anything they need to finish a task belongs on the screen.

## when not to use

- **to name an icon-only control.** an <C slug="icon-button" /> takes a `label`, which becomes its accessible name and the browser's own tooltip.
- **for anything people need**: an error, a warning, the consequence of an action. there is no hover on touch screens, so people there never see the bubble. put it on the page — a <C slug="field" /> hint or error, a <C slug="message-strip" />.
- **for more than a short phrase.** the bubble doesn't wrap. write it on the page, or in a <C slug="modal" /> when it needs a decision.
- **for links, buttons or anything else to act on.** the bubble can't be hovered or focused, and it disappears when the pointer leaves the trigger. offer the actions on the page, or behind an <C slug="action-menu" />.

## anatomy

1. **wrapper** — around your trigger. it is a tab stop of its own, and the bubble is its description.
2. **trigger** — your content: a value or a term.
3. **bubble** — `--surface-2` fill, 1px `--border-2`, square, 4px × 8px padding; jetbrains mono 12px / 1.4 in `--fg`; one line, at most 240px wide. it sits 6px from the trigger, centred on it, with no arrow.

## variants and states

the bubble goes `top` (the default), `bottom`, `left` or `right` of the trigger, centred along it. nothing flips or shifts it near the edge of the viewport, and a container that clips its overflow cuts it off: choose the side with room.

the bubble fades in over 100ms when the trigger is hovered or gets keyboard focus, and hides at once when both end. there is no delay before it shows.

triggers can sit inside running text:

</Example>

## behaviour

- hovering the wrapper shows the bubble, and leaving it hides it. the bubble ignores the pointer, so it can't be hovered, selected or clicked.
- the wrapper is a tab stop: keyboard focus on it shows the bubble, and the bubble hides when focus leaves it.

the keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs).

## accessibility

- the bubble describes the wrapper, not your trigger.
- the wrapper always adds a tab stop. on a value or a term, that is what makes the detail reachable by keyboard. on a button it means two tab stops for one control, and the button itself gets no description.

today:

- the focused wrapper draws no focus ring; the bubble appearing is the only sign of focus.
- `esc` doesn't hide the bubble, and the pointer can't rest on it: two of the three things wcag 1.4.13 asks of content that appears on hover or focus. keep only optional detail in it.
- text longer than 240px doesn't wrap; it runs out of the bubble.

## content

- one short phrase, lowercase, no full stop: `p95 over the last 5 minutes`, `built 2026-06-15 09:12`.
- add to the trigger; don't repeat it. a tooltip that says what the trigger already says is noise.
- plain text only: no links, no buttons, nothing that has to be read in a particular order.

## do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="an optional detail behind a compact value.">
    <div style={{ margin: '0 auto' }}>
      <Tooltip content="p95 over the last 5 minutes"><Term>142 ms</Term></Tooltip>
    </div>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="the consequence hides behind hover: people on touch screens, and anyone who doesn't hover, never see it. say it on the page.">
    <div style={{ margin: '0 auto' }}>
      <Tooltip content="restarts every worker; requests fail for a minute"><Button variant="primary">deploy release</Button></Tooltip>
    </div>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="an icon button names itself: its label is the accessible name and the native tooltip.">
    <div style={{ margin: '0 auto' }}>
      <IconButton label="restart worker-2"><Icon icon={RotateCw} /></IconButton>
    </div>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="wrapped in a tooltip, it shows two tooltips (the native one and the bubble) and puts an extra tab stop in front of the button.">
    <div style={{ margin: '0 auto' }}>
      <Tooltip content="restart worker-2"><IconButton label="restart worker-2"><Icon icon={RotateCw} /></IconButton></Tooltip>
    </div>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="one short line.">
    <div style={{ margin: '0 auto' }}>
      <Tooltip content="3 of 3 replicas ready"><Term>worker-2</Term></Tooltip>
    </div>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="a sentence doesn't wrap: past 240px it runs out of the bubble. if it needs a sentence, it belongs on the page.">
    <div style={{ margin: '0 auto' }}>
      <Tooltip content="worker-2 runs 3 replicas; they restart one at a time after each release, and the last restart took 48 seconds"><Term>worker-2</Term></Tooltip>
    </div>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
