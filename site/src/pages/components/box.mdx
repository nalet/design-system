# box

the base layout primitive: a square container with padding from the space scale, an optional canvas background and an optional hairline border.

<Example label="a box with padding, a background and a border">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- to pad a region with the space scale instead of hand-written css.
- to set a region on another canvas, such as a sunken `bg-2` area inside a surface.
- to frame something with a hairline when a card's header and footer would be too much.

## when not to use

- **for a titled group of content.** use a <C slug="card" />: the header, its rule, the footer and the standard padding come with it.
- **for the space between siblings.** use a <C slug="stack" /> and its gap. padding or margins on each child drift off the scale.
- **for a line between sections.** use a <C slug="divider" />.
- **for something people click.** a box is not a control: no focus, no keyboard. an action is a <C slug="button" />; an entry on the launchpad is a <C slug="tile" />.

## anatomy

1. **container** — square, never rounded, like every frame (see [shape](/design-system/styles/shape/#square-frames)). no padding, background or border unless you ask.
2. **padding** (optional) — on every side, on the left and right, or on the top and bottom, each a step of the [space scale](/design-system/foundations/layout/#the-scale).
3. **background** (optional) — one of the four canvas colours: `--bg`, `--bg-2`, `--surface` or `--surface-2`.
4. **border** (optional) — a 1px `--border` hairline on every side.

## variants and states

padding comes in the steps of the scale; there is no default.

## behaviour

- a box takes no focus and has no action. `tab` skips it and moves through what's inside it, in order.

the keyboard map and the element it renders are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-box--docs).

## accessibility

- a box adds no structure for assistive tech: it has no role and no name.
- landmarks and regions are yours: a region that people should find by its name needs a role and that name.
- the border is a decorative hairline, below 3:1 by design (see [shape](/design-system/styles/shape/#lines)). a box's border must never be the only thing that marks a control or a selected item.
- a background changes nothing else: text inside keeps its own colour, and every text colour meets 4.5:1 on all four backgrounds.

## content

a box has no text of its own. keep what's inside it about one thing; if it needs a title, it is a <C slug="card" />.

## do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="space between boxes from the stack's gap: one value, on the scale.">
    <Stack gap={12}>
      <Box p={12} bg="surface-2" border><Text variant="ui">storage-01</Text></Box>
      <Box p={12} bg="surface-2" border><Text variant="ui">storage-02</Text></Box>
      <Box p={12} bg="surface-2" border><Text variant="ui">storage-03</Text></Box>
    </Stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="margins on each box drift off the scale and never quite match.">
    <div>
      <Box p={12} bg="surface-2" border style={{ marginBottom: '10px' }}><Text variant="ui">storage-01</Text></Box>
      <Box p={12} bg="surface-2" border style={{ marginBottom: '5px' }}><Text variant="ui">storage-02</Text></Box>
      <Box p={12} bg="surface-2" border><Text variant="ui">storage-03</Text></Box>
    </div>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="a titled group is a card: header, rule and padding come with it.">
    <Card header="backups">every night at 02:00, kept 14 days.</Card>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a box with a hand-made title is a card that drifts: other padding, no header rule, the title in the wrong type.">

        <Text variant="ui">backups</Text>
        <Text variant="muted">every night at 02:00, kept 14 days.</Text>
      </Stack>
    </Box>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
