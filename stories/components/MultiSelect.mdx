## implementation notes

- it is controlled: pass `value` and `onChange`. every change calls `onChange` at once, with the new list of values in the order they were chosen. `clear` calls `onChange([])`.
- values in `value` that aren't in `options` stay in `value`, but the summary doesn't count them.
- the trigger is a `<button type="button">` with `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` pointing at the panel. it has no `aria-haspopup`: it opens a group of checkboxes, not a menu or a listbox.
- the options are a `fieldset` named by the facet's label (a visually hidden `legend`), and each is a native [checkbox](?path=/docs/components-inputs-checkbox--docs). the checkboxes have no `name`, so a form sends none of them.
- a closed panel is `hidden`.
- today: the panel isn't rendered into `document.body`, so an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` cuts it off. it is positioned under the trigger, at `z-index: 120`.
- `MultiSelect` passes no other attributes through; `className` goes on the wrapper around the trigger and the panel.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `enter` / `space` on the trigger | opens or closes the panel. focus stays on the trigger. |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | native: from the trigger into the checkboxes, then `clear`, then out. when focus leaves the multi select, the panel closes. |
| `space` on a checkbox | native: checks or unchecks it. |
| `esc` | closes the panel and puts focus back on the trigger. the key goes no further, so a modal around it stays open. |
