# illustrated message

An illustrated message takes the place of a list or a panel that has nothing to show, and tells people why. A small line drawing, a title and a sentence say what is true and what they can do next.

<Example label="an illustrated message">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- In place of a list, a table or a panel that has nothing to show, once you know why. There are five reasons, each with its own kind of message: nothing is there yet, nothing is left to do, the filters match nothing, the list couldn't load, or it can't be told yet.
- At the size of what it replaces: the small one inside a panel or a table (a <C slug="data-table" /> shows it in its empty body), the medium one for a section, and the large one for a whole page.

## when to use something else

- **While the list is still loading.** Use the loading state of the <C slug="data-table" />, or a <C slug="spinner" />. Say “nothing here” only once you know it is true.
- **Above content that still shows.** For the rows from 4 minutes ago with an error on top, use a <C slug="message-strip" />. It sits above the content and leaves it in place.
- **For the state of one item, in a list that is otherwise there.** Use an <C slug="object-status" /> in its row.
- **To confirm an action that just finished.** Use a <C slug="toast" />, which says so and goes away.

## anatomy

1. **Container.** A centred column that holds the rest, with room around it that grows with the size.
2. **Illustration.** A small line drawing of a square terminal window, in quiet lines, with one accent in the colour of the kind. It is decoration, so <Term id="assistive-technology">assistive technology</Term> skips it.
3. **Title.** What is true, in a few words, in <Term id="monospace">monospace</Term> and shown in lowercase. It is a real heading, at the level that fits the page.
4. **Description** (optional). One or two sentences under the title, in the <Term id="body-face">body face</Term>: why, and what people can do.
5. **Actions** (optional). Buttons under the text, centred.

## variants and states

### kinds

There are five kinds, one for each reason there is nothing to show. Pick the kind by what is true, not by the picture.

| kind | the drawing | when | what it offers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Empty (the default) | A prompt with nothing typed after it, in blue | Nothing here yet: a new list, nothing tracked | The action that adds the first item, if there is one |
| Done | The prompt, and a check in green | Nothing left to do: the good end of a <Term id="worklist">worklist</Term> | Usually nothing |
| Search | Rows under a square lens that finds none, in blue | The search or the filters match nothing | “clear filters” |
| Error | A line that doesn't connect, in red | It couldn't load | “try again” |
| Unknown | A question that can't be answered yet, in amber | It can't be told yet, for example because the holdings are unknown | The step that makes it known, such as “ask the catalog again” |

<Example label="the five kinds">
  <Kinds />
</Example>

The empty kind and the done kind both mean “nothing here”, for opposite reasons: empty before anything has happened, done after everything has.

When the filters hide everything, use the search kind, not the empty one: the data is there, and the filters hide it. [Empty states](/design-system/patterns/empty-states/#which-kind) goes through which kind fits which situation, case by case.

### sizes

There are three sizes, to match what the message replaces: small, medium (the default) and large. The drawing, the title and the room around them grow with each size.

### actions

The actions are buttons: at most one for the <Term id="emphasized-action">emphasized action</Term>, and an optional second one, standard or quiet.

### heading level

The title is a level-2 heading, unless you give it another <Term id="heading-level">heading level</Term>, from 2 to 6. Match the page's outline: inside a section whose heading is level 2, make the title level 3.

## behaviour

- An illustrated message is static: people read it, and the only things in it that take <Term id="focus">focus</Term> are its actions.
- It doesn't announce itself when it appears. When it replaces a list because the filters changed, what a <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term> hears is the live count of results in the <C slug="filter-bar" />.

The full keyboard map is on the illustrated message's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-empty-state--docs).

## accessibility

- The illustration is decoration, hidden from assistive technology. The title says what is true.
- The title is a real heading, so people who move through a page by its headings find it. Give it the level that keeps the page's outline in order.
- The accent colour only repeats the kind. The words carry the meaning.

Today:

- Pressing the action usually removes the message, and the button with it, so focus falls back to the page: after “clear filters”, nothing has focus. Move focus yourself, to the search field for instance.
- Nothing holds you to one emphasized action: the message shows whatever actions it is given.

## content

- **Title.** What is true, in a few words: “nothing missing”, “no matches”, “couldn't load”, “nothing tracked yet”, “nothing can be checked yet”. Lowercase, with no full stop.
- **Description.** One or two sentences, in <Term id="sentence-case">sentence case</Term>: why, and what the person can do. “Nothing fits these filters.” “Import a list to start tracking.”
- **Actions.** One emphasized action at most. When the view already has its one emphasized action, in a toolbar for example, make this one a standard button.
- **Tone.** Calm: say what is true. No apology, no joke, no “oops”.
- **The way out.** Name it for each kind: “clear filters” for search, “try again” for error, and for unknown, the step that makes it known.

## specs

| kind | in code | accent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty | `illustration="empty"` (the default) | `--cloud-blue` |
| Done | `illustration="done"` | `--state-success` |
| Search | `illustration="search"` | `--cloud-blue` |
| Error | `illustration="error"` | `--state-error` |
| Unknown | `illustration="unknown"` | `--state-warning` |

| size | in code | padding | illustration | space under it | title |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | `size="sm"` | 16px 12px | 96px wide | 12px | 14px |
| Medium | `size="md"` (the default) | 32px 16px | 160px wide | 16px | 16px |
| Large | `size="lg"` | 64px 16px | 220px wide | 24px | 22px |

- **Container:** a centred column, with its text centred.
- **Illustration:** an SVG line drawing on a 160 × 120 grid, scaled to the size's width: the window frame in `--border-control`, quiet lines in `--border-2`, one accent in the kind's colour. `aria-hidden="true"` and `focusable="false"`.
- **Title:** `title`. A real heading, an `h2` unless `headingLevel` sets another level, from 2 to 6. JetBrains Mono 600, line-height 1.3, `--fg`, lowercased with `text-transform`.
- **Description:** `description`, a paragraph. Inter 13px, line-height 1.55, `--fg-muted`, at most 46ch wide, 8px under the title.
- **Actions:** `actions`. Centred, 8px apart, wrapping, 16px under the text. Buttons: at most one `variant="primary"`, and an optional second, `variant="default"` or `variant="ghost"`. Nothing enforces a single primary.
- **Announcing:** none. It is not a live region.
- **In a data table:** the data table's `empty` prop takes it, and shows it in the table's empty body.
