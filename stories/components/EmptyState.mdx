## implementation notes

- It renders a `<div>` as a centred column: the SVG illustration, the title, the description, the actions and, with `details`, the toggle and the details.
- `illustration` picks the picture: `empty` (the default), `done`, `search`, `error` or `unknown`.
- `title` is a heading: an `h2`, unless `headingLevel` (2 to 6) sets another level. Pick the level that fits the page around it.
- `description` is a `<p>` under the title.
- `actions` takes the buttons, and renders whatever it is given.
- `details` takes the technical part of something that went wrong. With it, a `<button type="button">` follows the actions, on a line of its own, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; the details are a `<div>` under it with the `hidden` attribute until the toggle is pressed. Without `details`, neither is rendered.
- The details are closed whenever the empty state mounts, and there is no prop to open them. Once opened, they stay open until someone closes them or the empty state unmounts.
- `detailsLabel` names the toggle. Without one, it is “details”, from the words of the page's language (`words.emptyState.details`; see `Language`).
- The details keep their line breaks (`white-space: pre-wrap`), and a long id breaks anywhere (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`). The block is at most 46ch wide, and its text starts at the left.
- On a touch screen (`data-nc-screen="touch"`), the toggle keeps its 28px look, answers to a touch in a row 48px high around it, and stands 16px under the actions. On a TV (`data-nc-screen="tv"`), it is 42px high with a 14px label and shows the TV focus, and the description is 14px.
- `size` is `sm`, `md` (the default) or `lg`.
- The SVG has `aria-hidden="true"` and `focusable="false"`, so the picture is decoration and the words carry the message.
- It is not a live region: it doesn't announce itself when it appears.
- A [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs) takes it in its `empty` prop.
- Other `<div>` attributes and the `ref` reach the container, all but `title`, which is the heading.
- axe-core finds no violations in any kind, with a title, a description and an action, or with details, closed and open.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | Moves to and from your actions, in order, then the details toggle. The picture, the title, the description and the details take no focus. |
| `enter` / `space` | Native: activates the focused action; on the toggle, shows or hides the details. |
