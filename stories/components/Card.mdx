# card

a square surface panel with a 1px frame, an optional mono header row (with an aside for actions) and an optional footer row. the default container for grouped content.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- to group content about one thing under a short title: a volume's details, a run's summary, a section of settings.
- to give a table or a list a frame and a title, with `flush`.
- with `sunken`, for a panel nested inside another surface.

## when not to use

- **for something people open or launch.** a card is not clickable. an entry on the launchpad is a [tile](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile--docs); an action is a [button](?path=/docs/components-actions-button--docs).
- **for padding or a background without a title.** use a [box](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-box--docs).
- **for a message** — saved, could not load, needs attention. use a [message strip](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-message-strip--docs).
- **for content that must interrupt.** use a [modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs).

## anatomy

1. **container** — a `div` on `--surface` (`--bg-2` when `sunken`), 1px `--border`, square, text in `--fg-2`.
2. **header** (when `header` or `headerAside` is set) — a row with padding 12px 16px and a 1px `--border` rule below; the title on the left, the aside on the right, at least 12px apart.
3. **title** — jetbrains mono 600, 13px, letter-spacing 0.01em, `--fg`, rendered lowercase. it can shrink and wraps when long.
4. **aside** (optional) — right-aligned, never shrinks, its items 8px apart: actions, a count.
5. **body** — padding 16px; `flush` removes it.
6. **footer** (optional) — padding 12px 16px, with a 1px `--border` rule above.

## behaviour and keyboard

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` | skips the card; focus moves through the controls inside it — header aside, then body, then footer. |

- a card has no click, hover, selected or disabled state.
- the header comes first in the dom, so keyboard users reach the header actions before the body.

## accessibility

- a plain `div` with no role and no name.
- today: the title renders in a `div`. it is not a heading and doesn't name the card. when people should find the card by heading, pass a heading element as `header`; with `margin: 0` and `font: inherit` it keeps the title's look. to make the card a landmark, add `role="region"` and `aria-labelledby` pointing at that heading — `Card` passes both to its container.
- icon-only actions in the aside need a name: use an [icon button](?path=/docs/components-actions-icon-button--docs), whose `label` is required.
- the title is lowercased by css only; screen readers read it as you wrote it.

## content

- the title is a lowercase noun phrase naming what's inside: `volumes`, `last backup`, `storage-01`. no verbs, no full stop.
- the aside holds one or two actions or a count. more goes into an [action menu](?path=/docs/components-actions-action-menu--docs).
- the footer holds a summary (`3 of 12 shown`) or the card's closing actions.

## props

`Card` passes every native `div` attribute to its container (`role`, `aria-labelledby`, `id`, …) and forwards its ref there.
