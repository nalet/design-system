# heading

mono display type for the page title (`h1`, 32px) and its sections (`h2`, 22px), optionally led by the blue `>` chevron.

<Canvas of={HeadingStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- once per page, for its title (`level={1}`), with the chevron on product screens.
- for the sections of a page (`level={2}`).

## when not to use

- **for the title of a card or panel.** use the [card](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-card--docs) `header`.
- **for a third level or below.** today heading stops at `h2`. a sub-section gets a native `h3` through [text](?path=/docs/components-data-display-text--docs): `as="h3"`.
- **for emphasis or a big number.** headings are structure: screen-reader users jump between them. emphasis is [text](?path=/docs/components-data-display-text--docs) with `variant="ui"`.
- **for the product name or logo.** use the lockup — see [brand](https://nalet.github.io/design-system/styles/brand/).

## anatomy

1. **heading** — a native `h1` or `h2` with no margin, jetbrains mono, `--fg`. level 1: weight 700, 32px, line height 1.15, letter-spacing -0.015em. level 2: weight 600, 22px, line height 1.2.
2. **chevron** (optional, `chevron`) — a bold `>` in `--cloud-blue`, 0.4em before the text, hidden from assistive tech.

## behaviour and keyboard

a heading is static and native. it takes no focus.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` | skips it. headings are not in the tab order. |

- screen readers have their own keys for moving from heading to heading (e.g. `h` in browse mode). that is why the levels must follow the page's outline.

## accessibility

- renders a native `h1` or `h2`, so it is in the document outline and screen readers can jump to it.
- one `h1` per page; its sections are `h2`. pick the level for the outline, never for the size.
- the chevron is `aria-hidden`: the heading's name is your text alone. don't type a `>` into the text — it would be read as "greater than".
- no case is changed: the text is shown and read exactly as written. write it lowercase, as the voice asks.
- today: only two levels.

## content

- a lowercase noun phrase, no full stop: `volumes`, `recent runs`, `storage settings`.
- short. a heading that wraps at 32px mono is too long.
- the chevron once, on the page's `h1`. on every heading it stops meaning anything.

  <Do column caption="the chevron once, on the page title.">

  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a chevron on every heading is decoration; it no longer marks the page.">

    </Stack>
  </Dont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="use chevron: the glyph is blue and stays out of the heading's name.">
    <Heading level={1} chevron>volumes</Heading>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a typed chevron is the wrong colour and is read aloud as 'greater than'.">
    <Heading level={1}>{'> volumes'}</Heading>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="a short noun phrase that names the section.">
    <Heading level={2}>recent runs</Heading>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a sentence as a heading wraps and buries the word people scan for.">
    <Heading level={2}>here you can see all of the runs from the last week</Heading>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## props

`Heading` passes every native heading attribute through (`id` for in-page links, `aria-*`, …) and forwards its ref.
