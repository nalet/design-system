## implementation notes

- Renders a native `h1` (`level={1}`, the default) or `h2` (`level={2}`), with `margin: 0`.
- `chevron` puts a `span` with `aria-hidden="true"` before your text, holding a `>` in `--cloud-blue`.
- Every native heading attribute passes through (`id` for in-page links, `aria-*`, …), and the `ref` reaches the heading.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` | Skips it. Headings are not in the tab order. |
