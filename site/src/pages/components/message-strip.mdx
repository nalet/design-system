# message strip

a message about the content of the page: a square strip with a state rule, a glyph, one or two human sentences, a way forward, and the technical part behind a details disclosure.

<Example label="a message strip with actions and details, live" column>
  <Playground client:visible />
</Example>

## when to use

- for a condition of the page's content that lasts until it is resolved or dismissed: `105 titles can't be checked: the catalog holds them without an identity.`
- for an error from loading or from an action, with a way forward: `couldn't load the list.` and `try again`.
- above the content it is about. at most one or two at a time: more than that, and none of them reads as important.

## when not to use

- **to confirm an action someone just took** — `8 episodes queued`. use a <C slug="toast" />: it says so and leaves.
- **for one item** — a field's error, a row's state. use the <C slug="field" /> error text, or an <C slug="object-status" /> in the row.
- **in place of a list that has nothing to show** — it is empty, or it couldn't load and there is nothing older to show. that is an <C slug="illustrated-message" /> where the list would be. keep the strip for content that is still on screen.
- **to stop everything for a decision.** use a <C slug="modal" />.
- **for help that is always true about one control.** that is a <C slug="field" /> hint.

## anatomy

1. **container** — a row with 12px padding (16px on the left), a 3px rule on the left in the state's colour (`--state-*`), a 1px `--state-*-border` frame, a `--state-*-bg` fill and square corners. inter 13px / 1.5 in `--fg`.
2. **glyph** — the state's 14px glyph in its colour, set 3px down so it sits on the first line. hidden from assistive tech.
3. **text** — one or two sentences. it takes the free width, at least 24ch before the actions wrap under it.
4. **actions** (optional) — after the text, 8px apart. they wrap under the text when there is no room.
5. **details toggle** (with details) — a quiet text button: jetbrains mono 12px in `--fg-muted`, 28px tall, with a 10px caret that turns down while the details are open.
6. **details** (optional) — 8px under the text: jetbrains mono 12px / 1.55 in `--fg-muted`, 8px × 12px padding and a 1px `--state-*-border` rule on the left. line breaks are kept, and long ids break anywhere.
7. **dismiss** (optional) — a 28px square button at the right edge, with a 12px × in `--fg-muted`.

## variants and states

five states; `information` is the default.

| state | announced | use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `neutral` | politely, the text alone | a plain fact about the content: what runs on its own, what is switched off |
| `information` | politely, "information:" and the text | under way, or worth knowing: the list is being refreshed, an index is rebuilding |
| `success` | politely, "success:" and the text | a good condition that lasts — every source answered in the last hour — or what a save changed on this page: `saved. times on this page now show in utc.` a plain confirmation is a toast |
| `warning` | politely, "warning:" and the text | works, but someone should look: titles that can't be checked, sources over their limit, a refresh that failed while the last data still shows |
| `error` | at once, "error:" and the text | failed or blocked, needs action: an action that failed, a list that couldn't load. always give a way forward |

### details

the details put the technical part — ids, raw errors, http codes — behind a disclosure, in mono. they are closed until someone opens them, and an announcement never reads them out.

### dismiss

a strip can carry a dismiss button. offer it for a message people can read and move on from — what a save changed, an action that failed — and leave it off a condition that is still true, like a list that can't load. the strip doesn't remember a dismissal: the screen does, and brings the strip back when its condition changes again.

## behaviour

- the strip's controls take focus in this order: its actions, the details toggle, dismiss. the text takes no focus.
- `esc` does nothing. a strip is part of the page, not an overlay.
- the strip announces its text when it appears or changes; what that means for each state is under accessibility.

the keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-message-strip--docs).

## accessibility

- **only the text is live.** an update reads the whole message, and never the actions, the details or dismiss.
- **errors interrupt.** an `error` is announced at once; every other state politely, when the screen reader has a pause. a strip that is on the page from the start and doesn't change can be a static note, not announced at all: it is then read with the page, like any text.
- **the state word.** every state but `neutral` reads its name before the text, from visually hidden text: "warning: 105 titles can't be checked". the word can be translated, given to `neutral` as well, or left out. an <C slug="object-status" /> names only `warning` and `error`.
- the glyph is hidden from assistive tech. each state has its own glyph shape, so the state never rests on colour alone.
- closed details are neither read nor focusable.
- dismiss is named "dismiss" and is 28px square, above the 24px minimum target size. the toggle and dismiss show the 2px `--focus-ring` outline every control shares — see [interaction](/design-system/foundations/interaction/#focus).

today:

- a live region announces changes to content it already holds. a polite strip that appears together with its text may go unannounced in some screen readers; an alert is announced as it appears.
- dismissing removes the button that had focus, and focus falls back to the page. move it yourself, to the content's heading or the next control.
- the toggle and dismiss carry the same names on every strip. with two strips on a page, name them so they can be told apart.

## content

- **the text** — one or two human sentences: what is true, then what it means or what happens next. `105 titles can't be checked: the catalog holds them without an identity.` lowercase. no ids, codes or raw errors.
- **numbers** — exact, with thousands separators: `2,500`, not `2.5k`.
- **an error** says what failed and offers the way forward: `couldn't load the list.` and `try again`. when the last data still shows, keep it, and make the strip a `warning` that says how old it is: `couldn't refresh the list; it shows what was loaded 4m ago.`
- **actions** — one or two small ghost buttons (`sm`, `ghost`), each a verb: `show them`, `try again`, `ask the catalog again`. never `primary`: the view's one emphasized action is elsewhere.
- **details** — the raw part as it came: `GET /api/missing: http 502 bad gateway`, `request id 7d1c0e4a`. line breaks are kept.

## do and don't

  <Do caption="the human part first, the raw part behind details." column>
    <MessageStrip client:visible live="off" state="warning" details="item 3f2a9c1e-5b7d-4c11-9e02-6a1d0b8f4c21 has no identity">105 titles can't be checked: the catalog holds them without an identity.</MessageStrip>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="the raw part as the message: screen readers read the codes aloud, and nobody learns what it means." column>
    <MessageStrip live="off" state="warning">105 items: identity_id is null (catalog item 3f2a9c1e-5b7d-4c11-9e02-6a1d0b8f4c21)</MessageStrip>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="a condition that lasts. it stays until it is resolved or dismissed." column>
    <MessageStrip live="off" state="warning">2 sources are over their daily query limit until midnight. searches use the other 3.</MessageStrip>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="the confirmation of something just done is a toast. as a strip it stays, and pushes the list down." column>
    <MessageStrip live="off" state="success">8 episodes queued.</MessageStrip>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

  <Do caption="a small ghost action: the way forward, without competing with the view's emphasized action." column>

  </Do>

  </Dont>
</DoDont>
