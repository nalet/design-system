## implementation notes

- renders a `<div>`, a centred column: the svg illustration, the title, the description and the actions.
- `illustration` picks the picture: `empty` (the default), `done`, `search`, `error` or `unknown`.
- `title` is a heading: an `h2` unless `headingLevel` (2 to 6) sets another level. `description` is a `<p>` under it.
- `actions` takes the buttons and renders whatever it is given.
- `size`: `sm`, `md` (the default) or `lg`.
- the svg has `aria-hidden="true"` and `focusable="false"`.
- it is not a live region: it doesn't announce itself when it appears.
- a [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs) takes it in its `empty` prop.
- other `<div>` attributes (all but `title`, which is the heading) and the `ref` reach the container.
- axe-core finds no violations in any kind, with a title, a description and an action.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | moves to and from your actions, in order. the picture, the title and the description take no focus. |
| `enter` / `space` | native: activates the focused action. |
