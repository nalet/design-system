# switch

A switch turns one setting on or off, and the change applies the moment it is flipped.

<Example label="a switch">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- For one setting that takes effect at once, with no save button: “Email alerts”, “Maintenance mode”.
- In a list or a panel of settings: one switch per setting, each with its own label.

## when to use something else

- **Inside a form that is saved with a button.** Use a <C slug="checkbox" />. A switch promises an effect at once, which the form doesn't have.
- **For a choice between two things that aren't on and off**, such as “daily” or “weekly”. Use a <C slug="select" />, or <C slug="tabs" /> to change the view. A switch can only say on or off.
- **To start an action**, such as “deploy release”. Use a <C slug="button" />: an action happens once, while a switch holds a state.

## anatomy

1. **Hit area.** The track, a small gap and the label: the whole <Term id="target-size">target</Term> people can click or tap.
2. **Track.** A small, wide, square frame. Off, it has a thin grey border on a dark fill; on, it is filled with blue.
3. **Thumb.** A small square inside the track. Off, it is grey and sits at the left; on, it turns dark and slides to the right.
4. **Label** (optional). The name of the setting, in the <Term id="body-face">body face</Term>.

## variants and states

A switch comes in two sizes: medium, the usual one, for lists and panels of settings, and small for dense rows and tables.

<Example label="two sizes">
  <Sizes />
</Example>

| state | looks |
| --- | --- |
| Off | A dark track, with a grey thumb at the left |
| On | A blue track, with a dark thumb at the right |
| Focused | An outline around the thumb, for keyboard <Term id="focus">focus</Term> only |
| Disabled | The track and the label dimmed. It can't take focus, be changed or be sent with a form |

Nothing changes under the pointer, and there is no look for an invalid value.

## behaviour

- A click on the label flips the switch too.
- A switch is one <Term id="tab-stop">tab stop</Term>. <Kbd>space</Kbd> flips it; <Kbd>enter</Kbd> doesn't, as with any checkbox.
- If applying the setting can fail, set the switch back, and say why.

The full keyboard map is on the switch's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-inputs-switch--docs).

## accessibility

- <Term id="screen-reader">Screen readers</Term> announce it as a switch that is on or off.
- **Its label is its name.** Without a visible label, give it a name that is read out but not shown. Nothing checks for one today.
- The label must not change with the state: the “on” or “off” that is announced already says it.
- If the change takes time, or shows somewhere else, confirm it with a <C slug="toast" /> or a <C slug="banner" />. The switch only announces its own state.

Today:

- The focus outline is drawn around the thumb. On a switch that is on, the outline falls mostly on the blue track, against which its colour has a <Term id="contrast">contrast</Term> of only about 1.1 to 1. Only a thin edge of it shows against the page, so keyboard focus on a switch that is on is hard to see.
- There is no invalid state. Inside a <C slug="field" /> with an error, the switch doesn't change.
- There are no styles for <Term id="forced-colours">forced colours</Term>, the high-contrast setting some operating systems offer.

## content

- Name the setting, not the action, in <Term id="sentence-case">sentence case</Term>: “Email alerts”, “Maintenance mode”, not “Turn on email alerts”.
- Keep the label the same in both states, with no “on”, “off”, “enabled” or “disabled” in it.
- Phrase it so that on means the feature runs: “Email alerts”, not “Mute email alerts”.
- When the effect isn't obvious, say it in a hint, which a <C slug="field" /> adds: “Visitors see a maintenance notice”.

## specs

| size | in code | track | thumb | the thumb slides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | `size="md"` (the default) | 36 × 20px | 14px | 16px |
| Small | `size="sm"` | 30 × 16px | 10px | 14px |

| state | in code | looks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | The default | Track: a 1px `--border-control` border on `--bg-2`. Thumb: `--fg-dim`, at the left |
| On | `checked`, or `defaultChecked` | Track: `--cloud-blue` fill and border. Thumb: `--bg`, slid to the right |
| Focused | `:focus-visible`, keyboard focus only | A 2px `--focus-ring` outline, 2px outside the thumb |
| Disabled | `disabled` | The whole switch, track and label, at 50% opacity, with a not-allowed cursor; it can't be focused, changed or submitted |

- **Hit area:** a `<label>` around the track and the label text, 8px (`--s-2`) apart.
- **Track:** square (`border-radius: 0`), with 2px inner padding. Its fill and border change in 120ms `ease`.
- **Thumb:** square. It slides in 140ms (0.14s) `ease`, and its colour changes in 120ms. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it moves without sliding.
- **Label:** `label`, Inter 13px, line-height 1.4, in `--fg-2`.
- **Element:** a native `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">`, transparent over the track. It takes the focus, the clicks and the form value; its state is the native `checked`, so don't add `aria-checked`.
- **Name without a visible label:** `aria-label`. Nothing enforces it today.
- **Focus on a switch that is on:** `--focus-ring` against `--cloud-blue` contrasts about 1.1:1, and only a 1px edge of the outline shows against the page.
- **Not there:** no hover style, no invalid look, and no `forced-colors` styles.
