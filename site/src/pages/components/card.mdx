# card

a square surface panel with a 1px frame, an optional mono header row (with an aside for actions) and an optional footer row. the default container for grouped content.

<Example label="a card with a title">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- to group content about one thing under a short title: a volume's details, a run's summary, a section of settings.
- to give a table or a list a frame and a title: a `flush` card.
- a `sunken` card, for a panel nested inside another surface.

## when not to use

- **for something people open or launch.** a card is not clickable. an entry on the launchpad is a <C slug="tile" />; an action is a <C slug="button" />.
- **for padding or a background without a title.** use a <C slug="box" />.
- **for a message** — saved, could not load, needs attention. use a <C slug="message-strip" />.
- **for content that must interrupt.** use a <C slug="modal" />.

## anatomy

1. **container** — `--surface` (`--bg-2` when `sunken`), a 1px `--border` frame, square, text in `--fg-2`.
2. **header** (when there is a title or an aside) — a row with padding 12px 16px and a 1px `--border` rule below; the title on the left, the aside on the right, at least 12px apart.
3. **title** — jetbrains mono 600, 13px, letter-spacing 0.01em, `--fg`, shown lowercase. it can shrink and wraps when long.
4. **aside** (optional) — right-aligned, never shrinks, its items 8px apart: actions, a count.
5. **body** — padding 16px; `flush` removes it.
6. **footer** (optional) — padding 12px 16px, with a 1px `--border` rule above.

## behaviour

- a card has no click, hover, selected or disabled state. it takes no focus; the controls inside it do.
- the header comes first, so keyboard users reach the header actions before the body.

the keyboard map and the attributes it passes through are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-card--docs).

## accessibility

- a card has no role and no name of its own.
- today: the title is not a heading and doesn't name the card. when people should find the card by heading, make its title a real heading — it can keep the title's look — and name the card as a region by that heading, as the last example above does.
- icon-only actions in the aside need a name: use an <C slug="icon-button" />, whose label is required.
- the title is lowercased by css only; screen readers read it as you wrote it.

## content

- the title is a lowercase noun phrase naming what's inside: `volumes`, `last backup`, `storage-01`. no verbs, no full stop.
- the aside holds one or two actions or a count. more goes into an <C slug="action-menu" />.
- the footer holds a summary (`3 of 12 shown`) or the card's closing actions.
