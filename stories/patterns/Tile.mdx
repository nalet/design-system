# tile

a square launch tile. as an `app` tile it is one link or button into an app — icon, name, one line on what is there; as a `kpi` tile it shows a key figure and its trend. tiles live in a [tile group](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile-group--docs).

<Canvas of={TileStories.App} />

## when to use

- to offer an app or a tool as a destination on a launchpad or a hub page.
- to show a key figure and where it is heading, at a glance: cpu, latency, free space (`variant="kpi"`).
- with a `badge` or a `status` when a destination needs attention before people open it.

## when not to use

- **for records people work through.** use a [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs) — see the [worklist](?path=/docs/patterns-worklist--docs) pattern.
- **for an action on the current screen.** a tile goes somewhere; to do something here, use a [button](?path=/docs/components-actions-button--docs).
- **to switch between views of one object.** use [tabs](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tabs--docs).
- **for a state with its own words in a row or a form.** use [object status](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-object-status--docs).
- **for a panel of text and controls.** use a [card](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-card--docs).

## anatomy

1. **container** — an `<a>` with `href`, a `<button>` with `onClick`, your element with `as`, otherwise a `<div>`. square, 1px `--border`, `--surface` fill, 16px padding and 12px between rows; at least 7rem tall (112px at the default 16px root).
2. **icon** (optional) — the lucide glyph you pass, drawn at 22px with a 1.75 stroke in `--cloud-blue`, in a 28px square box. decoration only.
3. **title** — jetbrains mono 600, 14px, `--fg`, shown lowercase. one line, cut with an ellipsis.
4. **chevron** — the brand chevron, 14px, after the title on link and button tiles. it shows on hover and focus.
5. **badge** (optional) — top right, 18px high, mono 11px. a number becomes a count chip with tabular figures, a string a status pill.
6. **status** (optional) — an 8px square dot coloured by health.
7. **description** (`app`) — inter 13px / 1.45 in `--fg-muted`, at most two lines.
8. **figure** (`kpi`) — the value in mono 700, 28px; the unit in 14px `--fg-muted`; the delta in mono 12px with a small triangle. pinned to the bottom of the tile.

## variants and states

two variants: `app` (the default: icon, title, description) and `kpi` (title, value, unit, delta).

<Canvas of={TileStories.Kpi} />

the delta's `direction` (`up`, `down`, `flat`) draws the triangle; its `tone` picks the colour — `positive` `--signal-green`, `negative` `--signal-red`, `neutral` (the default) `--fg-muted`. the two are separate, so a latency that goes down can be good news.

three sizes: `sm` (at least 5.5rem tall, 12px padding, 8px between rows), `md` (7rem, the default) and `lg` (9rem).

<Canvas of={TileStories.Sizes} />

| state | how | looks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| interactive | `href`, `onClick` or `as` | hover: `--surface-2` fill and `--border-2`. focus: a 2px `--cloud-blue` frame. the chevron shows on both |
| selected | `selected` | a 2px `--cloud-blue` rule on the left edge: the current app |
| disabled | `disabled` | 45% opacity. renders a `<div>`: no link, no click, no focus |
| external | `external`, with `href` | opens in a new tab (`target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`). today nothing on the tile says so |
| display only | no `href`, `onClick` or `as` | a `<div>`: no hover, no chevron, not focusable |

<Canvas of={TileStories.States} />

`badgeTone` colours the badge's text and border on a tinted fill: `info` (the default, `--cloud-blue`), `success` (`--signal-green`), `warning` (`--signal-amber`), `danger` (`--signal-red`) and `neutral` (`--fg-muted` on `--surface-2`).

<Canvas of={TileStories.Badges} />

`status` draws the dot: `online` `--signal-green`, `degraded` `--signal-amber`, `offline` `--signal-red`, `unknown` `--fg-dim`.

## behaviour and keyboard

a link or button tile is a native `<a>` or `<button>`, so the browser provides the keys.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | moves focus to / from the tile. in a tile group, the group decides which tile is in the tab order — see [tile group](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile-group--docs). |
| `enter` | link: follows it. button: presses it. |
| `space` | button: presses it. |

- in a [tile group](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile-group--docs) or a [portal](?path=/docs/patterns-portal--docs), the arrow keys move between tiles. link and button tiles opt in with the `data-nc-tile` attribute they set.
- the [portal](?path=/docs/patterns-portal--docs) search matches against the title and the description, lowercased, which every tile carries in `data-nc-search`.
- `as` renders your component, e.g. a router link. today `Tile` passes `href` and `external` only to a plain `<a>`: give your component its destination yourself.
- transitions are off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## accessibility

- a link or button tile is one target. its accessible name is all of its text, in order: title, badge, status (`status: online`), then the description, or the value, unit and delta. keep every part short; people hear all of it.
- the icon, the chevron and the delta's triangle are `aria-hidden`.
- the status dot has `role="img"` and the label `status: <status>`. on screen it is colour only, so put the state into words too (the description) when it matters.
- the delta's direction is drawn, not written: write the sign into `value` (`+4`, `−11`) so that it is read.
- `selected` is visual only. pass `aria-current="page"` on the current app's tile yourself; other attributes reach the element.

today:

- focus shows as a 2px `--cloud-blue` frame, not the `--focus-ring` outline that other controls use.
- a disabled tile is a `<div>` with `aria-disabled`, which assistive tech ignores on a plain element: it is read as text, not as an unavailable link. if the reason matters, put it in the description.
- `external` opens a new tab without saying so. say it in the description.
- a count badge is read as a bare number (`metrics 3 …`). say what it counts in the description.

## content

- **title** — the app's name: one or two words, a noun, no verb. `pipelines`, not `open pipelines`. the lowercase look is css, so write it as it should be read.
- **description** — what people find there, in a few words: `builds and releases`. it stops after two lines.
- **badge** — how many things wait (`3`), or one word for a state (`new`, `failed`).
- **kpi** — the figure and its unit (`142`, `ms`); the delta with its sign (`−11`). round to what people can act on.
