# space & grid

one 4-base scale for every gap and padding, three control heights, density per component, and components that change with their own width. there is no grid component: layouts are stacks, boxes, cards and css grid.

<Canvas of={SpaceStories.Scale} sourceState="none" />

## the scale

eight steps, `--s-1` to `--s-8`, from `tokens/tokens.json`. nothing between them: no 10px, no 20px.

| token | px | where the library uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--s-1` | 4 | the smallest gaps inside a part; the cells of a `dense` table, top and bottom; a tooltip's padding, top and bottom |
| `--s-2` | 8 | between a control's parts (icon and label, a field's label, control and hint); between buttons in a row; cell padding, top and bottom |
| `--s-3` | 12 | a `md` field's padding; a card's header and footer, top and bottom; a message strip's padding; cell padding, left and right |
| `--s-4` | 16 | a `md` button's padding; the body of a card or a modal; a tile's padding; the default gap of a stack |
| `--s-5` | 24 | a `lg` button's padding; under the portal's title bar; the toaster's distance from the bottom |
| `--s-6` | 32 | between the portal's groups; an illustrated message's padding |
| `--s-7` | 48 | the room a modal leaves: it is at most the viewport's height minus 48px |
| `--s-8` | 64 | a `lg` illustrated message's padding, top and bottom |

- the space between components and around them is always a step. inside small parts a few components nudge by 1–6px to centre a glyph or a label: 2px in the switch track and between a filter tab's count and label, 3px to sit a glyph on the first line of text, 6px between an object status glyph and its text, around a tooltip and inside inline code and kbd. those values belong to the components; keep your own layout on the scale.
- space shows structure: things that belong together sit closer than the groups they form. 8px inside a field, 16px between fields, 24px or more between sections.

## control heights

every control comes in the same three heights, so controls in a row line up.

| size | height | label size | padding: button / field | where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | 28px | 12px (button), 13px (field) | 12px / 8px | toolbars, table rows, dense panels. toolbar actions are always `sm` |
| `md` | 34px | 13px | 16px / 12px | the default: forms, the filter bar |
| `lg` | 42px | 14px | 24px / 16px | a lone call to action, a sign-in form |

<Canvas of={SpaceStories.ControlHeights} />

- [button](?path=/docs/components-actions-button--docs) and [input](?path=/docs/components-inputs-input--docs) take `size` and `inputSize`, [select](?path=/docs/components-inputs-select--docs) `selectSize`, [icon button](?path=/docs/components-actions-icon-button--docs) `size` (a square: 28, 34 or 42px). [multi select](?path=/docs/components-inputs-multi-select--docs) has `sm` and `md` only.
- the [checkbox](?path=/docs/components-inputs-checkbox--docs) box is 16px and the [switch](?path=/docs/components-inputs-switch--docs) track 20px high (`md`) or 16px (`sm`). their whole label is the target.
- outside the three heights: action menu items are at least 32px high, the portal's search field is 32px and a modal's close button 26px.
- a [textarea](?path=/docs/components-inputs-textarea--docs) has no height: `rows` sets it, 4 by default.

## density

density is a prop on each component that has one. there is no page-wide switch.

| component | default | dense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs) `dense` | cells 8px 12px | cells 4px 8px |
| [table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-table--docs) `dense` | headers 8px 12px, cells 12px | 4px 8px |
| [portal](?path=/docs/patterns-portal--docs) `density="compact"` | 24px under the title bar, 32px between groups | 16px, 24px |
| [tile](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile--docs) `size="sm"` | 16px padding, 12px gap | 12px padding, 8px gap |
| controls `sm` | 34px (`md`) | 28px |

<Canvas of={SpaceStories.Density} />

- use the dense table for long lists that people scan and compare with a pointer: an inventory, a log.
- keep the default where rows carry a second line (a status with its reason), and where people use touch.
- one density per view. a dense table under default-sized filters is fine; two tables of different density side by side are not.

## responsive behaviour

three components change their layout by their own width, measured with a `ResizeObserver`. they don't read the viewport: a table in a narrow side panel on a wide screen responds the same as one on a phone. the library has no viewport media queries; its only media queries are for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

| component | at its own width | what changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs) | 720px and below | columns with `importance: 'low'` pop in: they leave the header row and show under the row's first column, each with its label |
| | 480px and below | `importance: 'medium'` columns pop in too, the cells' side padding drops to 8px, and a long group label wraps instead of being cut |
| [filter bar](?path=/docs/components-inputs-filter-bar--docs) | below 640px (`collapseBelow`) | the facets fold behind a `filters` button that says how many are set (`filters · 2`); opened, they stack under the search, with `clear all` at their end |
| [toolbar](?path=/docs/components-actions-toolbar--docs) | below 560px (`collapseBelow`) | every action but the emphasized one moves into the `more actions` menu |

<Canvas of={SpaceStories.Responsive} />

- the width is the element's content box, rounded to the pixel, so padding inside it counts against it. the toolbar has 12px of padding and a 1px border on each side: its actions move while its outer edge is still under 586px. the data table's 1px border puts its steps at 722 and 482px outside.
- until a component has been measured, and where `ResizeObserver` doesn't exist (server rendering, jsdom tests), it shows its wide layout.
- `collapseBelow` moves the filter bar's and the toolbar's threshold. the data table's 720 and 480 are fixed; a column's `importance` decides what goes first, and the first column (or the one marked `rowHeader`) never pops in.
- the rest is css: rows of the filter bar, the toolbar, a worklist's header, the portal's title bar and a message strip wrap when they run out of room; [filter tabs](?path=/docs/components-navigation-filter-tabs--docs) scroll sideways, with a fade on the edge where more are hidden; a modal is at most 480px wide (`width`), 16px from the edges; the toaster is at most the viewport's width minus 32px.

## grid and layout

there is no grid component, no column system and no breakpoints. a layout is composed:

- [stack](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-stack--docs) — one direction, a `gap` from the scale. the workhorse.
- [box](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-box--docs) — padding from the scale and a canvas background.
- [card](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-card--docs) — a titled group with the standard padding.
- css grid in your app, for rows and columns, with gaps from the tokens.
- [tile group](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile-group--docs) is the one component with a grid of its own: `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(14rem, 1fr))` by default (`minTileWidth`), or a fixed `columns` count, with gaps of 4, 8, 16 (default) or 24px.

<Example label="a page composed from a stack, cards and a css grid">
  <Stack gap={24} style={{ width: '100%' }}>
    <Text variant="ui">storage · 3 volumes</Text>
    <div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: 'repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 12rem), 1fr))', gap: 'var(--s-4)' }}>
      <Card header="storage-01">412 gb used, checked today.</Card>
      <Card header="storage-02">1.2 tb used, one disk slow.</Card>
      <Card header="db-0">240 gb used, offline since 12 oct.</Card>
    </div>
  </Stack>
</Example>

```css
/* columns at least 12rem wide, as many as fit; one column on a narrow screen. */
.volumes {
  display: grid;
  grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 12rem), 1fr));
  gap: var(--s-4);
}
```

## how to apply it

- **css.** `var(--s-1)` … `var(--s-8)`, from `tokens.css`.
- **box and stack.** `p`, `px`, `py` and `gap` take the step in px: 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 or 64. typescript rejects any other number; at run time an off-scale number resolves to `0`.
- **javascript.** from `@nalet/design-system`, `space` is the helper behind those props: `space(16)` returns `'var(--s-4)'`. the values themselves are `tokens.space` (`tokens.space['s-4']` is `'16px'`), or `space` from `@nalet/design-system/tokens`.
- **tailwind.** the preset sets the spacing keys `1`–`8` (and `s-1`–`s-8`) to the scale, so `p-5` is 24px here, not tailwind's 20px, and `gap-8` is 64px. tailwind's other spacing keys (`0.5`, `10`, `12` …) are still there and off the scale: don't use them.

## accessibility

- `sm` controls (28px) still meet the 24px minimum target size. the 16px checkbox and the switch count on their label as the target; unlabelled boxes in a list need 24px from one to the next.
- zoomed to 400%, a 1280px window is 320px wide. the width-aware components then respond as they would in a narrow panel: only high-importance columns stay in the header, the facets fold, the actions move to the menu.
- a popped-in column is still read: its label and value are text under the row's first cell, not hidden.
- in your own layouts, keep the order on screen the order in the dom: no `order`, no reversed directions. then what people see, what they hear and where `tab` goes agree.
