# multi select

A multi select lets people filter a list by several values of one <Term id="facet">facet</Term> at once, such as the states “missing” and “on its way”. A button shows what is chosen, and a panel of checkboxes opens under it.

  <Playground client:visible />
</Example>

## when to use

- In a <C slug="filter-bar" />, for a facet that can keep several values at once, such as a state facet that keeps “missing” and “on its way”.
- For a handful of options that fit in a small panel, each with a count of what it would keep, when you know it.

## when to use something else

- **For one value at a time, such as a kind or a time range.** Use a <C slug="select" />; the filter bar takes those as select facets.
- **For a setting, or a form that is sent.** Use <C slug="checkbox">checkboxes</C> in a <C slug="field" />. A form sends none of the multi select's choices.
- **For many options.** The panel has no search and no maximum height. Let people type in the <C slug="filter-bar" />'s search instead.
- **To run commands.** Use an <C slug="action-menu" />.

## anatomy

1. **<Term id="trigger">Trigger</Term>.** A button with a thin border, in one of two heights. It holds:
   - The facet's **label**, in a muted colour and in lowercase.
   - The **summary** of what is chosen: “any” while nothing is, the option's label when one is, and “2 chosen” when several are. It turns blue once something is chosen, and ends in an <Term id="ellipsis">ellipsis</Term> when it is too long.
   - A small blue **arrow**, which turns over while the panel is open.
2. **Panel.** A box with a thin border and a shadow, just under the trigger and lined up with its left edge. It is at least as wide as the trigger, and never wider than a set maximum, or than the screen.
3. **Options.** One <C slug="checkbox" /> per option, in a group named after the facet. Each option's label is a filter value, in <Term id="monospace">monospace</Term>, as the trigger shows it. When you give a count, it follows the label, in a dimmer colour.
4. **Clear.** A button at the bottom, below a thin rule, that clears every choice: “clear” and the facet's label, such as “clear state”. It is there only while something is chosen.

## variants and states

| state | the trigger shows |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing chosen | The label, then the empty text, “any” |
| One chosen | The label, then that option's label, in blue |
| Several chosen | The label, then a count such as “2 chosen”, in blue |
| Open | The arrow turned over, and the panel under it |
| Disabled | The whole trigger dimmed. It can't take <Term id="focus">focus</Term> or open |

### an action under the options

Where people make the options themselves, such as their own lists, the panel can end with one action that adds an option: “create a list”, in a picker of the lists that hold a title. It sits in the panel's foot, under the thin rule and before “clear …”, so both stay in view however long the options run. The action is planned: the drawings show the idea.

<figure class="ds-figure">
  <ActionUnderOptions />
  <figcaption>The lists that hold a title, two of three ticked. Under the rule, create a list, and clear lists while something is ticked.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **One action, a verb and its object**, with a plus before it: “create a list”. It is a quiet text button, like “clear …”, and it is there whether or not anything is chosen.
- **It closes the panel first.** Pressing it closes the panel and puts <Term id="focus">focus</Term> back on the trigger, and then it runs, as an item of an <C slug="action-menu" /> does. So a dialog it opens, to name the new list, gives focus back to the trigger when it closes.
- **What it adds** is among the options the next time the panel opens.
- **The order for the keyboard:** the trigger, the checkboxes, the action, then “clear …”.
- **A picker, not a filter.** A multi select like this one sets something about one thing, the lists that hold this title, instead of filtering a list. It works the same way: each tick applies at once, with no apply button.

### a long list of options

The options scroll inside the panel once they would be taller than 320px, or would come closer than 8px to the bottom of the window: the 320px are the panel's own largest width, and the 8px the margin an action menu keeps. The foot stays under them, in view. When the panel wouldn't fit below the trigger and there is more room above it, the panel opens above, as an action menu does. The scrolling is planned: the drawings show the idea.

- The scroll keeps a list that grows, such as people's own lists, inside the window. It doesn't make the multi select fit for many options: those still need a search, as [when to use something else](#when-to-use-something-else) says.
- <Kbd>tab</Kbd> still goes through every checkbox, and the panel scrolls the one with focus into view. Scrolling the options doesn't scroll the page behind them.

## behaviour

- The multi select is a <Term id="disclosure">disclosure</Term>, not a menu: the trigger shows and hides a panel of checkboxes. Opening it doesn't move focus.
- A click on the trigger, or <Kbd>enter</Kbd> or <Kbd>space</Kbd> on it, opens or closes the panel. A press anywhere outside closes it, and so does focus leaving the multi select.
- <Kbd>esc</Kbd> closes the panel and puts focus back on the trigger. The key goes no further, so a <C slug="modal" /> around the multi select stays open.
- The arrow keys do nothing special: each checkbox is its own <Term id="tab-stop">tab stop</Term>. <Kbd>tab</Kbd> moves from the trigger through the checkboxes, then to the clear button, and out. <Kbd>space</Kbd> ticks or clears the checkbox that has focus.
- Every change applies at once. There is no apply button.

The full keyboard map is on the multi select's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-inputs-multi-select--docs).

## accessibility

- **The trigger's name is its text:** the facet's label, then the summary, as in “state 2 chosen”. Write the label so that the two read as a phrase.
- A <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term> announces the checkboxes as one group, named after the facet. Each checkbox is named by its option's label and its count.
- A closed panel isn't read out, and the keyboard can't reach it.
- Focus is never moved for you, except back to the trigger when people press <Kbd>esc</Kbd>.
- The trigger and the clear button show the focus outline that every control shares; each checkbox shows its own.

Today:

- The clear button closes the panel and moves focus back to the trigger, because the button itself goes once nothing is chosen.
- The clear button's words are fixed, in English: “clear” and the label. They can't be translated.
- The panel always opens below the trigger, from its left edge. It doesn't flip near the edges of the screen, and it has no maximum height.

## content

- **Label.** The facet's name, a noun: “state”, “aired”, “kind”.
- **Options.** The values in the list's own words, in an order that means something, such as the states in their usual order rather than sorted by count: “missing”, “on its way”, “needs attention”.
- **Counts.** How many items each option would keep, when you know it.
- **Empty text.** What no choice means: “any”, the default, or “all states”.

## specs

| size | in code | height | side padding | text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | `size="md"` (the default) | 34px | 12px | 13px |
| Small | `size="sm"` | 28px | 8px | 12px |

| part | spec |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | A `<button type="button">` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and no `aria-haspopup`: it opens a group of checkboxes, not a menu. A 1px `--border-control` border on `--bg-2`, square, JetBrains Mono, `--fg`; the label, the summary and the chevron 8px apart. Hover: a `--cloud-blue` border, in 120ms `ease`. Keyboard focus: the 2px `--focus-ring` outline, 1px outside. |
| Label | `label`, in `--fg-muted`, lowercased with `text-transform`. It also names the group of checkboxes. |
| Summary | `emptyText` (default “any”) while nothing is chosen, the option's `label` when one is, “n chosen” (with a thousands separator) when several are. `--cloud-blue` once something is chosen. One line, ending in an ellipsis when it is long. |
| Chevron | 12px, `--cloud-blue`. It turns over (180°) while the panel is open, in 120ms `ease`, with no movement under reduced motion. |
| Panel | 4px under the trigger, from its left edge, at `z-index: 120`. `--surface` fill, a 1px `--border-2` border, the shadow `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`, padding 8px 0. At least as wide as the trigger, at most 320px or the viewport minus 16px. No maximum height, and no flip near the viewport's edges. `hidden` while closed. It isn't rendered into the page's `<body>`, so an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` cuts it off. |
| Options | `options`: `{ value, label, count? }`. A `fieldset` named by a visually hidden `legend` (the `label`), with one native `Checkbox` (a 16px box) per option, padding 8px 12px, `--surface-2` on hover. The option's label in JetBrains Mono 13px, `--fg-2`, at least 10ch wide. Its count 12px after it: mono 12px, `--fg-dim`, tabular figures, with a thousands separator. |
| Clear | “clear” and the `label`, only while something is chosen. Full width, 4px under the options, padding 8px 12px, a 1px `--border` rule above. JetBrains Mono 12px, `--fg-muted` (`--fg` on hover), left-aligned, lowercase. Keyboard focus: the 2px `--focus-ring` outline, drawn inside. |
| Disabled | `disabled`: 50% opacity and a not-allowed cursor. It can't be focused or opened. |

- **Value:** controlled. `value` holds the chosen values, and an empty list means no filter. `onChange` gets the new list at once, on every change; “clear” calls it with `[]`.
- **Forms:** the checkboxes have no `name`, so a form sends none of them.
- **Closing:** a `pointerdown` anywhere outside, focus leaving the multi select, or <Kbd>esc</Kbd> while it is open, which also focuses the trigger and stops the key there.
- **Action (planned):** one quiet button in the foot, under the rule and before clear, there whether or not anything is chosen. It looks like clear: full width, padding 8px 12px, JetBrains Mono 12px in `--fg-muted` (`--fg` on hover), left-aligned, lowercase, with a 12px plus icon, the size for small controls, 8px before its words. Pressing it closes the panel and focuses the trigger before its own handler runs.
- **Long lists (planned):** the options in a part of their own that scrolls, at most 320px tall, with `overflow-y: auto` and `overscroll-behavior: contain`; the foot stays outside it. The panel measures itself before it shows, keeps 8px from the viewport's bottom edge, and opens above the trigger when it wouldn't fit below and there is more room above, as the action menu's placement does.
