## implementation notes

- The selection belongs to your app. `DataTable` takes `selectable`, `selected` (a set of row keys) and `onSelectionChange`, and hands the next set to `onSelectionChange`.
- `DataTable` doesn't prune `selected` when `rows` change. When a filter, a search or a tab changes the list, keep, drop or clear the selection yourself.
- `isRowSelectable` gives a row a disabled checkbox and keeps it out of every way of selecting.
- `rowLabel` names each row's checkbox, for example “select S04E07”.
- With `onRowPress`, a click outside a row's controls, or `enter`, opens the row. Without it, a click on the row toggles its checkbox.
- Rows carry `aria-selected`, and the table carries `aria-multiselectable`, so screen readers can tell which rows are selected.
- `SelectionToolbar` takes `selectedCount` and `onClearSelection`.
- `primaryAction(n)` returns the emphasized action for exactly that selection (its label and handler), or `null` while nothing is selected.
- `actions` are never emphasized. `overflow: true` puts one in the “more actions” menu.
- `selectAll` offers “select all n”, and `formatSelected` words the count.
- The toolbar announces each change of the count politely, but never its first state.
- A group's action comes from `groupAction(group)` on the table, next to `groupBy` and `groupHeader`.
- The toast comes from `toast()`, and the one `<Toaster />` near the app's root shows it.
