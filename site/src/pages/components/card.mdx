# card

A card groups content about one thing in a framed panel, often under a short title. It is the usual container for grouped content.

<Example label="a card with a title">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- To group content about one thing under a short title: a volume's details, a run's summary, a section of settings.
- To give a table or a list a frame and a title, with a flush card.
- As a sunken card, for a panel inside another panel.

## when to use something else

- **For something people open or launch.** A card isn't clickable. An entry on the <Term id="home">home page</Term> is a <C slug="tile" />; an action is a <C slug="button" />.
- **For padding or a background without a title.** Use a <C slug="box" />.
- **For a message**, such as a save that worked, a list that could not load, or something that needs attention. Use a <C slug="message-strip" />.
- **For content that has to interrupt people.** Use a <C slug="modal" />.

## anatomy

1. **Frame.** A square panel with a thin border.
2. **Header** (when there is a title or an aside). A row across the top, with a thin line below it: the title on the left, the aside on the right, and a gap between them.
3. **Title.** A short name for what's inside, in <Term id="monospace">monospace</Term> and lowercase. It can shrink, and it wraps onto a second line when it is long.
4. **Aside** (optional). The card's own actions, or a count, at the right end of the header. It never shrinks.
5. **Body.** The content, with space around it. A flush card leaves that space out.
6. **Footer** (optional). A row across the bottom, with a thin line above it.

## behaviour

- A card has no click, no look under the pointer, and no selected or disabled state. It takes no <Term id="focus">focus</Term>; the controls inside it do.
- The header comes first. So <Kbd>tab</Kbd> reaches the actions in the header before the body, and the footer last.

The full keyboard map is on the card's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-card--docs).

## accessibility

- A card has no role and no name of its own.
- An action in the aside that is only an icon needs a name. Use an <C slug="icon-button" />, which requires one.
- The title is lowercase only in its look. A <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term> reads it as you wrote it.

Today: the title isn't a heading, and it doesn't name the card. When people should find the card by its heading, make its title a real heading; it can keep the title's look. Then make the card a region named by that heading, as the last example above does.

## content

- **The title.** A short noun phrase in lowercase that names what's inside: “volumes”, “last backup”, “storage-01”. No verbs, and no full stop.
- **The body.** The reader's text, written in <Term id="sentence-case">sentence case</Term>.
- **The aside.** One or two actions, or a count. Anything more goes into an <C slug="action-menu" />.
- **The footer.** A summary, such as “3 of 12 shown”, or the card's closing actions.

## specs

| part | spec |
| --- | --- |
| Frame | A `--surface` fill (`--bg-2` when `sunken`), a 1px `--border` frame, `border-radius: 0`; text in `--fg-2`. |
| Header | Shown when `header` or `headerAside` is set. Padding 12px 16px and a 1px `--border` rule below; the title on the left and the aside on the right, at least 12px apart. |
| Title | `header`. JetBrains Mono 600, 13px, letter-spacing 0.01em, `--fg`, lowercased with `text-transform`. It can shrink (`min-width: 0`) and wraps when long. |
| Aside | `headerAside`. Right-aligned and never shrinks; its items 8px apart. |
| Body | The card's children. Padding 16px; `flush` removes it. |
| Footer | `footer`. Padding 12px 16px and a 1px `--border` rule above. |

| setting | in code | what it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunken | `sunken` | The card on `--bg-2` instead of `--surface`, for a panel inside another surface. |
| Flush | `flush` | No body padding, so a table meets the frame. |
| A named section | A heading element as `header`, and `role="region"` with `aria-labelledby` on the card | The title is a real heading that keeps the title's look (`margin: 0`, `font: inherit`), and the card is a region named by it. |

- **Order:** the header, then the body, then the footer, so `tab` reaches the aside's controls first.
- **Role and name:** none of its own. The title renders in a `div`, not a heading.
