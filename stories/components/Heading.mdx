## implementation notes

- It renders a native `h1` (`level={1}`, the default) or `h2` (`level={2}`), with `margin: 0`.
- Because it is a native heading, screen reader users can list the headings on a page and jump between them.
- `chevron` puts a `span` with `aria-hidden="true"` before your text, holding a `>` in `--cloud-blue`, so a screen reader reads only your text.
- Every native heading attribute passes through, such as `id` for in-page links and `aria-*`, and the `ref` reaches the heading.

| key | does |

| `tab` | Skips it. Headings are not in the tab order. |
