# motion

small and quick, and only to say something: that a control heard you, that a state changed, that work is under way. nothing bounces, slides in from far away or plays for show.

## what moves

| motion | where | duration, easing | it says | with reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| colour change | button, icon button, input, select, textarea, checkbox, switch track, tabs, filter tabs, multi select trigger, modal close button | 120ms `ease` | the control heard you: hover, press, focus, on | unchanged: colour, not movement |
| tile hover | tile surface and border; the brand chevron fades in | 80ms `linear` | this tile is a way in | instant |
| tooltip | the bubble fades in; it hides at once | 100ms `ease` | here is the detail | unchanged |
| switch thumb | slides 16px (14px at `sm`) | 140ms `ease` | the setting changed | no transition: the thumb moves at once |
| disclosure caret | message strip details (turns 90°), multi select (180°), a data table group (90°) | 120ms `ease` | open, or closed | instant |
| toast | rises 6px as it fades in; leaves at once | 160ms `ease-out` | something just happened | appears at once |
| spinner | turns at a steady speed, without end | 0.7s a turn, `linear` | working on it | turns once every 1.6s; it doesn't stop |
| busy rule | on a loading data table, a 2px `--cloud-blue` bar, 30% wide, runs along the top edge, over and over | 1.1s a run, `ease-in-out` | this list is being refreshed | stops: a still 2px rule across the full width; the rows still dim |
| lockup cursor | the cursor block blinks: on for half of each cycle, off for the other half | 1.05s a cycle, without end | the brand's one cursor | keeps blinking (see accessibility) |

## what doesn't move

- modals, action menus and the multi select panel open and close at once. only the multi select's chevron turns.
- toasts and tooltips leave at once; only their entrance is animated.
- sorting, selecting a row, pop-in, the filter bar folding and actions moving into the overflow menu happen at once. nothing animates a width, a height or a row's position.
- the active tab's underline changes colour in place; it doesn't slide between tabs.
- keyboard focus rings (`--focus-ring` outlines) appear at once. a field's blue focus border comes in with its 120ms colour change.
- nothing scrolls smoothly: moving focus, or the roving tab stop, jumps.

## durations and easing

- **80ms `linear`** — the tile, a surface people sweep the pointer over.
- **100ms `ease`** — the tooltip's fade.
- **120ms `ease`** — every other change of state: colours and carets.
- **140ms `ease`** — the switch thumb, the one control part that travels.
- **160ms `ease-out`** — the toast, the one thing that enters.
- **loops** — the spinner (0.7s a turn), the busy rule (1.1s a run) and the lockup cursor (1.05s a cycle) repeat for as long as they show.

today: there are no motion tokens. each component writes its durations and easings in its own css, so an app can't read them from the library; match the values above.

## how to apply it in your app

- keep a response to a person at 120ms, and never past 160ms. a reaction that takes longer feels like lag, not polish.
- fade, or move a few pixels, as the toast does with its 6px. don't slide panels across the screen or scale things up on hover.
- run a loop only while something is busy, and stop it the moment the work is done.
- don't animate layout. rows, columns and panels change at once, as the data table's do.
- put every movement and every loop behind `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, and there stop it or show its end state, as the toast and the busy rule do.
- to see the reduced versions, turn on reduced motion in the system settings, or emulate `prefers-reduced-motion` in the browser's developer tools.

## accessibility

with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:

- **stop:** the toast's entrance, the carets, the tile's hover and its chevron, the busy rule (it becomes a still rule).
- **slow down:** the spinner, to one turn every 1.6s. it keeps turning.
- **unchanged:** the colour changes and the tooltip's fade, which don't move anything; and today the switch thumb, which slides 16px in 140ms.
- **doesn't stop:** the lockup cursor. the stylesheet hides the cursor's `<animate>` element with `display: none`, but `display` doesn't apply to svg animation elements, so the blink carries on.

and:

- the lockup cursor blinks for as long as the page is open. content that moves for more than five seconds next to other content needs a way to stop it (wcag 2.2.2): wherever the lockup stays in view, pass `showCursor={false}`. see [brand](?path=/docs/foundations-brand--docs).
- nothing flashes. the only thing that turns on and off is the cursor, less than once a second, far under the limit of three flashes a second.
- motion never says something alone: a loading data table also sets `aria-busy`, a spinner is a `status` named "loading", a toast is text in a live region, and a switch announces on or off.
