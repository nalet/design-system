# selection and bulk actions

how people choose rows in a data table and act on them: pick the selection mode by the actions, keep pressing a row apart from selecting it, give the whole selection one emphasized action in the selection toolbar, give each row one action menu, and say what happened in a toast.

## when to use

- when people act on many rows the same way: grab 8 episodes, restart 3 workloads, archive 40 items.
- in a [worklist](/design-system/patterns/worklist/), where the list is the work and its toolbar holds what to do with it.
- when some rows can't take the action right now: they stay in the list, can't be selected, and say why.

## when not to use

- **for one action on one row.** use the row's <C slug="action-menu" />, or let a press open the row. don't make people select a row to act on it.
- **to choose values in a form**, like the volumes a backup includes. use <C slug="checkbox">checkboxes</C> or a <C slug="multi-select" />: a form value is saved with the form, not acted on at once.
- **to narrow a list.** use the <C slug="filter-bar" /> or <C slug="filter-tabs" />. selecting rows hides nothing.
- **for records nobody acts on.** a short read-only list is a <C slug="table" />, with no selection at all.

## selection modes

pick the mode by the actions the list has, not by habit.

| mode | when | what changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **none** | the list has no bulk actions | nothing: a press on a row opens it |
| **single** | a list next to a detail, the two side by side | the selected row is highlighted; no checkbox |
| **multi** | the list has actions that apply to several rows at once | a checkbox column appears; nothing else changes |

today: the <C slug="data-table" /> has two of the three modes: none, and multi (its `selectable`). single selection, a list beside its detail, is **planned**.

## press versus select

- **a press opens, the checkbox selects.** a press on a row opens the row's detail, and a `>` at the row's end says that it will; the checkbox selects it. one click target never does both.
- **a row with no detail, in a multi-select list:** a press anywhere on the row toggles its checkbox.

today: the data table keeps the two apart as described — with a row press set, a click outside the row's controls (or `enter`) opens the row and only the checkbox and `space` select; without one, a click on the row toggles its checkbox. the `>` at the end of a row that opens is not drawn yet: it is **planned**.

## anatomy

1. **selection toolbar** — a <C slug="toolbar">selection toolbar</C> in place of the table's title bar: at least 45px tall, padding 8px 12px, 1px `--border`. with nothing selected it shows its heading, what the list holds ("40 missing"). with a selection it shows the count in `--cloud-blue` ("8 selected"), "clear", and "select all 37" when you offer it; its border turns `--state-information-border`, with a 3px `--cloud-blue` rule on its leading edge.
2. **the emphasized action** — the toolbar's one primary button, labelled with the count, "grab 8 selected". it is there only while something is selected.
3. **other actions** — default buttons, or items of the toolbar's "more actions" menu (always there when the toolbar is narrower than 560px). never emphasized.
4. **select-all checkbox** — the <C slug="data-table" />'s header checkbox, "select every visible row".
5. **row checkboxes** — one per row, named by the row: "select S04E07". a selected row is `--surface-selected` with a 2px `--cloud-blue` rule on its leading edge.
6. **row action menu** — one <C slug="action-menu" /> per row, in the last column (44px wide and right-aligned in the examples, with a visually hidden "actions" header), named by the row: "actions for S04E07". its trigger is a 28px ghost icon button.
7. **group row and group action** — the group's row with its count, and one small ghost button that acts on the group: "grab all 9".
8. **rows that can't be selected** — a disabled checkbox, and a <C slug="table-cells">status cell</C> that says why ("on its way").
9. **the confirmation** — a <C slug="toast" />, shown by the one toaster near the app's root: "8 episodes queued".

## select all

- **the header checkbox selects the rows that are loaded and match the filters.** the selection toolbar then offers the rest — `select all 2,409` for every match — and its count says which of the two is in effect.
- **after select all, the count says what will be acted on**: `2,409 selected`, never "all". the action's label carries the same number: `grab 2,409 selected`.
- it shows mixed while some rows are selected but not every one it covers, and checked while every one that can be selected is. pressed while checked, it clears the whole selection.
- "select all n" matters most right after the header checkbox: that is when people wonder whether they have "only these, or all of them".

today: the header checkbox selects every *visible* row — the rows on screen, not the rows past "show n more" and not the rows of collapsed groups, even though they are loaded. "select all n" is the selection toolbar's `selectAll`, and the table doesn't report which rows it shows, so the app works out when to offer it from what it passed: the example above offers it once its first page is selected.

## the selection toolbar

- **it takes the title bar's place while something is selected**: the count, `clear`, and the actions. with nothing selected, it is the list's title bar again.
- **one emphasized action**: the one people use most, labelled with a verb, its object and the count — "grab 8 selected". the count keeps it honest; people check it before they press. every other action is a default button or sits in the overflow menu.
- **an action that applies to only some of the selected rows stays enabled.** it acts on those it can and says what it skipped: `6 queued · 2 already on their way`.
- **a reversible action runs at once**, without a confirmation. one that deletes or can't be undone asks first, in a <C slug="modal" /> with the count in its title and on its button.

## per-row actions

- one action menu per row, in the last column, with the actions for that row: "grab now", "search again". no buttons in rows: a row of buttons on every row repeats the same labels down the page and buries the content.
- name the menu by the row, "actions for S04E07". disable an item that doesn't apply to a row rather than leaving it out, so every row's menu has the same shape.
- a per-row action doesn't clear the selection. if it leaves its row unselectable, drop that row from the selection.

## group actions

- a group row can carry one action for the whole group: a small ghost button. "grab all 9" acts on the season's rows that can be grabbed, and its label counts them.
- leave the action out when it would act on nothing.
- a group action works without a selection, and pressing it doesn't toggle the group.

## after the action

- update the rows: their status says the new state, "on its way". keep the rows people just acted on in the list, unless the list is defined by the old state (a "needs attention" tab); then say that they left.
- clear the selection the action used. the toolbar goes back to its heading and announces "selection cleared".
- confirm in a toast that counts what happened, and what didn't and why: "8 episodes queued"; "6 episodes queued · 2 skipped: on their way already". a toast is polite, leaves on its own and never carries an action.
- rows get skipped when their state changed after they were selected, or when a service refuses some. count them; don't hide them.
- when the action fails, or partly fails and people need to do something, say so in a <C slug="message-strip" /> above the list, with a way forward, not in a toast. see [message handling](/design-system/patterns/message-handling/).

## rows that can't be selected

- keep them in the list with a disabled checkbox, and say why in their status: "on its way". they are left out of every way of selecting, select all and ranges included.
- don't hide the checkbox or the row. people need to see that the row is there, and why it is left out.

## keeping a selection

- **sorting, growing ("show n more") and refreshing keep it.**
- **filtering keeps it.** rows hidden by a filter stay selected, and the toolbar says so: `8 selected · 3 hidden by filters`.
- **leaving the view clears it**, and so does a finished bulk action, which says its result in a toast.

today: the data table keeps the selection it is given — sorting, growing and new rows don't change it — and it never drops rows on its own: keeping or clearing the selection is the app's part. nothing counts the rows a filter hides; the toolbar can say it (`formatSelected`), from a count the app keeps. the [worklist](/design-system/patterns/worklist/) example predates this rule: it drops a selected row as soon as a filter hides it.

## keyboard

the toolbar is one tab stop, and the table's rows are another; the table's header controls (select all, the sort buttons) are tab stops of their own between them. from the toolbar, `tab` reaches the header controls, then the focused row, then that row's checkbox and action menu.

| where | key | does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| table | `↑` / `↓`, `home` / `end`, `page up` / `page down` | moves between rows. |
| table | `space` | toggles the focused row. |
| table | `shift` + click, `shift+space`, `shift+↑` / `shift+↓` | selects a range from the last row toggled. |
| table | `ctrl+a` / `cmd+a` | selects every loaded row that matches the filters. |
| table | `esc` | clears the selection. |
| table | `tab` | goes into the focused row's checkbox and action menu. |
| toolbar | `←` / `→`, `home` / `end` | moves between the toolbar's controls; it wraps around. |
| action menu | `enter`, `space`, `↓` | opens it on its first item (`↑` opens it on its last). |
| action menu | `esc` | closes it, with focus back on its trigger. |

today: `ctrl+a` / `cmd+a` selects every visible row, as the header checkbox does, and `esc` doesn't clear the selection yet: it is **planned**.

## accessibility

- names: each checkbox "select S04E07", the header checkbox "select every visible row", each menu "actions for S04E07", the toolbar the list's name ("missing episodes").
- announcements: the selection toolbar announces each change of the count politely ("8 selected", "selection cleared"), never its first state. the toast is a polite live region and never takes focus. the table itself announces nothing about selection: each row says whether it is selected.
- selection isn't shown by colour alone: the checkbox carries it, and the row's tint and rule add to it.
- focus stays put when the pressed control goes away. pressing "clear", "select all n" or the bulk action changes the selection and removes the button; the selection toolbar then holds focus itself. a group action that goes once its group has nothing left, or a focused row that leaves the list, hands focus to the table's tab stop.

today: the header checkbox's name ("select every visible row") and the "select" before each row's label are fixed english.

## content

- **the count** — `8 selected`, with thousands separators: `2,500 selected`. the toolbar formats it.
- **the bulk action** — a verb, its object and the count: `grab 8 selected`, `restart 3 workloads`. never `ok`, `apply` or `submit`.
- **select all n** — the number that will be selected: `select all 2,500`.
- **hidden rows** — the count after the selection: `8 selected · 3 hidden by filters`.
- **clear** — `clear`.
- **the heading** — what the list holds: `2,500 missing`, `40 workloads`.
- **row menu items** — a verb for the one row: `grab now`, `search again`. lowercase, no full stop.
- **group actions** — the count they act on: `grab all 9`.
- **the toast** — what happened, counted, in the object's words, then what was skipped and why: `6 episodes queued · 2 skipped: on their way already`. not `done`, `success` or `action completed`.
- **why a row can't be selected** — its status, in the object's words: `on its way`, `held`. not `disabled` or `n/a`.
