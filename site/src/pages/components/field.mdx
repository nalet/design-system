# field

the label, hint and error around one control, in a vertical stack, wired to the control so the parts stay connected for assistive tech.

<Example label="a field with a label and a hint">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- around every <C slug="input" />, <C slug="select" /> and <C slug="textarea" /> in a form. they have no label of their own.
- around a <C slug="checkbox" /> or a <C slug="switch" /> only to add a hint or an error; their own label already names them.
- whenever a value can be wrong: the field shows the error in place of the hint and marks the control invalid.

## when not to use

- **to label a group** of checkboxes, switches or radios. a field labels one control; use a native fieldset with a legend — see [forms and settings](/design-system/patterns/forms-and-settings/).
- **where a visible label does not fit**, such as a search box in a toolbar. use an <C slug="input" /> with an `aria-label` and a leading search icon, or the <C slug="filter-bar" />.
- **for a message about the whole form**, such as "could not save". use a <C slug="message-strip" /> above the form — see [message handling](/design-system/patterns/message-handling/).

## anatomy

1. **container** — a vertical stack with 8px between the parts.
2. **label** (optional) — inter 500, 13px, in `--fg-2`, shown lowercase.
3. **required marker** (optional) — ` *` after the label, in `--cloud-blue`, hidden from assistive tech.
4. **control** — the one control the field labels: an input, a select, a textarea, a checkbox or a switch.
5. **message** (optional) — the hint in `--fg-dim`, or the error in `--state-error`; inter 12px. the error replaces the hint.

## variants and states

a label with a hint, a required field, and an error. the error replaces the hint and turns the border of an input, select or textarea `--state-error`.

## behaviour

- the field adds no keys and no tab stop of its own: `tab` reaches the control, and the control keeps its own keys.
- a click on the label focuses the control, or toggles a checkbox or a switch.

the keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-inputs-field--docs).

## accessibility

- the label's text is the control's name. the lowercase look is css: write the label as you want it read.
- the hint or the error is read after the name and the value.
- with an error, the control is marked invalid for assistive tech too, not only by its border.
- around a checkbox or a switch, leave the field's label out. two labels name the control twice.

today:

- the marker is only drawn, and it is hidden from assistive tech; the field doesn't make the control required. make the control required itself, and say once per form what the asterisk means.
- an error is not announced when it appears. after a failed submit, move focus to the first invalid control so its error is read — see [message handling](/design-system/patterns/message-handling/).
- an error around a checkbox or a switch doesn't mark the box: it keeps its normal look, and the error text is the only cue.

## content

- **label** — a noun phrase for the value, lowercase, no colon: `release name`, `region`, `timeout (ms)`.
- **hint** — what is accepted and why, in one line: `lowercase letters, digits and hyphens`. don't repeat the label.
- **error** — what is wrong and how to fix it: `use 63 characters or fewer`. no blame, no codes, no `invalid input`. the error replaces the hint, so repeat the rule when people still need it.
- **required** — mark the required fields and explain the asterisk once at the top of the form. when most fields are required, mark the optional ones in their label instead: `description (optional)`.

  <Do caption="the error says how to fix it." column>

  <Dont caption="a verdict without a way out." column>
    <InputField label="host" value="api" error="invalid value" />

  <Do caption="the hint adds what the label cannot: the format." column>
    <InputField label="release name" hint="lowercase letters, digits and hyphens" placeholder="the-lantern-2024" />
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="a hint that repeats the label is noise, read out on every visit." column>
    <InputField label="release name" hint="the name of the release" placeholder="the-lantern-2024" />
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="around a checkbox, the field adds only the hint." column>
    <CheckboxField checkbox="send a weekly summary" hint="a summary of last week's releases, every monday" />
  </Do>

    <CheckboxField label="weekly summary" checkbox="send a weekly summary" hint="a summary of last week's releases, every monday" />
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
