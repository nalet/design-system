## implementation notes

- renders an `<a>` with `href`, a `<button type="button">` with `onClick`, your element with `as`, otherwise a `<div>`. a `disabled` tile is always a `<div>`: no link, no click, no focus.
- link, button and `as` tiles are interactive: they get the hover, the focus frame and the chevron. a display-only `<div>` gets none of them and is not focusable.
- `as` renders your component, e.g. a router link. today `Tile` passes `href` and `external` only to a plain `<a>`: give your component its destination yourself.
- `external`, with `href`, sets `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. today nothing on the tile says it opens a new tab.
- interactive tiles set the `data-nc-tile` attribute. in a [tile group](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile-group--docs) or a [portal](?path=/docs/patterns-portal--docs), the arrow keys move between the elements that carry it.
- every tile carries its title and description, lowercased, in `data-nc-search`. the [portal](?path=/docs/patterns-portal--docs) search matches against it.
- `icon` takes the lucide component, not an element: the tile draws it at 22px with a 1.75 stroke. the icon, the chevron and the delta's triangle are `aria-hidden`.
- the status dot has `role="img"` and the label `status: <status>`.
- `selected` is visual only. pass `aria-current="page"` on the current app's tile yourself; other attributes reach the element.
- today: a disabled tile is a `<div>` with `aria-disabled`, which assistive tech ignores on a plain element.
- today: focus shows on `:focus-visible` as a 2px `--cloud-blue` frame, not the `--focus-ring` outline that other controls use.
- transitions are off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- a tile fills its grid cell; on its own it needs a width, like the playground's 280px.
- every other attribute and the `ref` reach the element it renders (`aria-current`, `target`, `data-*`, …).

a link or button tile is a native `<a>` or `<button>`, so the browser provides the keys.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | moves focus to / from the tile. in a tile group, the group decides which tile is in the tab order — see [tile group](?path=/docs/components-navigation-tile-group--docs). |
| `enter` | link: follows it. button: presses it. |
| `space` | button: presses it. |
