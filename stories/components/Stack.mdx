## implementation notes

- renders a `div` with `display: flex` (`inline-flex` with `inline`), `box-sizing: border-box` and `min-width: 0`, so it can shrink inside other flex and grid layouts.
- `gap` takes 0, 4, 8, 12, 16 (the default), 24, 32, 48 or 64 and resolves to the `--s-*` tokens.
- a plain `div` with no role. for a real list, render `ul` and `li`, or pass `role="list"` to the stack and `role="listitem"` to each child.
- today: it is always a `div`; there is no `as` prop.
- every native `div` attribute passes through, and the `ref` reaches the `div`.
- your `style` is merged over the base layout, so it can change `display`, the direction and the `gap`; `align`, `justify` and `wrap` are applied after it and win.
