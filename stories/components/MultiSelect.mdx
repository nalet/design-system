# multi select

a facet that keeps several values: a trigger that shows the facet's name and what is chosen — `state`, `2 chosen` — and a panel of native checkboxes. it is a disclosure, not a menu: `tab` moves through the checkboxes, and `esc` or a press outside closes it.

<Canvas of={MultiSelectStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- in a [filter bar](?path=/docs/components-inputs-filter-bar--docs), for a facet that can keep several values at once: `state` — `missing` and `on its way`.
- for a handful of options that fit a small panel, each with a count of what it would keep, when you know it.

## when not to use

- **for one value at a time** — a kind, a time range. use a [select](?path=/docs/components-inputs-select--docs); the filter bar takes those as `select` facets.
- **for a setting, or a form that submits.** use [checkboxes](?path=/docs/components-inputs-checkbox--docs) in a [field](?path=/docs/components-inputs-field--docs). the multi select's checkboxes have no `name`, so a form sends none of them.
- **for many options.** the panel has no search and no maximum height. let people type in the [filter bar](?path=/docs/components-inputs-filter-bar--docs)'s search instead.
- **to run commands.** use an [action menu](?path=/docs/components-actions-action-menu--docs).

## anatomy

1. **trigger** — a `<button>` 34px tall (`md`, the default) or 28px (`sm`), with 0 × 12px padding (8px at `sm`), a 1px `--border-control` frame on `--bg-2`, square corners, jetbrains mono 13px (12px at `sm`). it holds:
   - the facet's **label**, in `--fg-muted`, lowercase;
   - the **summary**: `emptyText` (`any`) while nothing is chosen, the option's label when one is, `2 chosen` when several are. it turns `--cloud-blue` once something is chosen, and ends in an ellipsis when it is long;
   - a 12px **chevron** in `--cloud-blue`, which turns over while the panel is open.
2. **panel** — 4px under the trigger, lined up with its left edge, at `z-index: 120`: `--surface` fill, a 1px `--border-2` frame, a shadow, 8px of padding above and below. at least as wide as the trigger, at most 320px (or the viewport minus 16px).
3. **options** — a `fieldset` named by the facet's label, with one native [checkbox](?path=/docs/components-inputs-checkbox--docs) per option, 8px × 12px padding each, `--surface-2` on hover. an option's count, when you give one, follows its label in `--fg-dim`, mono 12px, tabular figures.
4. **clear** — `clear` and the facet's label (`clear state`), at the bottom under a 1px `--border` rule, only while something is chosen.

## variants and states

| state | the trigger shows |
| --- | --- |
| nothing chosen | the label, then `any` (`emptyText`) |
| one chosen | the label, then that option's label in `--cloud-blue` |
| several chosen | the label, then `2 chosen` in `--cloud-blue` |
| open | the chevron turned over, and the panel under it |
| disabled | 50% opacity. it can't be focused or opened |

## behaviour and keyboard

a disclosure: the trigger shows and hides a panel of native checkboxes. opening it doesn't move focus.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `enter` / `space` on the trigger | opens or closes the panel. focus stays on the trigger. |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | native: from the trigger into the checkboxes, then `clear`, then out. when focus leaves the multi select, the panel closes. |
| `space` on a checkbox | native: checks or unchecks it. |
| `esc` | closes the panel and puts focus back on the trigger. the key goes no further, so a modal around it stays open. |

- a click on the trigger opens or closes the panel; a press anywhere outside closes it.
- every change calls `onChange` at once, with the new list of values in the order they were chosen. there is no apply button.
- `clear` calls `onChange([])`.
- the arrow keys do nothing special: each checkbox is its own tab stop.
- values in `value` that aren't in `options` stay in `value`, but the summary doesn't count them.

## accessibility

- the trigger is a `<button>` with `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` pointing at the panel. it has no `aria-haspopup`: it opens a group of checkboxes, not a menu or a listbox.
- its name is its text: the facet's label, then the summary — "state 2 chosen". write the label so the two read as a phrase.
- the options are a `fieldset` named by the facet's label (a visually hidden `legend`), so the checkboxes are announced as one group. each is a native checkbox, named by its option's label and count.
- a closed panel is `hidden`: not read and not focusable.
- focus is never moved for you, except back to the trigger on `esc`.
- the trigger and `clear` show a 2px `--focus-ring` outline; each checkbox shows its own.

today:

- `clear` closes the panel and moves focus back to the trigger: the button itself goes once nothing is chosen.
- `clear` is fixed english — "clear" and the label — and can't be translated.
- the panel isn't rendered into `document.body`, so an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` cuts it off. it always opens below, from the trigger's left edge, with no flip near the viewport's edges, and it has no maximum height.

## content

- **label** — the facet's name, a noun: `state`, `aired`, `kind`.
- **options** — the values in the list's words, in an order that means something (the states in their usual order, not sorted by count): `missing`, `on its way`, `needs attention`.
- **counts** — how many items each option would keep, when you know it.
- **`emptyText`** — what no choice means: `any`, the default, or `all states`.

## props

`MultiSelect` passes no other attributes through; `className` goes on the wrapper around the trigger and the panel.
