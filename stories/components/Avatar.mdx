## implementation notes

- It renders a `<span>` with `role="img"` and `aria-label={name}`, holding the picture or the initials.
- With `decorative`, it has `aria-hidden="true"` and no role or name. Use it beside the name, or inside a link or a button that has a name, as `UserMenu` does.
- The initials are the first letter of the first and of the last word of `name`, in lowercase. A name of one word gives one letter.
- `initials` replaces them. They are written in lowercase too.
- The initials are in a span with `aria-hidden="true"`: the name is on the avatar itself.
- With `src`, it renders an `<img>` with `alt=""` and `object-fit: cover`, so a picture of any shape is cropped to the square from its middle.
- When the picture fails to load, the initials show. A new `src` gets its own try.
- The avatar takes no focus and has no handlers of its own.
- Its sizes are in rem, so it grows with the reader's text size.
- Under `data-nc-screen="tv"`, the initials are 14px at every size.
- Other `<span>` attributes and the `ref` reach the root element.
- axe-core finds no violations with initials, with a picture and as decoration.
