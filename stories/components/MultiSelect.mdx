## implementation notes

- It is controlled: pass `value` and `onChange`.
- Every change calls `onChange` at once, with the new list of values in the order they were chosen.
- `clear` calls `onChange([])`.
- Values in `value` that aren't in `options` stay in `value`, but the summary doesn't count them.
- The trigger is a `<button type="button">` with `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` pointing at the panel.
- The trigger has no `aria-haspopup`, because it opens a group of checkboxes, not a menu or a listbox.
- The options are a `fieldset`, named by the facet's label in a visually hidden `legend`, so a screen reader announces them as one group.
- Each option is a native [checkbox](?path=/docs/components-inputs-checkbox--docs).
- The checkboxes have no `name`, so a form sends none of them.
- A closed panel is `hidden`, so it is neither read nor focusable.
- Today: the panel isn't rendered into `document.body`, so an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` cuts it off. It is positioned under the trigger, at `z-index: 120`.
- `MultiSelect` passes no other attributes through.
- `className` goes on the wrapper around the trigger and the panel.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `enter` / `space` on the trigger | Opens or closes the panel. Focus stays on the trigger. |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | Native: from the trigger into the checkboxes, then `clear`, then out. When focus leaves the multi select, the panel closes. |
| `space` on a checkbox | Native: checks or unchecks it. |
| `esc` | Closes the panel and puts focus back on the trigger. The key goes no further, so a modal around it stays open. |
