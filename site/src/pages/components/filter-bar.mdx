# filter bar

what narrows a list: a search field, facets that keep one value (a select) or several (a multi select), `clear all`, and the count of what is left. on a narrow screen the facets fold behind one `filters` toggle.

  <div style={{ width: '100%' }}>
    <Playground client:visible />
  </div>
</Example>

## when to use

- above a list or a table, for the search and the two to four facets that narrow it.
- with the result count — `312 of 2,500` — so people see the filters work, and screen-reader users hear it.
- under <C slug="filter-tabs" /> and above the <C slug="toolbar">toolbar</C>, the way the [worklist](/design-system/patterns/worklist/) arranges them.

## when not to use

- **for a few key figures that each pick one subset.** use <C slug="filter-tabs" />.
- **to sort.** sort with the column headers of the <C slug="data-table" />.
- **for a form that is submitted** — settings, a new item. use <C slug="field">fields</C> and a save button, as in [forms and settings](/design-system/patterns/forms-and-settings/). the filter bar filters as values change and submits nothing.
- **for actions on the list.** use a <C slug="toolbar">toolbar</C>.

## anatomy

1. **container** — a search landmark, named "filters" unless you name it: a row that wraps, with 8px × 12px gaps.
2. **search** (optional) — a search field, 34px tall, with a square search glyph in front. it starts at 240px and grows or shrinks between 180 and 420px.
3. **facets** — a `select` facet is a visible label (jetbrains mono 12px, `--fg-muted`, lowercase) and a native <C slug="select" />, at least 128px wide; while it filters, its value shows in `--cloud-blue`. a `multiselect` facet is a <C slug="multi-select" /> at `md`.
4. **your controls** (optional) — after the facets. they fold with them.
5. **end** — pushed to the right: the result count (jetbrains mono 12px, `--fg-muted`, tabular figures) and `clear all`, a ghost `md` button. on a narrow bar, `clear all` moves to the end of the folded facets.
6. **filters toggle** (narrow only) — a default `md` button: `filters`, or `filters · 2` while two facets are set.

## variants and states

wide, the bar is one row that wraps when it has to.

narrow — below 640px by default, measured on the bar itself — the facets fold behind the `filters` toggle. the search and the toggle share the first line; the facets open in a column under them, with `clear all` at their end; the count keeps a line of its own, and takes no room while there is no count. the toggle counts the facets that are set, not the search.

a facet is **set** while it filters: a `select` facet whose value isn't its no-filter option, a `multiselect` facet with anything chosen. `clear all` is enabled while any facet is set or the search holds text, and disabled otherwise.

## behaviour

- the list filters as the values change: as people type in the search, keystroke by keystroke, and as they change a facet.
- `clear all` clears the search and every facet.
- `esc` in the search clears it.
- the `filters` toggle starts closed and keeps its state while the bar is on the page. folded facets are out of the tab order.

the keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-inputs-filter-bar--docs).

## accessibility

- the bar is a search landmark, so screen-reader users can jump to it. with two filter bars on one page, give each its own name.
- the search field has a name of its own, "search" unless you give it one. the placeholder is only a hint: it is gone with the first keystroke.
- a facet's label is its name: "kind".
- **the result count is announced**: each change is read whole — "312 of 2,500" — when the screen reader has a pause. it is how someone typing hears that the list changed, also when it is left empty.
- `clear all` disables itself once everything is clear, so it moves focus to the search field first.

today:

- narrow, `clear all` folds with the facets, so it can be reached only once `filters` is open.
- `clear all` and `filters` are fixed english and can't be translated.
- `clear all` only knows the search and the facets. a control of your own, after the facets, doesn't enable it.
- narrow, a bar with controls of your own or `clear all` but no facets shows no toggle: the controls and `clear all` stay folded away, with no way to open them.

## content

- **search** — the placeholder says what can be searched, `search titles`. give the search field's name the same words.
- **facet labels** — a noun: `kind`, `aired`, `state`.
- **the no-filter option** — first in every select, in plain words: `all`, `any time`.
- **result count** — what is left, of what: `312 of 2,500`, with thousands separators. while nothing filters, the total (`2,500`), or nothing.
- two to four facets. with more, the bar turns into a form.
