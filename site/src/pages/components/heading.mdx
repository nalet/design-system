# heading

mono display type for the page title (`h1`, 32px) and its sections (`h2`, 22px), optionally led by the blue `>` chevron.

<Example label="a page title with its first section">
  <SectionHeader />
</Example>

## when to use

- once per page, for its title (level 1), with the chevron on product screens.
- for the sections of a page (level 2).

## when not to use

- **for the title of a card or panel.** use the <C slug="card" />'s title.
- **for a third level or below.** today heading stops at `h2`. a sub-section gets a native `h3` through <C slug="text" />: `as="h3"`.
- **for emphasis or a big number.** headings are structure: screen-reader users jump between them. emphasis is <C slug="text" /> in its `ui` variant.
- **for the product name or logo.** use the lockup — see [brand](/design-system/styles/brand/).

## anatomy

1. **heading** — a real `h1` or `h2` with no margin, in the `h1` or `h2` [type role](/design-system/styles/typography/#the-roles): jetbrains mono in `--fg`, 32px or 22px.
2. **chevron** (optional) — a bold `>` in `--cloud-blue`, 0.4em before the text, hidden from assistive tech.

## variants and states

the level sets both the element and the size: an `h2` always looks like an `h2`.

## behaviour

- a heading is static: it takes no focus and isn't in the tab order.
- screen readers have their own keys for moving from heading to heading (e.g. `h` in browse mode). that is why the levels must follow the page's outline.

the keyboard map and the element it renders are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-heading--docs).

## accessibility

- it is a real `h1` or `h2`, so it is in the document outline and screen readers can jump to it.
- one `h1` per page; its sections are `h2`. pick the level for the outline, never for the size.
- the chevron is hidden from assistive tech: the heading's name is your text alone. don't type a `>` into the text — it would be read as "greater than".
- no case is changed: the text is shown and read exactly as written. write it lowercase, as the [voice](/design-system/foundations/content-voice/) asks.
- today: only two levels.

## content

- a lowercase noun phrase, no full stop: `volumes`, `recent runs`, `storage settings`.
- short. a heading that wraps at 32px mono is too long.
- the chevron once, on the page's `h1`. on every heading it stops meaning anything.

## do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="the chevron once, on the page title.">
    <Stack gap={8}>
      <Heading level={1} chevron>volumes</Heading>
      <Heading level={2}>recent runs</Heading>
    </Stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a chevron on every heading is decoration; it no longer marks the page.">
    <Stack gap={8}>
      <Heading level={2} chevron>recent runs</Heading>
      <Heading level={2} chevron>snapshots</Heading>
    </Stack>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="use chevron: the glyph is blue and stays out of the heading's name.">
    <Heading level={1} chevron>volumes</Heading>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a typed chevron is the wrong colour and is read aloud as 'greater than'.">
    <Heading level={1}>{'> volumes'}</Heading>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="a short noun phrase that names the section.">
    <Heading level={2}>recent runs</Heading>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a sentence as a heading wraps and buries the word people scan for.">
    <Heading level={2}>here you can see all of the runs from the last week</Heading>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
