# filter tabs

Filter tabs show the key figures of one list as a row of tabs, each a count over a label, such as “3” over “needs attention”. Choosing a tab filters the list below it.

    <Playground client:visible />
  </div>
</Example>

## when to use

- At the top of one list, to show the few parts of it that people act on, and to let them pick one: “missing”, “on their way”, “needs attention”.
- When each part's count is worth seeing before people choose it.
- For one part at a time, out of two to about six. The [worklist](/design-system/patterns/worklist/) puts them above its table.

## when to use something else

- **To switch between views of one thing**, such as its overview, logs and metrics. Use <C slug="tabs" />.
- **For filters people combine**, such as a kind, and a time, and a state. Use the facets of a <C slug="filter-bar" />, which combine.
- **For a figure that filters nothing.** Show the number as text, or as a <C slug="badge" /> beside what it counts.
- **To go to another page.** Use a link.

## anatomy

1. **Row of tabs.** The tabs side by side, with a thin line under the row. When they don't all fit, the row scrolls sideways, and the edge where more tabs are cut off fades out.
2. **Tab.** A block of <Term id="monospace">monospace</Term> text, aligned to the left, with a minimum width. The count sits on top, but the label is read first: “needs attention 3”.
3. **Count** (optional). A large number with <Term id="thousands-separator">thousands separators</Term>, such as “2,500”, in bright text or in the colour of the tab's <Term id="semantic-state">state</Term>.
4. **Label.** Small, lowercase and on one line, in a muted colour that brightens under the pointer and when the tab is selected.
5. **Selection.** A blue underline under the selected tab, drawn over the row's line.
6. **Panel.** Yours: the list the tabs filter. It isn't part of the component; [accessibility](#accessibility) says how it is named.

## variants and states

| state | looks |
| --- | --- |
| At rest | A muted label; the count in bright text, or in its state's colour |
| Under the pointer | A slightly lighter fill, and a bright label |
| Selected | A bright label, and a blue underline |
| Keyboard focus | An outline inside the tab |
| Disabled | Dimmed. It can't be focused or chosen, and the arrow keys pass over it |

### states on counts

A state colours the count, and only the count, when the number carries a meaning: error for “needs attention”, warning for “can't be checked”, information for “on their way”. These are the colours of the [status vocabulary](/design-system/patterns/status-vocabulary/).

Leave the state off for counts that only say how many, such as “missing” or “not yet aired”: they stay plain. A tab without a count shows no state at all. The example at the top of this page uses both kinds.

Plain counts, with no state anywhere:

Leave the count out when the number isn't known. The tab then shows its label alone.

## behaviour

- A click on a tab selects it.
- The row is one <Term id="tab-stop">tab stop</Term>: <Kbd>tab</Kbd> goes to the selected tab, and the next <Kbd>tab</Kbd> leaves the row.
- The selection follows <Term id="focus">focus</Term>. The left and right arrow keys move to the previous or the next tab and select it, so every press filters the list. <Kbd>home</Kbd> and <Kbd>end</Kbd> go to the first and the last tab.
- Because every press filters, keep the filtering quick. Usually it is, as the list is filtered on the page itself. For a list that is slow to load, let the selection settle before the list loads.

The full keyboard map is on the filter tabs' [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-navigation-filter-tabs--docs).

## accessibility

- The row of tabs must have a name: what it filters, and by what, such as “filter missing by state”. Only the selected tab is in the tab order.
- A tab's name is its label, then its count: “needs attention 3”.
- The list below is the tabs' panel, and it is named after the selected tab: “needs attention 3”.
- The colour of a count is a second signal; the label says what the count means. A count has no <Term id="glyph">glyph</Term>, unlike a <C slug="status" />, so never let the colour say what the label doesn't.
- When a count changes, it isn't announced.

Today: <Term id="disabled">disabled</Term> tabs can't take focus, so people who use a <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term> don't learn that they exist.

## content

- **Label.** What the tab keeps, in the list's own words: “needs attention”, “on their way”, “can't be checked”. A few words, in lowercase.
- **Count.** Exact, not rounded. Leave it out when it isn't known, rather than showing “0”.
- **First tab.** The whole list, such as “missing” or “all”, so there is always a way back.
- **The row's name.** What it filters, and by what: “filter missing by state”.

## specs

| part | in code | spec |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Row | `FilterTabs`, named by `label` (required) | `role="tablist"`, a 1px `--border` rule below. Scrolls sideways when the tabs don't fit; a cut-off edge fades out over 48px. |
| Tab | `tabs`: `{ id, label, count, state, disabled }` | A `button` with `role="tab"`. At least 96px wide, padding 8px 16px 12px, JetBrains Mono, left-aligned. The label comes first, so it is read first; the count is drawn on top of it, 2px above. |
| Count | `count` (leave it out when unknown) | 20px, weight 600, line height 1.1, letter-spacing -0.01em, tabular figures, with thousands separators. `--fg`, or the colour of its state. |
| Label | `label` | 12px, weight 500, line height 1.3, lowercased with `text-transform`, one line, `--fg-muted`; `--fg` on hover and when selected. |
| Selection | `value` and `onChange` | A 2px `--cloud-blue` underline, laid over the row's rule. |
| Panel | `panelId` and `idPrefix` | Yours: `role="tabpanel"`, named by the selected tab with `aria-labelledby={filterTabId(idPrefix, value)}`. Every tab points at it with `aria-controls`. |

| state | spec |
| --- | --- |
| Rest | Label in `--fg-muted`; count in `--fg` or in its state's colour |
| Hover | `--surface` fill, label in `--fg` |
| Selected | Label in `--fg`, a 2px `--cloud-blue` underline |
| Focus | A 2px `--focus-ring` outline inside the tab |
| Disabled | `disabled` on the tab: 45% opacity, a not-allowed cursor. Not focusable, not chosen, skipped by the arrow keys |

- **States on counts:** `state` on a tab. `'information'`, `'success'`, `'warning'` and `'error'` colour the count in `--state-information`, `--state-success`, `--state-warning` and `--state-error`; `'neutral'`, or no state, leaves it `--fg`.
- **Keys:** automatic activation, so the selection follows focus. Only the selected tab has `tabindex="0"`. The left and right arrow keys select the previous or the next enabled tab, wrapping at the ends; <Kbd>home</Kbd> and <Kbd>end</Kbd> select the first and the last.
- **Motion:** colour, underline and fill change in 120ms `ease`.
