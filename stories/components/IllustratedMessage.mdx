# illustrated message

what a list or a panel shows when it has nothing to show: a line illustration, a title that says what is true, a description that says what to do, and at most one primary action. five kinds — `empty`, `done`, `search`, `error`, `unknown` — one for each reason there is nothing.

<Canvas of={IllustratedMessageStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- in place of a list, a table or a panel that has nothing to show, once you know why: nothing is there yet, nothing is left to do, the filters match nothing, it couldn't load, or it can't be told yet.
- at the size of what it replaces: `sm` inside a panel or a table (a [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs) takes it in its `empty` prop), `md` for a section, `lg` for a whole page.

## when not to use

- **while the list is still loading.** use `loading` on the [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs), or a [spinner](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-spinner--docs). say "nothing here" only once you know.
- **above content that still shows** — the rows from 4 minutes ago, with an error on top. use a [message strip](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-message-strip--docs).
- **for one item's state** in a list that is otherwise there. use an [object status](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-object-status--docs) in its row.
- **to confirm an action that just finished.** use a [toast](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-toast--docs).

## anatomy

1. **container** — a centred column. 32px × 16px padding at `md`, 16px × 12px at `sm`, 64px × 16px at `lg`.
2. **illustration** — a 160 × 120 line drawing of a square terminal window: the frame in `--border-control`, quiet lines in `--border-2`, one accent in the kind's colour. 96px wide at `sm`, 160px at `md`, 220px at `lg`, with 12, 16 or 24px under it. hidden from assistive tech.
3. **title** — a real heading (`h2` unless you set `headingLevel`): jetbrains mono 600, 14, 16 or 22px by size, line-height 1.3, `--fg`, shown lowercase.
4. **description** (optional) — inter 13px / 1.55 in `--fg-muted`, at most 46ch wide, 8px under the title.
5. **actions** (optional) — centred, 8px apart, 16px under the text.

## variants and states

### kinds

pick the kind by what is true, not by the picture.

| kind | picture | accent | when | what it offers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `empty` (default) | a prompt with nothing typed after it | `--cloud-blue` | nothing here yet: a new list, nothing tracked | the action that adds the first item, if there is one |
| `done` | the prompt, and a check | `--state-success` | nothing left to do: the good end of a worklist | usually nothing |
| `search` | rows under a square lens that finds none | `--cloud-blue` | the search or the filters match nothing | `clear filters` |
| `error` | a line that doesn't connect | `--state-error` | it couldn't load | `try again` |
| `unknown` | a question that can't be answered yet | `--state-warning` | it can't be told yet, for example because the holdings are unknown | the step that makes it known: `ask the catalog again` |

<Canvas of={IllustratedMessageStories.Kinds} />

`empty` and `done` both mean "nothing here", for opposite reasons: `empty` before anything has happened, `done` after everything has. `search` is never `empty`: the data is there, and the filters hide it.

### sizes

`sm` (96px illustration, 14px title), `md` (160px, 16px, the default) and `lg` (220px, 22px).

<Canvas of={IllustratedMessageStories.Sizes} />

### actions

pass the actions as buttons in `actions`: at most one `primary`, and an optional second, `default` or `ghost`.

<Canvas of={IllustratedMessageStories.WithAction} />

### heading level

the title is an `h2` unless you set `headingLevel` (2 to 6). match the page's outline: inside a section whose heading is an `h2`, use `3`.

## behaviour and keyboard

an illustrated message is static. the only things in it that take focus are the actions you pass.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | moves to and from your actions, in order. the picture, the title and the description take no focus. |
| `enter` / `space` | native: activates the focused action. |

- it doesn't announce itself when it appears: it is not a live region. when it replaces a list because the filters changed, the [filter bar](?path=/docs/components-inputs-filter-bar--docs)'s live result count is what a screen reader hears.

## accessibility

- the illustration is decoration: `aria-hidden="true"` and `focusable="false"`. the title says what is true.
- the title is a real heading, so people who move through a page by its headings find it. keep the outline in order with `headingLevel`.
- the accent colour repeats the kind; the words carry it.
- axe-core finds no violations in any kind, with a title, a description and an action.

today:

- pressing the action usually removes the message, and the button with it, so focus falls back to the page: after `clear filters`, nothing has focus. move it yourself, to the search field for instance.
- nothing holds you to one primary action: `actions` renders whatever it is given.

## content

- **title** — what is true, in a few words: `nothing missing`, `no matches`, `couldn't load`, `nothing tracked yet`, `nothing can be checked yet`. lowercase, no full stop.
- **description** — one or two sentences: why, and what the person can do. `nothing fits these filters.` `import a list to start tracking.`
- **actions** — one primary at most. when the view already has its one emphasized action (in a toolbar, say), make this one `default`.
- calm: say what is true. no apology, no joke, no `oops`.
- name the way out for each kind: `clear filters` for `search`, `try again` for `error`, the step that makes it known for `unknown`.
