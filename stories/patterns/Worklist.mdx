## implementation notes

- `Worklist` lays the parts out; the parts are the components: `tabs` are `FilterTab`s, `messages` message strips, `filterBar` a `FilterBar`, `toolbar` a `Toolbar` or `SelectionToolbar`, and `children` the `DataTable` — or the `IllustratedMessage` when there is nothing to show.
- it renders a `section` named by its title. `headingLevel` sets the title's level: 1 when the worklist is the page, 2 inside an app shell that owns the `h1`.
- with `tabs` and `activeTab`, the tabs are a `tablist` and the content below them its `tabpanel`, labelled by the selected tab; `tabsLabel` names the tab list ("filter the list" by default).
- the toolbar and the table header stick while the list scrolls (`stickyToolbar`, on by default): the worklist measures its toolbar and offsets the table's sticky header under it with `--nc-dt-sticky-top`.
- pass the tab and the filters as the table's `growingResetKey`: growing starts over when the view changes, and a refresh of the data keeps the person's place.
- give the table an `aria-label` and a `rowLabel`, so each checkbox and each row menu says what it acts on.
- it passes other `section` attributes, and its `ref`, through.
