# spinner

A spinner says “working on it” when you can't say how long the wait will be. It is a faint square with a gap running round it: the one moving thing on an otherwise still screen.

## when to use

- For a short wait of unknown length in a small part of the screen: one value, a count, the content of a panel.
- In place of a value while it loads, with a name, read out by <Term id="screen-reader">screen readers</Term>, that says which value is loading.
- Beside a short line of text that says what is loading.

## when to use something else

- **For an action someone started.** Use the busy state of the <C slug="button" />, or of the <C slug="icon-button" />. The spinner then sits inside the control that was pressed, and the button ignores a second press.
- **While a table's rows load.** Use the loading state of the <C slug="data-table" />. It marks the table as busy and runs a thin line along its top edge, so the wait shows where the rows will be.
- **For the result.** When the work ends, replace the spinner with what happened: the content itself, a <C slug="toast" /> for a background job that finished, or a <C slug="banner" /> for a failure.
- **For long work whose progress is known.** Today there is no progress bar. Say the progress in a line of <C slug="text">text</C> next to the spinner: “12 of 40 copied”.

## anatomy

1. **Frame.** A small square outline with square corners, in blue unless it is given another colour. It is drawn faintly, it is always there, and it never moves.
2. **Lit part.** Three quarters of the frame, in full colour, with square ends. It starts in the middle of the top edge, so no corner ever shows a notch.
3. **Gap.** The quarter that isn't lit. It runs round the frame; nothing else moves.

The line gets a little thicker as the spinner gets bigger.

## variants and states

A spinner can be any size, and its line grows with it. Buttons hold small ones, and the same small size sits well beside a line of small text. On its own, a spinner is a little bigger, and a spinner for a whole panel bigger still. The exact sizes are in the [specs](#specs).

A spinner is blue by default, and on its own it doesn't take on the colour of the text around it. Inside a button, it takes the colour of the button's label, so it still shows on the emphasized button's blue fill. Anywhere else it can take any colour: one of the system's colour <Term id="token">tokens</Term>, such as the muted text colour, or the colour of the text around it.

## behaviour

- The frame stays still while the gap runs round it at a slow, steady pace, without end. The spinner takes no focus.
- With <Term id="reduced-motion">reduced motion</Term> on, it stands still, three quarters lit, and its name carries the state.
- It never stops on its own. Remove it when the work ends.

What to show while something loads, and when, is described in [loading and progress](/design-system/patterns/loading-and-progress/).

The full keyboard map is on the spinner's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-spinner--docs).

## accessibility

- A screen reader knows a spinner as a status message, named “loading” unless it is given a name of its own.
- **Name the thing that loads**, such as “loading volumes”, rather than keeping the general “loading”.
- Beside visible text that says the same thing, the name is read twice. Hide the spinner from <Term id="assistive-technology">assistive technology</Term>, and let the text speak.
- With reduced motion on, nothing moves, so the words carry the state: the spinner's name, or the text beside it.
- The lit part stands out from every background with a <Term id="contrast">contrast</Term> of at least 3 to 1, and the default blue with at least 6.8 to 1; the faint frame is only decoration. A colour of your own must keep 3 to 1.

Today:

- The spinner's name isn't text on the page, and a <Term id="live-region">live region</Term> announces changes to its text. A spinner that is added to the page carries no text, so most screen readers won't announce it appearing. When the wait matters, put a short message in a live region that is already on the page, and change its text.

## content

- The name says what is loading, with no <Term id="ellipsis">ellipsis</Term>: “loading volumes”, “checking storage-01”. It is read out, not shown.
- Text beside it says the same words, in sentence case: “Loading volumes”. No “please wait”.
- When a wait runs past a few seconds, say more: what is happening, and whether people can leave the page in the meantime.

## specs

| size | where | line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12px | In a small button | 2px |
| 14px | In a medium or a large button, and beside 13px text | 2px |
| 16px (the default) | On its own | 2px |
| 24 to 40px | For a panel | 2px up to 24px, 3px at 40px |

| setting | in code | default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size | `size`, in px; the frame is `size` × `size` | `16` |
| Colour | `color`, any CSS colour: a token such as `var(--fg-muted)`, or `currentColor` to follow the text around it | `--cloud-blue`, set by the spinner's own CSS |
| Name | `label`, which becomes the `aria-label` of the `role="status"` element | `loading` |
| Hidden beside text | `aria-hidden="true"` | Not hidden |

- **Frame:** one square path the spinner's size, at 20% opacity (`stroke-opacity: 0.2`). It never moves.
- **Lit part:** the same path drawn again, as a dash three quarters (75%) of the frame long, with square ends (`stroke-linecap: butt`) and sharp corners (`miter`). The path starts in the middle of the top edge.
- **Gap:** the other quarter (25%) of the dash pattern, which travels round the frame.
- **Line:** `max(2, round(size / 16))` px: 2px up to 24px, then a pixel more for every 16px, so 3px at 40px.
- **Motion:** the dash's offset moves by the frame's length, one lap in 4s, `linear`, without end. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` there is no animation.
- **In a button:** a 12px spinner in a small button and 14px in a medium or large one, with `color: inherit`, so it takes the label's colour. In an icon button it takes the button's colour.
- **Contrast:** the lit part at least 3:1 against every background, and `--cloud-blue` at least 6.8:1. The faint frame is decoration. A custom colour must keep 3:1.
- **Focus:** none.
