## implementation notes

- `Tooltip` renders an inline-flex `<span>` with `position: relative`, and `className` goes on it.
- That span holds two spans: the wrapper around your `children`, and the bubble, with `role="tooltip"`.
- The wrapper has `tabindex="0"`, so it can take keyboard focus, and `aria-describedby` pointing at the bubble, so the bubble is read as its description.
- The bubble's ID comes from `useId`.
- It is CSS only, with no script.
- The bubble shows while the wrapper is hovered or has keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`), with a 100ms opacity transition.
- The wrapper sets `outline: none`, so it shows no focus outline of its own.
- The bubble is `position: absolute`, 6px from the trigger, at `z-index: 50`, with `pointer-events: none`, `white-space: nowrap` and `max-width: 240px`.
- `side` places the bubble with CSS alone, so an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` clips it.
- `Tooltip` passes no other attributes through.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | Moves focus to the wrapper, which shows the bubble. When your trigger is itself focusable, the next `tab` moves into it and the bubble hides. |
| `esc` | Nothing. Today: the bubble can't be dismissed from the keyboard. It hides when focus leaves the wrapper. |
