# table cells

Table cells are three ready-made cells for the columns of a <C slug="data-table" />, so every table says the same things the same way. The status cell shows a status and its reason, the date cell a date and how long ago it was, and the identifier cell what names the row.

<Example column label="the three cells in a data table">
  <CellsInATable />
</Example>

## when to use

- **Status cell.** For the state column of a data table: one status per row, from the object's one table of states, with the reason or the next step under it when there is one.
- **Date cell.** For a date column where age matters: “aired”, “last searched”, “created”, “expires”.
- **Identifier cell.** For the <Term id="row-header">row header</Term> column: what people call the row, with a quieter second line that says more.

## when to use something else

- **For a status outside a table**, such as beside a heading, in a card or in a sentence. Use a <C slug="status" /> on its own. The status cell only adds the detail line, sized for a row.
- **For a message about the whole list**, such as “105 titles can't be checked”. That is a <C slug="banner" /> above the table, not a status in every row.
- **For tags, kinds and categories.** They aren't states. Use a <C slug="badge" />.
- **For numbers and sizes.** Use a plain column, aligned to the right, with the unit in its header. The content rules of the <C slug="table" /> show how.

## anatomy

### status cell

1. **Wrapper.** The status, with its detail close under it, at the cell's <Term id="leading-edge">leading edge</Term>. It is never wider than the cell.
2. **Status.** A <C slug="status" />: a small chip in the state's colour, with the state's <Term id="glyph">glyph</Term> and a few words in lowercase <Term id="monospace">monospace</Term>, on one line.
3. **Detail** (optional). A quieter line under the status, in small monospace, at most 44 characters wide. At most two lines show. It can break anywhere, so a long code can't widen the column.

### date cell

1. **Wrapper.** The date and how long ago it was, side by side on one line; in a narrow column, the second part wraps under the date. Both are in monospace with <Term id="tabular-figures">tabular figures</Term>, so dates line up down a column.
2. **Date.** The date itself, in lowercase: “12 oct 2026”, with the day, the short month and the year. Resting the pointer on it shows the full weekday, date and time.
3. **How long ago** (on unless you turn it off). In a quieter colour: “5d ago” on screen, “5 days ago” for a <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term>.
4. **Empty.** A dash in the quieter colour, read as “no date”.

### identifier cell

1. **Wrapper.** The title and the text, one above the other, close together.
2. **Title.** What people call the row, in monospace and the brightest text colour: “S04E07”, “worker-2”.
3. **Text** (optional). A second line in the <Term id="body-face">body face</Term>, smaller and quieter, on one line. It ends in an <Term id="ellipsis">ellipsis</Term> when its box is narrower than the text.

## variants and states

### status cell

The state sets the colour, the glyph and what a screen reader hears first; there is no setting for the colour. The five states, as the [status vocabulary](/design-system/patterns/status-vocabulary/) uses them:

| state | means | for instance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral | A plain fact | “missing” |
| Information | Under way, or worth knowing | “on its way” |
| Success | Done and good, and not for a problem | “held” |
| Warning | Works, but someone should look | “can't be checked” |
| Error | Failed or blocked; needs action | “needs attention” |

The detail is the line under the status: why it is so, or what happens next. Like every status, the one in a cell can also be shown as text: without the chip, for dense rows, keeping the glyph and slightly larger words.

### date cell

How long ago counts whole calendar days, in the viewer's time zone:

| how far | on screen | read as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The same day | “today” | “today” |
| One day | “yesterday”, “tomorrow” | “yesterday”, “tomorrow” |
| 2 to 13 days | “5d ago”, “in 3d” | “5 days ago”, “in 3 days” |
| 14 to 59 days | “7w ago” | “7 weeks ago” |
| 60 days to just under two years | “20mo ago” | “20 months ago” |
| Two years or more | “2y ago” | “2 years ago” |

The title is what identifies the row; the text is a second line, when there is one to give. The title can be a link to the row's own page.

## behaviour

- The cells are text. None of them takes focus or handles a key: the keys belong to the row they sit in, and the <C slug="data-table" /> handles them. <Kbd>tab</Kbd> passes the cells by, unless an identifier's title is a link.
- The date cell works out “today” when it is drawn. It doesn't update on its own: a page left open past midnight says “today” about yesterday, until the cell is drawn again.
- The texts that show under the pointer, a status detail in full and a date's full time, are the browser's own tooltips.

The full keyboard map is on the table cells' [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-data-display-table-cells--docs).

## accessibility

- **Status cell.** Screen readers skip the glyph: the words carry the state, and warnings and errors are read with their state first, as in “error: needs attention”. The detail is there in full for a screen reader, so it reads all of it, including the lines cut off on screen.
- **Date cell.** The date is read as it is written, “12 Oct 2026”, because the lowercase is only a style. Then comes how long ago, in words: “5 days ago”. Screen readers skip the short “5d ago”. An empty value is read as “no date”.
- **Identifier cell.** Plain text. As the row header, it names the row for every other cell in it.

Today:

- A status detail longer than two lines, and a date's full time, show only in the browser's own tooltip: under a mouse pointer. People who use a keyboard or a touch screen can't open it. Screen readers read the whole detail, but the time of day is only in that tooltip, which most of them don't read for plain text.
- A detail that isn't plain text, such as one that holds a link, gets no tooltip at all, so the lines that are cut off can't be seen on screen.
- The spoken words are fixed, in English: “no date”, and the words for how long ago, such as “5 days ago”, whatever the language of the dates.
- An identifier's second line that doesn't fit is cut short with an ellipsis, and has no tooltip.

## content

- **Status.** The state in the object's own words, one to three words: “missing”, “on its way”, “needs attention”. Write the vocabulary once, as the [status vocabulary](/design-system/patterns/status-vocabulary/) describes, and use it in every row.
- **Detail.** Why, or what happens next, on one short line: “nothing found · again in 6h”, “waiting for the catalog”. A reason a person understands, not a raw error or a code.
- **Dates.** Give the cell the date itself and let it write the date out; don't turn the date into text beforehand. Keep how long ago where it matters how recent a date is, as for “aired” or “last searched”. Turn it off where it doesn't, as for a fixed release date.
- **Identifier.** The title is what people call the row, “S04E07” or “worker-2”; the text is its name, spelled the way the name is spelled: “The Long Way Round”. Don't use an internal id, a long string of letters and digits, as the title.

## specs

| cell | part | spec |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status (`StatusCell`) | Wrapper | A `flex` column with 3px between its lines, its items at the start, `max-width: 100%`, `min-width: 0`. |
| Status | Status | A status: a 20px chip with 8px side padding, a 1px `--state-*-border`, a `--state-*-bg` fill and `--state-*` text, the state's 12px glyph, JetBrains Mono 11px, lowercase, on one line. As text (`appearance="text"`): no chip, the glyph and 12px text. |
| Status | Detail | `detail`. JetBrains Mono 11px, line-height 1.45, `--fg-dim`, `max-width: 44ch`, clamped to two lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2`), `overflow-wrap: anywhere`. In the page in full. A string detail is also its `title`, the native tooltip; a detail that isn't a string gets none. |
| Date (`DateCell`) | Wrapper | `inline-flex`, wrapping, on one baseline, the parts 8px (`--s-2`) apart. JetBrains Mono 12px, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. |
| Date | Date | A `<time>` with the ISO `datetime`, in `--fg-2`, lowercased with `text-transform`. Its `title` holds the full weekday, date and time (`dateStyle: 'full'`, `timeStyle: 'short'`). |
| Date | How long ago | `relative` (default `true`), in `--fg-dim`. The short form is `aria-hidden`; the long form is visually hidden. |
| Date | Empty | `emptyText`, by default an em dash (—), `aria-hidden`, in `--fg-dim`, and a visually hidden “no date”. |
| Identifier (`IdentifierCell`) | Wrapper | An `inline-flex` column with 2px between its lines, `min-width: 0`. |
| Identifier | Title | `title`, any node, a link included. JetBrains Mono 13px, `--fg`. |
| Identifier | Text | `text`. Inter 12px, `--fg-muted`, on one line (`white-space: nowrap`), with `text-overflow: ellipsis` and no tooltip. |

| date setting | in code | default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The date | `value`: a `Date`, an ISO string or epoch milliseconds | An empty or unreadable value shows the empty text |
| Language of the date | `locale` | `'en-GB'`: “12 oct 2026” |
| Format | `format`, an `Intl.DateTimeFormatOptions` | Day, short month, year: `{ day: 'numeric', month: 'short', year: 'numeric' }` |
| How long ago | `relative` | `true` |
| Today | `now` | The current time; pinned in stories and tests |

- **How long ago, the rule:** whole calendar days in the viewer's time zone. Under 14 days it counts days, under 60 weeks (days ÷ 7), under 730 months (days ÷ 30.44), then years (days ÷ 365.25), each rounded. `relativeDay(then, now)` is the same wording as a function, for a date outside a cell: it returns `{ short: '5d ago', long: '5 days ago' }`.
- **Fixed words:** `emptyText` changes only the visible dash, and `locale` only the absolute date; “no date” and the words for how long ago stay English.
- **Status cell props:** every status prop (`state`, `text` or children, `icon`, `appearance`, `stateLabel`) and `detail`. Spread a vocabulary entry in: `<StatusCell {...BACKLOG_STATES[row.state]} detail={why(row)} />`.
- **Identifiers:** an internal id, such as a UUID, is never the title.
- **Focus:** none in any cell, unless an identifier's `title` is a link.
