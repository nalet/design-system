# divider

a 1px rule in `--border` (`--border-2` when `heavy`), horizontal or vertical. a horizontal rule can carry a short mono label on the line.

<Example label="a rule between two groups">
  <Horizontal />
</Example>

## when to use

- to separate two groups of content in the same container, when space alone doesn't make the break clear.
- vertically, between groups of short items in a row: counts, sizes, a timestamp.
- with a label, for a quiet break in a long list: `older`, `archived`.

## when not to use

- **as a frame or an edge.** to outline a region, use the border of a <C slug="box" /> or a <C slug="card" />. a divider is a break between things, not a box around them.
- **for a section title.** a label on the line isn't read as a heading (see [accessibility](#accessibility)). use a <C slug="heading" />.
- **between every item of a list.** space already separates the items: use a <C slug="stack" />'s gap. a <C slug="table" /> rules its own rows.

## anatomy

1. **rule** — 1px of `--border`, or `--border-2` when `heavy`. horizontal: the full width. vertical: 1px wide, stretched to the height of its row, at least 16px tall.
2. **label** (optional, horizontal only) — centered between two rules, 12px from each: jetbrains mono 12px, letter-spacing 0.04em, `--fg-dim`, shown lowercase, never wraps.

the divider has no margin. the space around it comes from its parent's gap.

## variants and states

`heavy` uses `--border-2`: a little stronger, still 1px.

## behaviour

- a divider takes no focus and has no action: `tab` skips it.

the keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-divider--docs).

## accessibility

- screen readers announce a divider as a separator.
- today: most screen readers won't read the label: it sits inside the separator, and a separator's contents aren't read. if the words matter, give the divider the same text as its name, or better, use a heading.
- a divider that only decorates — inside something whose structure is already clear — can be hidden from assistive tech, so it isn't announced.
- the rule is a decorative hairline, about 1.1:1 to 1.5:1 against the canvases, by design (see [shape](/design-system/styles/shape/#lines)). never let a divider be the only thing that tells two groups apart: space and headings carry the structure.

## content

- a label is one or two lowercase words naming what follows: `older`, `archived`, `2023`.
- no punctuation, no sentences, nothing people need to act on.

## do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="a label as a quiet break in a long list.">
    <Stack gap={12}>
      <Text variant="ui">run #1543</Text>
      <Divider label="older" />
      <Text variant="ui">run #1502</Text>
    </Stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a section title on the rule. screen-reader users can't find it or jump to it; use a heading.">
    <Stack gap={12}>
      <Divider label="storage settings" />
      <Text variant="muted">412 gb used of 1 tb</Text>
    </Stack>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="items in a list are separated by space.">
    <Stack gap={12}>
      <Text variant="ui">storage-01</Text>
      <Text variant="ui">storage-02</Text>
      <Text variant="ui">storage-03</Text>
    </Stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="a rule per item is noise, and each one is announced as a separator.">
    <Stack gap={12}>
      <Text variant="ui">storage-01</Text>
      <Divider />
      <Text variant="ui">storage-02</Text>
      <Divider />
      <Text variant="ui">storage-03</Text>
    </Stack>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="a section starts with a heading; space sets it apart.">
    <Stack gap={8}>
      <Heading level={2}>backups</Heading>
      <Text variant="muted">every night at 02:00, kept 14 days</Text>
    </Stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="rules above and below fake a frame. use a card or a box border.">
    <Stack gap={8}>
      <Divider />
      <Text variant="muted">every night at 02:00, kept 14 days</Text>
      <Divider />
    </Stack>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
