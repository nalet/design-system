## implementation notes

- renders a `<div>` around a `<div role="tablist">`. each tab is a `<button type="button">` with `role="tab"` and `aria-selected`; only the selected tab has `tabindex="0"`, the others `-1`.
- `Tabs` renders no panel, no ids and no `aria-controls`. give your panel `role="tabpanel"`, name it after the selected tab with `aria-label`, and give it `tabIndex={0}` when it holds nothing focusable. the with-panel story does this.
- a disabled item is a disabled `<button>`, so it leaves the tab order, and the arrow keys skip it.
- a focused tab shows a 2px `--focus-ring` outline on `:focus-visible`, inside the tab.
- the labels are lowercased with `text-transform`; the dom keeps the text you wrote.
- today: `aria-label` and other attributes go on the wrapper `<div>`, not on the tablist, so the tablist can't be named.
- today: the tabs have no ids, so a panel can't point at its tab with `aria-labelledby`.
- today: a `value` that matches no item leaves every tab at `tabindex="-1"`, and the row can't be reached with `tab`.
- other `<div>` attributes and the `ref` reach the wrapper around the tablist.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` | moves focus into the row, onto the selected tab — the only tab with `tabindex="0"` — and out of it on the next press. |
| `→` / `←` | selects the next / previous enabled tab, wrapping at the ends, calls `onChange` and moves focus to it. |
| `enter` / `space` | native button: selects the focused tab. |
| `home` / `end` | selects and focuses the first / last enabled tab. |
