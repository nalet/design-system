# switch

A square on/off toggle for a setting that applies the moment it is flipped.

<Example label="a switch">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- For one setting that takes effect at once, with no save button: `email alerts`, `maintenance mode`.
- In a settings list or panel: one switch per setting, each with its own label.

## when not to use

- **Inside a form that is saved with a button.** Use a <C slug="checkbox" />. A switch promises an immediate effect that the form does not have.
- **For a choice between two things that are not on and off** (`daily` or `weekly`). Use a <C slug="select" />, or <C slug="tabs" /> to change the view.
- **To start an action** such as `deploy release`. Use a <C slug="button" />: an action happens once, a switch holds a state.

## anatomy

1. **Hit area** — the track, an 8px gap and the label text.
2. **Track** — square, 36 × 20px (`md`) or 30 × 16px (`sm`), 2px inner padding. Off: 1px `--border-control` border on `--bg-2`. On: `--cloud-blue` fill and border.
3. **Thumb** — square, 14px (`md`) or 10px (`sm`). Off: `--fg-dim`, at the left. On: `--bg`, slid to the right in 0.14s.
4. **Label** (optional) — Inter 13px in `--fg-2`.

## variants and states

Two sizes: `md` (default) for settings lists and panels, `sm` for dense rows and tables.

<Example label="two sizes">
  <Sizes />
</Example>

| state | looks |
| --- | --- |
| Off | Dark track, `--fg-dim` thumb at the left |
| On | `--cloud-blue` track, dark thumb at the right |
| Focused | 2px `--focus-ring` outline, 2px around the thumb; keyboard focus only |
| Disabled | Track and label at 50% opacity; cannot be focused, changed or submitted |

There is no hover style and no invalid look.

## behaviour

- A click on the label text toggles it too. A switch is one tab stop, and `space` flips it.
- If applying can fail, set the switch back and say why.

The keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-inputs-switch--docs).

## accessibility

- Screen readers announce it as a switch that is on or off.
- Its label names it. Without a visible label, give it an `aria-label`; nothing enforces it today.
- The label must not change with the state — the announced on or off already says it.
- If the change takes time, or shows somewhere else, confirm it with a <C slug="toast" /> or a <C slug="message-strip" />. The switch only announces its own state.
- Today: the focus ring is drawn around the thumb. On an on switch it falls mostly on the `--cloud-blue` track, where `--focus-ring` contrasts about 1.1:1, and only a 1px edge shows against the page — keyboard focus on an on switch is hard to see.
- Today: there is no invalid state. Inside a <C slug="field" /> with an error, the switch does not change.
- Today: there are no forced-colours (Windows high contrast) styles.

## content

- Name the setting, not the action, in sentence case: `Email alerts`, `Maintenance mode` — not `Turn on email alerts`.
- Keep the label the same in both states. No `on`, `off`, `enabled` or `disabled` in it.
- Phrase it so that on means the feature runs: `Email alerts`, not `Mute email alerts`.
- When the effect is not obvious, say it in a <C slug="field" /> hint: `Visitors see a maintenance notice`.
