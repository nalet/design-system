# message strip

a message about the content of the page: a square strip with a state rule, a glyph, one or two human sentences, a way forward, and the technical part behind a `details` disclosure. only its text is a live region.

<Canvas of={MessageStripStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- for a condition of the page's content that lasts until it is resolved or dismissed: `105 titles can't be checked: the catalog holds them without an identity.`
- for an error from loading or from an action, with a way forward: `couldn't load the list.` and `try again`.
- above the content it is about. at most one or two at a time: more than that, and none of them reads as important.

## when not to use

- **to confirm an action someone just took** — `8 episodes queued`. use a [toast](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-toast--docs): it says so and leaves.
- **for one item** — a field's error, a row's state. use the [field](?path=/docs/components-inputs-field--docs) error text, or an [object status](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-object-status--docs) in the row.
- **in place of a list that has nothing to show** — it is empty, or it couldn't load and there is nothing older to show. that is an [illustrated message](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-illustrated-message--docs) where the list would be. keep the strip for content that is still on screen.
- **to stop everything for a decision.** use a [modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs).
- **for help that is always true about one control.** that is a [field](?path=/docs/components-inputs-field--docs) hint.

## anatomy

1. **container** — a flex row with 12px padding (16px on the left), a 3px rule on the left in the state's colour (`--state-*`), a 1px `--state-*-border` frame, a `--state-*-bg` fill and square corners. inter 13px / 1.5 in `--fg`.
2. **glyph** — the state's 14px glyph in its colour, set 3px down so it sits on the first line. hidden from assistive tech.
3. **text** — your `children`, and the live region. it takes the free width, at least 24ch before the actions wrap under it.
4. **actions** (optional) — after the text, 8px apart. they wrap under the text when there is no room.
5. **details toggle** (with `details`) — a quiet text button: jetbrains mono 12px in `--fg-muted`, 28px tall, with a 10px caret that turns down while the details are open.
6. **details** (with `details`) — 8px under the text: jetbrains mono 12px / 1.55 in `--fg-muted`, 8px × 12px padding and a 1px `--state-*-border` rule on the left. line breaks are kept, and long ids break anywhere.
7. **dismiss** (with `onDismiss`) — a 28px square button at the right edge, with a 12px × in `--fg-muted`.

## variants and states

five states; `information` is the default.

| state | announced | use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `neutral` | politely (`role="status"`), the text alone | a plain fact about the content: what runs on its own, what is switched off |
| `information` | politely, "information:" and the text | under way, or worth knowing: the list is being refreshed, an index is rebuilding |
| `success` | politely, "success:" and the text | a good condition that lasts — every source answered in the last hour — or what a save changed on this page: `saved. times on this page now show in utc.` a plain confirmation is a toast |
| `warning` | politely, "warning:" and the text | works, but someone should look: titles that can't be checked, sources over their limit, a refresh that failed while the last data still shows |
| `error` | at once (`role="alert"`), "error:" and the text | failed or blocked, needs action: an action that failed, a list that couldn't load. always give a way forward |

### details

`details` puts the technical part — ids, raw errors, http codes — behind a `details` disclosure, in mono. it is closed until someone opens it; `defaultDetailsOpen` opens it on the first render. it sits outside the live region, so an announcement never reads it out.

<Canvas of={MessageStripStories.WithDetails} />

### dismiss

`onDismiss` adds the dismiss button. the strip doesn't hide itself: it calls `onDismiss`, and you stop rendering it. offer it for a message people can read and move on from — what a save changed, an action that failed — and leave it off a condition that is still true, like a list that can't load. the strip doesn't remember a dismissal: the screen does, and brings the strip back when its condition changes again.

## behaviour and keyboard

the strip's own controls are native buttons. the order is: your actions, the details toggle, dismiss.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | moves through the actions, the details toggle and dismiss. the text takes no focus. |
| `enter` / `space` | on the details toggle: shows or hides the details. on dismiss: calls `onDismiss`. on your actions: whatever they do. |

- `esc` does nothing. a strip is part of the page, not an overlay.
- the strip keeps the details' open state itself. `defaultDetailsOpen` is read once, on the first render.
- the strip announces its text when it appears or changes; what that means for each state is under accessibility.

## accessibility

- **only the text is live.** `role="status"` or `role="alert"` sits on the text alone, with `aria-atomic="true"`: an update reads the whole message, and never the actions, the details or dismiss.
- **the role follows the state.** `error` is an alert (assertive: it interrupts); every other state is a status (polite: it waits for a pause). `live` overrides that: `"polite"` gives a status, `"assertive"` an alert, and `"off"` no role at all, for a strip that is on the page from the start and doesn't change. it is then read with the page, like any text.
- **the state word.** every state but `neutral` reads its name before the text, from visually hidden text: "warning: 105 titles can't be checked". a `stateLabel` string replaces the word (and gives `neutral` one); `false` leaves it out. an [object status](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-object-status--docs) names only `warning` and `error`.
- the glyph is hidden from assistive tech. each state has its own glyph shape, so the state never rests on colour alone.
- the details toggle has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` pointing at the details. closed details are `hidden`: not read and not focusable.
- dismiss is named by `dismissLabel` (default "dismiss") and is 28px square, above the 24px minimum target size. the toggle and dismiss show a 2px `--focus-ring` outline.
- axe-core finds no violations in any state, with actions, open details and dismiss.

today:

- a live region announces changes to content it already holds. a polite strip that appears together with its text may go unannounced in some screen readers; an alert is announced as it appears.
- dismissing removes the button that had focus, and focus falls back to the page. move it yourself in `onDismiss`, to the content's heading or the next control.
- the toggle and dismiss carry the same names on every strip. with two strips on a page, set `detailsLabel` and `dismissLabel` so they can be told apart.

## content

- **the text** — one or two human sentences: what is true, then what it means or what happens next. `105 titles can't be checked: the catalog holds them without an identity.` lowercase. no ids, codes or raw errors.
- **numbers** — exact, with thousands separators: `2,500`, not `2.5k`.
- **an error** says what failed and offers the way forward: `couldn't load the list.` and `try again`. when the last data still shows, keep it, and make the strip a `warning` that says how old it is: `couldn't refresh the list; it shows what was loaded 4m ago.`
- **actions** — one or two small ghost buttons (`size="sm" variant="ghost"`), each a verb: `show them`, `try again`, `ask the catalog again`. never `primary`: the view's one emphasized action is elsewhere.
- **details** — the raw part as it came: `GET /api/missing: http 502 bad gateway`, `request id 7d1c0e4a`. line breaks are kept.

  </Dont>

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="a condition that lasts. it stays until it is resolved or dismissed." column>
    <MessageStrip live="off" state="warning">2 sources are over their daily query limit until midnight. searches use the other 3.</MessageStrip>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="the confirmation of something just done is a toast. as a strip it stays, and pushes the list down." column>

  </Dont>
