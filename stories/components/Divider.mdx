# divider

a 1px rule in `--border` (`--border-2` when `heavy`), horizontal or vertical. a horizontal rule can carry a short mono label on the line.

<Canvas of={DividerStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- to separate two groups of content in the same container, when space alone doesn't make the break clear.
- vertically, between groups of short items in a row: counts, sizes, a timestamp.
- with a label, for a quiet break in a long list: `older`, `archived`.

## when not to use

- **as a frame or an edge.** to outline a region, use the border of a [box](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-box--docs) or a [card](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-card--docs). a divider is a break between things, not a box around them.
- **for a section title.** a label on the line isn't read as a heading (see accessibility). use a [heading](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-heading--docs).
- **between every item of a list.** space already separates the items: use a [stack](?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-stack--docs) `gap`. a [table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-table--docs) rules its own rows.

## anatomy

1. **rule** — 1px of `--border`, or `--border-2` when `heavy`. horizontal: the full width. vertical: 1px wide, stretched to the height of its row, at least 16px tall.
2. **label** (optional, horizontal only) — centered between two rules, 12px from each: jetbrains mono 12px, letter-spacing 0.04em, `--fg-dim`, rendered lowercase, never wraps.

the divider has no margin. the space around it comes from its parent's `gap`.

## behaviour and keyboard

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` | skips it. a divider takes no focus and has no action. |

## accessibility

- renders a `div` with `role="separator"`, so screen readers announce a separator. the unlabelled divider also sets `aria-orientation` to match its orientation.
- today: the label sits inside the separator, and a separator's children are presentational, so most screen readers won't read the label — and no `aria-label` is set for it. if the words matter, pass the same text as `aria-label`, or better, use a heading.
- a divider that only decorates — inside something whose structure is already clear — can be hidden from assistive tech with `aria-hidden="true"`, so it isn't announced.
- the rule is a decorative hairline, about 1.1:1 to 1.5:1 against the canvases, by design. never let a divider be the only thing that tells two groups apart: space and headings carry the structure.

## content

- a label is one or two lowercase words naming what follows: `older`, `archived`, `2023`.
- no punctuation, no sentences, nothing people need to act on.

## props

`Divider` passes every native `div` attribute through and forwards its ref. what you pass wins over the defaults, including `role`, `aria-orientation` and `aria-label`.
