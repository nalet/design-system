# modal

a square dialog over a dim backdrop for one task that needs an answer before the page can go on. it keeps `tab` inside, closes on `esc` and on a backdrop click, locks page scroll and gives focus back to the element that opened it.

<Example label="confirm a delete, live">
  <Confirm client:visible />
</Example>

## when to use

- to confirm an action that deletes something or can't be undone: `delete volume`, `revoke token`.
- to collect two or three inputs for one task without leaving the page: `add server`.
- when the next step depends on the answer, and nothing else on the page should happen until it is given.

## when not to use

- **to report that something happened.** use a <C slug="toast" /> for a passing confirmation, or a <C slug="message-strip" /> for a message that stays on the page. a dialog that only says `saved` interrupts and asks nothing.
- **in front of an action that can be undone.** do it, and confirm it with a <C slug="toast" />; the way back is the opposite action on the page (toasts carry no actions).
- **for a long form or several steps.** give it a page; see [forms and settings](/design-system/patterns/forms-and-settings/).
- **for a short explanation.** an optional detail fits a <C slug="tooltip" />; help for an input is the <C slug="field" /> hint.
- **to offer a list of actions.** use an <C slug="action-menu" />.

## anatomy

1. **backdrop** — covers the viewport in the canvas colour (`--bg`) at 72% opacity.
2. **dialog** — `--surface` fill, 1px `--border-2`, square corners, centred. 480px wide at most by default; it is never taller than the viewport minus 48px and keeps 16px from the viewport's edges.
3. **header** (with a title only) — 12px × 16px padding and a 1px `--border` rule below. it holds:
   - **title** — an `h2` in jetbrains mono 600, 15px, `--fg`, shown lowercase.
   - **close button** — 26px square, an `×` in `--fg-muted`, named "close".
4. **body** — 16px padding, inter 14px / 1.55 in `--fg-2`. it scrolls when the content is taller than the dialog; the header and the footer stay in place.
5. **footer** (optional) — the actions, right-aligned with 8px between them, 12px × 16px padding and a 1px `--border` rule above.

## variants and states

| setup | how | use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| confirm | a title, one or two sentences, a footer with `cancel` and the action | an action that deletes or can't be undone |
| form | a title, fields in the body, a footer | a few inputs for one task |
| no title | a name for assistive tech instead of a title | a short readout. there is no header, so no close button: put one in the footer |
| persistent | `esc` and the backdrop don't close it | work that a stray key must not hide. the header's close button and the footer still close it |

a form in the body can be submitted from the footer, and `enter` in a field submits it.

## behaviour

- **on open**, focus moves to the first control in the dialog: with a title, that is the header's close button; without one, the first control in the body or the footer, or the dialog itself when there is none.
- **while it is open**, `tab` and `shift+tab` stay in the dialog: from its last control, focus wraps to the first, and back. the page behind doesn't scroll.
- **closing** — `esc`, a click on the backdrop and the close button close it; a persistent dialog ignores `esc` and the backdrop. an open <C slug="action-menu" /> in the dialog takes the first `esc`, and the dialog closes on the next.
- **on close**, focus returns to the element that had it when the modal opened.

the keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs).

## accessibility

- the title names the dialog. without a title, give it a name for assistive tech instead: with neither, the dialog has no name.
- the title is always an `h2`.
- the close button is named "close" and is 26px square, above the 24px minimum target size.
- a click on plain text puts focus on the dialog itself; `tab` and `shift+tab` from there stay inside.

today:

- you can't choose where focus starts. with a title it is always the close button.
- screen readers announce the title, not the consequence, so make the title say what happens (`delete volume`, not `are you sure?`).
- one modal at a time: every open modal listens for `esc`, so one press closes them all.
- the scroll lock doesn't make up for the scrollbar's width, so the page behind can shift sideways when the modal opens.
- if the opener is gone when the modal closes (you deleted its row), focus has nowhere to return to. move it yourself, to the next sensible element.

## content

- **title** — a verb and its object, in the same words as the control that opened it: `delete volume`, `add server`. not a question, no full stop. the lowercase look is css, so write it as it should be read.
- **body** — one or two sentences: what happens, to what, and whether it can be undone. name the object: `this permanently deletes storage-01 and its 3 snapshots.`
- **actions** — the confirming button repeats the title's verb (`delete volume`); the way out is `cancel`, as a ghost button. the confirming action goes last, on the right. `danger` only for an action that deletes or can't be undone.
