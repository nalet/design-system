## implementation notes

- renders a `<div>` with `data-state`: the glyph, the text, `actions`, the details toggle, the details and dismiss. its own controls are native buttons, in this order: your actions, the details toggle, dismiss.
- the text alone is the live region: `role="status"` (polite) by default, `role="alert"` for `error`, with `aria-atomic="true"`, so an update reads the whole message and never the actions, the details or dismiss.
- `live` overrides the role: `"polite"` gives a status, `"assertive"` an alert, and `"off"` no role at all, for a static note — a strip that is on the page from the start and doesn't change.
- every state but `neutral` puts its name, visually hidden, before the text. a `stateLabel` string replaces the word (and gives `neutral` one); `false` leaves it out.
- the glyph is `aria-hidden`.
- `details` is a disclosure: the toggle has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` pointing at the details, and closed details are `hidden`, so they are neither read nor focusable. the details sit outside the live region. `detailsLabel` names the toggle (default "details").
- the strip keeps the details' open state itself. `defaultDetailsOpen` opens them on the first render, and is read only then.
- `onDismiss` adds the dismiss button, named by `dismissLabel` (default "dismiss"). the strip doesn't hide itself: it calls `onDismiss`, and you stop rendering it.
- the toggle and dismiss show a 2px `--focus-ring` outline on `:focus-visible`.
- other `<div>` attributes (all but `title`) and the `ref` reach the container.
- axe-core finds no violations in any state, with actions, open details and dismiss.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | moves through the actions, the details toggle and dismiss. the text takes no focus. |
| `enter` / `space` | on the details toggle: shows or hides the details. on dismiss: calls `onDismiss`. on your actions: whatever they do. |

- dismissing removes the button that had focus: move focus yourself in `onDismiss`.
- with two strips on a page, set `detailsLabel` and `dismissLabel` so they can be told apart.
