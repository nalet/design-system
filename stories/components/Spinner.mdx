# spinner

a square-cornered ring that turns: the one moving thing in an otherwise still interface. it says "working on it" when you can't say how long. it renders `role="status"`, named by its `label`.

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- for a short wait of unknown length in a small region: one value, a count, a panel's content.
- in place of a value while it loads, with a label that names the value.
- beside a short line of text that says what is loading.

## when not to use

- **for an action the user started.** use the `loading` state of the [button](?path=/docs/components-actions-button--docs) (or [icon button](?path=/docs/components-actions-icon-button--docs)). the spinner sits inside the control that was pressed, the button is disabled against a second press, and `aria-busy` is set.
- **while a table's rows load.** use the [data table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-data-table--docs) with `loading`: it marks the table busy (`aria-busy`) and runs a progress rule along its top edge, so the wait shows where the rows will be.
- **for the result.** when the work ends, replace the spinner with what happened: the content, a [toast](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-toast--docs) for a background job that finished, a [message strip](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-message-strip--docs) for a failure.
- **for long work with known progress.** today there is no progress bar. say the progress in [text](?path=/docs/components-data-display-text--docs) next to the spinner: `12 of 40 copied`.

## anatomy

1. **frame** — an inline-flex `span`, `size` × `size` px (default 16), in `--cloud-blue` unless you pass `color`.
2. **track** — the full ring in the same colour at 20% opacity.
3. **arc** — a quarter of the ring in full colour, with square ends.

the stroke is an eighth of the size: 2px at 16px, 5px at 40px.

## variants and states

`size` takes any number of pixels, and the stroke scales with it. buttons use 12px (small) and 14px (medium, large); 14px also sits well beside 13px text; 16px on its own; 24 to 40px for a panel.

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Sizes} />

the ring is `--cloud-blue` by default — it does not pick up the surrounding text colour on its own. `color` takes any css colour: a token such as `var(--fg-muted)`, or `currentColor` to follow the text around it.

## behaviour and keyboard

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` | skips it. a spinner takes no focus. |

- it turns once every 0.7s, at a steady speed, without end. with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it still turns, more slowly: once every 1.6s.
- it never stops by itself. remove it when the work ends.

## accessibility

- renders `role="status"` with `aria-label` set from `label` (default `loading`). the svg inside is `aria-hidden`.
- give the label the specific thing: `label="loading volumes"`, not the default.
- today: the label is a name, not text content, and a live region announces changes to its text. a spinner added to the page carries no text, so most screen readers won't announce it appearing. when the wait matters, put a short message in a live region that is already on the page and update its text.
- beside visible text that says the same thing, the label is read twice. pass `aria-hidden="true"` to the spinner and let the text speak.
- the ring meets 3:1 against every background (`--cloud-blue` is at least 6.8:1); the track is decoration. a custom `color` must keep 3:1.
- today: under reduced motion the ring slows down but doesn't stop.

## content

- the label says what is loading, lowercase, without an ellipsis: `loading volumes`, `checking storage-01`.
- text beside it uses the same words as the label. no "please wait".
- when a wait runs past a few seconds, say more: what is happening, and whether people can leave the page meanwhile.

## props

`Spinner` passes every native `span` attribute through and forwards its ref. `style` is merged over its size and colour, and attributes you pass win over its own, including `role` and `aria-label`.
