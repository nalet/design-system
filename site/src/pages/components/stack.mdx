# stack

A stack lines things up in a column or a row, with even space between them. It is the workhorse for laying out everything else.

<Example label="three items in a column, the usual gap apart">
  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- To line things up in one direction, with even space between them: the fields of a form, a row of badges, a header with a title and an action.
- Wherever you would otherwise build a row or a column with a gap in <Term id="css">CSS</Term>.

## when to use something else

- **For a grid of items in rows and columns.** A stack runs in one direction only. Tiles on the <Term id="home">home page</Term> go in a <C slug="tile-group" />; for other grids, use a grid in CSS, as [layout](/design-system/foundations/layout/#grid-and-layout) describes.
- **For a row of actions.** Use a <C slug="toolbar">toolbar</C>. It gives one action the emphasis, and moves what doesn't fit into an <Term id="overflow-menu">overflow menu</Term>.
- **For space around things, or a background.** A stack only spaces its children apart. Space around them comes from a <C slug="box" /> or a <C slug="card" />.

## anatomy

1. **Container.** A column or a row. It can shrink to fit inside other layouts.
2. **Gap.** The space between the children, never around them: a step of the [space scale](/design-system/foundations/layout/#the-scale), the usual one unless you choose another.
3. **Children.** The things in the stack, laid out in the order they come in the page's code.

## variants and states

| setting | choices | unless you choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | A column, or a row | A column |
| Gap | A step of the space scale, from none to the largest | The usual step |
| Alignment, across the stack | The start, the centre, the end, stretched, or lined up on the line their text sits on | Stretched |
| Spread, along the stack | Packed at the start, in the centre or at the end, pushed apart to both ends, or with even space around each child | Packed at the start |
| Wrapping | Children flow onto a new line when they run out of room | Off |
| Inline | The stack is only as wide as its content, and sits in the line of text | Off |

With no alignment chosen, children stretch across the stack: to the full width in a column, and to the full height in a row.

## behaviour

- A stack takes no <Term id="focus">focus</Term>. <Kbd>tab</Kbd> moves through its children in the order they come in the page's code.
- There is no reverse direction, so what people see, the order they read in and where focus goes all stay the same. Keep it that way: don't reorder the children with CSS.

The full keyboard map is on the stack's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-stack--docs).

## accessibility

- A stack adds no structure for <Term id="assistive-technology">assistive technology</Term>. To a <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term>, it isn't there at all.
- A stack of similar items isn't a list to a screen reader. When the items are a list, mark them up as one.

## content

A stack has no text of its own. Order its children the way people read and act: what the group is about first, and its actions last.

## specs

| setting | in code | values | default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | `direction` | `'vertical'` (a column), `'horizontal'` (a row) | `'vertical'` |
| Gap | `gap` | `0`, `4`, `8`, `12`, `16`, `24`, `32`, `48` or `64` (px), from 0 to 64px | `16` |
| Alignment, across | `align` (`align-items`) | `'start'`, `'center'`, `'end'`, `'stretch'`, `'baseline'` | Not set: children stretch |
| Spread, along | `justify` (`justify-content`) | `'start'`, `'center'`, `'end'`, `'between'` (`space-between`), `'around'` (`space-around`) | Not set: children pack at the start |
| Wrapping | `wrap` (`flex-wrap: wrap`) | On or off; meant for horizontal stacks | Off |
| Inline | `inline` (`display: inline-flex`) | On or off | Off |

- **Element:** a `div` with `display: flex`, `flex-direction: column` or `row`, `box-sizing: border-box` and `min-width: 0`, so it can shrink inside other flex and grid layouts. It has no role.
- **Gap:** between children only, from the space tokens: 4 `--s-1`, 8 `--s-2`, 12 `--s-3`, 16 `--s-4`, 24 `--s-5`, 32 `--s-6`, 48 `--s-7`, 64 `--s-8`. The first example is 16px apart; the gap example shows 4, 16 and 32px.
- **In CSS terms:** use it wherever you would write `display: flex` and a `gap`.
- **Order:** there is no reverse direction. Don't reorder children with CSS `order` or a reversed `flex-direction` (`row-reverse`).
- **Wrapping a column:** only with a fixed height.
- **A real list:** render `ul` and `li`, or pass `role="list"` to the stack and `role="listitem"` to each child.
