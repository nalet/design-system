## implementation notes

- Renders a native `<kbd>` and nothing else: no key listener, no focus. It is `inline-flex` and `white-space: nowrap`, with no `text-transform`, so the label shows as written.
- Every native `<kbd>` attribute passes through, and the `ref` reaches the `<kbd>`. Its children are the key's label.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` | Skips it. A keycap takes no focus. |
