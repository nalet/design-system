# heading

A heading names a page, or a section of it, in large <Term id="monospace">monospace</Term> type. The page's title can start with the blue <Term id="chevron">chevron</Term>.

<Example label="a page title with its first section">
  <SectionHeader />
</Example>

## when to use

- Once per page, for its title: the first <Term id="heading-level">level</Term>. On product screens, the title starts with the chevron.
- For the sections of a page: the second level.

## when to use something else

- **For the title of a card or a panel.** Use the <C slug="card" />'s own title.
- **For a third level, or deeper.** Today the heading stops at the second level. A sub-section gets its third-level heading from the <C slug="text" /> component instead.
- **For emphasis, or a big number.** Headings are the structure of a page: people who use a <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term> jump from one heading to the next. For emphasis, use the text component in its interface style.
- **For the product's name or logo.** Use the <Term id="lockup">lockup</Term>; [brand](/design-system/styles/brand/) shows it.

## anatomy

1. **Heading.** A real heading, of the first or the second level, with no space of its own around it. It is set in the page title's or the section title's <Term id="type-role">type role</Term> (see [typography](/design-system/styles/typography/#the-roles)): bright monospace, large for a page and a step smaller for a section.
2. **Chevron** (optional). The brand's mark, bold and blue, a short space before the text. <Term id="assistive-technology">Assistive technology</Term> doesn't see it.

## variants and states

The level sets both what the heading is and how big it looks, so a second-level heading always looks like one.

## behaviour

- A heading doesn't do anything, and the keyboard passes over it: it takes no focus and isn't in the tab order.
- Screen readers have their own keys for jumping from heading to heading, such as <Kbd>h</Kbd> while they read a page in browse mode. That is why the levels must follow the page's outline.

The keyboard map is on the heading's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-layout-structure-heading--docs).

## accessibility

- A heading is a real heading, so it is part of the page's outline, and screen readers can jump to it.
- One first-level heading per page, for its title; its sections are second-level headings. Pick the level for the outline, never for the size.
- The chevron is hidden from assistive technology, so the heading's name is your text alone. Don't type a chevron into the text yourself: a typed “>” is read out as “greater than”.
- The heading changes no letters to capitals or to lowercase: the text is shown and read exactly as you write it. Write it in lowercase, as [content & voice](/design-system/foundations/content-voice/) asks.

Today: there are only two levels.

## content

- A lowercase noun phrase, with no full stop: “volumes”, “recent runs”, “storage settings”.
- Keep it short. A heading that wraps onto a second line at the page title's size is too long.
- The chevron once, on the page's title. On every heading, it stops meaning anything.

## do and don't

  <Do column caption="The chevron once, on the page title.">
    <Stack gap={8}>
      <Heading level={1} chevron>volumes</Heading>
      <Heading level={2}>recent runs</Heading>
    </Stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="A chevron on every heading is decoration: it no longer marks the page.">
    <Stack gap={8}>
      <Heading level={2} chevron>recent runs</Heading>
      <Heading level={2} chevron>snapshots</Heading>
    </Stack>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="The heading's own chevron: it is blue, and it stays out of the heading's name.">
    <Heading level={1} chevron>volumes</Heading>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="A typed chevron is the wrong colour, and it is read aloud as “greater than”.">
    <Heading level={1}>{'> volumes'}</Heading>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do column caption="A short noun phrase that names the section.">
    <Heading level={2}>recent runs</Heading>
  </Do>
  <Dont column caption="A sentence as a heading wraps, and buries the word people scan for.">
    <Heading level={2}>here you can see all of the runs from the last week</Heading>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## specs

| level | in code | renders | type role and spec |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Page title | `level={1}` (the default) | a native `h1` | `h1`: JetBrains Mono 700, 32px, line height 1.15, letter-spacing -0.015em, `--fg` |
| Section | `level={2}` | a native `h2` | `h2`: JetBrains Mono 600, 22px, line height 1.2, letter-spacing 0, `--fg` |

- **Chevron:** `chevron`. A bold (700) `>` in `--cloud-blue`, 0.4em before the text, in a `span` with `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Spacing:** `margin: 0`.
- **Case:** no `text-transform`: the text is shown and read as written.
- **Third level:** none today. `Text` with `as="h3"` renders a native `h3`.
- **Emphasis:** `Text` with `variant="ui"`, not a heading.
- **Keyboard:** not focusable, not in the tab order.
