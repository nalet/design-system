# action menu

actions behind one trigger: a menu button and a menu of verbs, drawn above the page so no table cell or panel cuts it off. use one per table row, and as the overflow of a toolbar.

<Example label="a menu in a row, live">
  <InARow client:visible />
</Example>

## when to use

- for the actions on one object, behind one trigger per row: `pick a release`, `grab now`. one menu per row keeps a table calm.
- for the overflow of a toolbar. the <C slug="toolbar">toolbar</C> puts what doesn't fit behind `more actions` itself.
- for a few related commands behind a text trigger: `export`, then `as csv` or `as json`.

## when not to use

- **for the one emphasized action of a view.** show it as a <C slug="button" />; in a menu, nothing reads as the next step.
- **for actions on several selected rows.** use a <C slug="toolbar">selection toolbar</C>, which says how many it acts on.
- **to choose a value** — a sort order, a filter. a menu runs commands and doesn't show the current choice. use a <C slug="select" />, or a <C slug="multi-select" /> for several values.
- **to go to another page.** use a link. a menu item is a button, and can't be opened in a new tab.
- **to ask for a decision.** use a <C slug="modal" />.

## anatomy

1. **trigger** — by default a ghost <C slug="icon-button" /> with three 2.5px squares: 28px square at `sm` (the default), 34px at `md`. as a text trigger, a default <C slug="button" /> with your text and a trailing chevron.
2. **menu** — above everything else on the page: `--surface` fill, a 1px `--border-2` frame, square corners, 4px of padding above and below, and a shadow. at least 160px wide, or as wide as the trigger if that is wider; never wider than the viewport minus 16px.
3. **item** — at least 32px tall, 4px × 12px padding, jetbrains mono 13px in `--fg-2`, lowercase, on one line. hover and focus give it `--surface-2` and `--fg`; keyboard focus adds a 2px `--focus-ring` bar on its left edge.
4. **item icon** (optional) — decoration before the label, in `--fg-muted`.

## variants and states

the trigger is the icon (the default) or a text label. use the icon in a row, where the row already names the object; use text where the trigger stands on its own.

an item can be disabled — 45% opacity, it can't be run and the arrow keys skip it — or `danger`, for an action that deletes or can't be undone: its label stays `--signal-amber` at rest, on hover and on focus. put danger items last.

busy, the trigger shows a spinner and the menu can't open. disabled, the whole menu is off. either way the trigger can't be focused.

## behaviour

- the trigger opens a menu, which holds the focus until it closes. a click on the trigger opens or closes it.
- on the keyboard, the menu opens on its first item (on its last with `↑`), the arrow keys move between the items, and `esc` closes it with focus back on the trigger.
- choosing an item closes the menu and puts focus back on the trigger before the action runs, so a <C slug="modal" /> opened by the item returns focus there when it closes.
- a click on a disabled item does nothing, and the menu stays open.
- a press anywhere outside the menu and its trigger closes it; focus stays where that press put it.
- in a <C slug="modal" />, `esc` closes the menu and the dialog stays open.
- the menu opens 4px under the trigger. when it wouldn't fit below (it would come within 8px of the viewport's bottom edge) and there is room above, it opens above. it always keeps 8px from the viewport's left and right edges.
- no table cell, scroll container or panel that hides its overflow cuts the menu off. while it is open it follows the trigger when anything scrolls or the window resizes, and it never flashes in the wrong place.

the keyboard map and the attributes it sets are on its [react page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-actions-action-menu--docs).

## accessibility

- **name the object, not the action.** the icon trigger's name is `label`, which is also the browser's own tooltip: `actions for S04E07`, `actions for worker-2`. a table whose fifty triggers are all called "more" gives a screen-reader user fifty buttons they can't tell apart.
- the menu takes its name from its trigger.
- **with a text trigger, the visible text is the name**: what people see is what speech-input users say (wcag 2.5.3, label in name).
- danger is a colour only. the label has to say what happens (`delete worker`); for what can't be undone, ask in a <C slug="modal" /> once the item is chosen.
- item icons are hidden from assistive tech.

today:

- the arrow keys skip disabled items, so a keyboard user never lands on one and may not learn that it exists, or why it is off. say the reason on the page, in the row's status.
- no type-ahead: typing a letter doesn't move to the item that starts with it.
- no separators or groups inside the menu.
- there are no forced-colours (windows high contrast) styles. a focused item shows only a fill and an inset bar, and forced colours remove both.

## content

- **items** — a verb and its object, lowercase: `pick a release`, `grab now`, `open logs`, `delete worker`.
- **order** — the most used first; danger last.
- **the same shape on every row** — in a list, disable an item that doesn't apply to a row rather than leaving it out, and let the row's status say why.
- **the icon trigger's `label`** — `actions for` and the object's name as the row shows it: `actions for S04E07`.
- **a text trigger** — one or two words for what is behind it: `export`.

  <Do caption="verbs with their objects, the most used first, danger last.">
    <DemoMenu client:visible label="actions for storage-01" items={[{ id: 'snap', label: 'take a snapshot' }, { id: 'resize', label: 'resize volume' }, { id: 'delete', label: 'delete volume', danger: true }]} />
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="danger first and vague labels: people read “delete” before they learn what it deletes.">

  </Dont>
</DoDont>

  <Do caption="a value is chosen in a select, which shows the current choice.">
