# progress bar

A progress bar shows how far work has come when its size is known: a copy, an upload, a check of many files. A bar fills from the start, and the words under it say exactly how far. People read the words; the bar shows it at a glance.

  <Playground />
</Example>

## when to use

- For work that knows how much there is and how much of it is done: a copy, an upload, a scan.
- Where the work shows: in the panel or the row of the thing that is copied or checked, in the place where its result will stand.
- With “cancel” where stopping is safe, and with “pause” before it where stopping would lose what is done.

## when to use something else

- **For a wait of unknown length.** Use a <C slug="spinner" />. Until the work knows its total, a bar would stand empty, and a bar that doesn't move reads as stuck.
- **For an action that takes a moment**, like a save. Use the busy state of the <C slug="button" />: the wait then sits in the button that was pressed.
- **While a table's rows load.** The <C slug="data-table" /> shows that by itself.
- **For how full something is**, such as a disk. That is a measure, not work under way, and a progress bar promises an end. Say it in words: “1.4 of 2.2 gb used”.

## anatomy

1. **What is under way.** The first line, in the <Term id="body-face">body face</Term>: “Copying 3 files”. It is also the bar's name for a <Term id="screen-reader">screen reader</Term>.
2. **Cancel and pause** (optional). Quiet buttons at the end of the first line.
3. **Bar.** A track as wide as its container, thin and square at both ends. It fills from the start as the work gets done.
4. **The value in words.** Under the bar, in <Term id="monospace">monospace</Term>: the percentage, then the amount.
5. **The end**, in the value's place. When the work failed: the error's <Term id="glyph">glyph</Term>, a sentence that says what happened, and “try again”. When it is done: a check, and the result.

The drawing with the numbers is on [loading and progress](/design-system/patterns/loading-and-progress/#the-progress-bar).

## variants and states

The bar's colour follows the work, as a <C slug="status" /> does.

| state | the bar | under it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under way | Blue, the colour of information | The value in words: “62% · 1.4 of 2.2 gb” |
| Paused, or waiting | Stays where it is, in grey | A dash and “paused · 62%”, or the words for what it waits for |
| Failed | Stays where it stopped, in red | The error's cross, what happened and what to do next, and “try again” when it can be tried again |
| Done | Gone | A check and the result, in the item's own words: “copied · 2.2 gb” |

### cancel and pause

Work that is safe to stop gets “cancel”. Where stopping would lose what is done, “pause” comes before it, and turns into “resume” while the work is paused. A failed bar carries neither: its way forward is “try again”.

### waiting

When the work stalls, the words say so, with the reason when it is known. The bar turns grey, so it doesn't look as if it were still filling.

## behaviour

- **It shows only what is known.** The bar has no look for work of unknown size. Until the work knows its total, show the spinner.
- **It moves at a true pace.** A bar that runs to 90% in seconds and then waits for minutes makes people wonder whether the work stopped. The bar never says 100% before the work is done: the last percent stays open until it is.
- **The filled part slides** to its new place in a fifth of a second. With <Term id="reduced-motion">reduced motion</Term> it changes without sliding; the value still shows.
- **The bar holds no state of its own.** The app tells it how far the work is and how it ended, and does the stopping, the pausing and the trying again.
- **The buttons take <Term id="focus">focus</Term>** in reading order: pause or resume, then cancel; on a failed bar, try again. <Kbd>enter</Kbd> or <Kbd>space</Kbd> presses them. The bar itself takes no focus.

What to show while people wait, and when a bar is the right answer, is in [loading and progress](/design-system/patterns/loading-and-progress/). The full keyboard map is on the progress bar's [React page](/design-system/react/?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-progress-bar--docs).

## accessibility

- **It is the browser's own progress element**, so a screen reader says that it is a progress bar. Its name is the first line, and its value is in words: “62%, 1.4 of 2.2 GB”.
- **It is read when people come to it**, not every few percent. The words under the bar are for the eye only; a screen reader gets them once, from the bar.
- **The end is said once.** When the work fails or is done, the sentence or the result under the bar is announced, politely, at the screen reader's next pause.
- **A paused or a failed bar says so**: “paused, 62%”, “stopped at 62%”.
- **Not by colour alone.** Grey comes with a dash and the word “paused”, red with a cross and a sentence, and the result with a check.
- **The filled part stands out from the track** with a <Term id="contrast">contrast</Term> of at least 3 to 1 in every state: the blue 5.14, the grey 6.46 and the red 3.88 to 1.
- **Without a first line**, the bar needs a name that is read out but not shown, or a <C slug="field" /> around it, whose label then names it. Nothing checks for one.

Today:

- A bar that is already failed or done when it appears isn't announced: nothing changed while people were there. It is read with the page.
- “cancel”, “pause” and “try again” have the same names on every bar. A screen reader reads the bar's first line just before them, which is what tells two bars apart.
- There are no styles for <Term id="forced-colours">forced colours</Term>, the high-contrast setting some operating systems offer.

## content

- **The first line.** What is under way, in the item's own words, in <Term id="sentence-case">sentence case</Term>: “Copying 3 files”, “Checking 2,400 files”. No “please wait”.
- **The value.** The bar writes the percentage itself. Give it the amount, with <Term id="thousands-separator">thousands separators</Term> and the unit: “1.4 of 2.2 GB”, “880 of 2,400 files”. Together they read “62% · 1.4 of 2.2 gb”.
- **Waiting.** What it waits for, then how far it is: “waiting for the other disk · 62%”.
- **Failed.** What happened and what to do next, as a sentence: “Couldn't finish: the disk is full. Free 0.9 GB, then try again.” No codes; they belong behind the details of a <C slug="banner" />.
- **Done.** The result in the item's own words, like a status: “copied · 2.2 gb”.
- **Another language.** The percentage and the buttons follow the page's language: “62 %” and “abbrechen” on a German page.

## specs

| state | in code | the bar | announced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Under way | `state="running"` (the default) | `--state-information` | Read when people come to it: `aria-valuetext`, “62%, 1.4 of 2.2 GB” |
| Paused | `state="paused"` | `--state-neutral`, at its value | “paused, 62%”, or `valueText` |
| Failed | `state="failed"` | `--state-error`, at its value | “stopped at 62%” on the bar; `message` once, in the `role="status"` under it |
| Done | `state="done"` | Removed | `message` once, in the `role="status"` |

| part | spec |
| --- | --- |
| Box | A column as wide as its container, 8px (`--s-2`) between the first line, the bar and the line under it. It carries `data-state`. |
| First line | `label`. Inter 13px, line height 1.4, `--fg`. It names the bar (`aria-labelledby`). |
| Cancel, pause, resume | Shown for `onCancel`, `onPause` (under way) and `onResume` (paused), at the end of the first line, 8px apart. Quiet buttons: JetBrains Mono 600, 12px, lowercase, `--fg-2`, 28px high, padding 0 8px; on hover `--surface-2` and `--fg`. The first line keeps the height of its text. |
| Bar | A native `<progress>` over a track 4px high (`--s-1`) in `--border-2`, square. The filled part starts at the track's start, `value` ÷ `max` of its width. |
| Value | JetBrains Mono 12px, line height 1.4, lowercase, `--fg`; `aria-hidden`. The percentage is rounded, and 99 at most until `value` reaches `max`. With `amount`: `{percent} · {amount}`. Paused: the neutral glyph, 12px in `--state-neutral`, and `paused · {percent}`. `valueText` replaces the words. |
| Failed | The error glyph, 12px in `--state-error`, 6px before `message`: Inter 13px, line height 1.5, `--fg`. Without `message`: `stopped at {percent}`. |
| Try again | Shown for `onRetry` while failed, after the message, under it when there is no room. A framed small button: `--surface`, a 1px `--border-2` frame, `--fg`, 28px high, padding 0 12px; on hover `--surface-2` and a `--cloud-blue` frame. |
| Done | The success glyph, 12px in `--state-success`, and `message` in JetBrains Mono 12px, lowercase, `--fg`. |
| Focus | The buttons: the 2px `--focus-ring` outline, 1px outside, on keyboard focus only. |

| screen | in code | what changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Touch | `data-nc-screen="touch"` on an ancestor | Each button answers in 48 × 48 (`3rem`), centred on it and not drawn. The line that holds a button is 48px high. |
| TV | `data-nc-screen="tv"` on an ancestor | The first line and the failed sentence are 14px. The buttons are 42px high with 14px text and 24px of padding, 12px apart. Focused: a 3px `--focus-ring` outline 2px outside, `--surface-focus`, and 1.05 times the size, in 150ms `ease-out`. |

- **Motion:** the filled part changes its width in 200ms `ease`; the buttons' colours in 120ms. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` nothing is animated, and a focused button on a TV doesn't grow.
- **Contrast, on the `--border-2` track:** `--state-information` 5.14:1, `--state-neutral` 6.46:1, `--state-error` 3.88:1.
- **Words:** “cancel”, “pause”, “resume”, “try again”, “paused” and the percent sign's place come from the page's language, `words.progressBar`; English and German are built in.
- **Sizes** are in `rem`, so the bar, the text and the buttons grow with the reader's text size.
- **In a field:** the field's label names the bar, its hint or error is the bar's description, and an error draws the bar as failed.
- **Not there:** no look for work of unknown size, no sizes, no `forced-colors` styles.
