## implementation notes

- `Worklist` lays the parts out, and the parts are the components: `tabs` are `FilterTab`s, `messages` are message strips, `filterBar` is a `FilterBar`, `toolbar` is a `Toolbar` or a `SelectionToolbar`, and `children` is the `DataTable`, or the `EmptyState` when there is nothing to show.
- It renders a `section` named by its title.
- `headingLevel` sets the title's level: 1 when the worklist is the page, and 2 inside an app shell that owns the `h1`.
- With `tabs` and `activeTab`, the tabs are a `tablist`, and the content below them is its `tabpanel`, labelled by the selected tab.
- `tabsLabel` names the tab list (“filter the list” by default).
- The toolbar and the table header stick while the list scrolls (`stickyToolbar`, on by default), so the actions and the column headers stay in view.
- To do that, the worklist measures its toolbar and offsets the table's sticky header under it with `--nc-dt-sticky-top`.
- Pass the tab and the filters as the table's `growingResetKey`. Growing then starts over when the view changes, and a refresh of the data keeps the person's place.
- Give the table an `aria-label` and a `rowLabel`, so each checkbox and each row menu says what it acts on.
- It passes other `section` attributes and its `ref` through.
