## implementation notes

- It renders a `<div>` with `data-state`, holding the glyph, the text, `actions`, the details toggle, the details and dismiss.
- Its own controls are native buttons, in this order: your actions, the details toggle, then dismiss.
- Only the text is the live region: `role="status"` (polite) by default, and `role="alert"` for `error`.
- The live region has `aria-atomic="true"`, so an update reads the whole message, and never the actions, the details or dismiss.
- `live` overrides the role: `"polite"` gives a status, `"assertive"` an alert, and `"off"` no role at all.
- Use `"off"` for a static note: a strip that is on the page from the start and doesn't change.
- Every state but `neutral` puts its name, visually hidden, before the text, so a screen reader hears the state as well as the message.
- A `stateLabel` string replaces that word, and gives `neutral` one. `false` leaves it out.
- The glyph is `aria-hidden`, so a screen reader skips it.
- `details` is a disclosure: the toggle has `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` pointing at the details.
- Closed details are `hidden`, so they are neither read nor focusable.
- The details sit outside the live region.
- `detailsLabel` names the toggle (“details” by default).
- The strip keeps the details' open state itself. `defaultDetailsOpen` opens them on the first render, and is read only then.
- `onDismiss` adds the dismiss button, named by `dismissLabel` (“dismiss” by default).
- The strip doesn't hide itself: it calls `onDismiss`, and you stop rendering it.
- The toggle and dismiss show a 2px `--focus-ring` outline on `:focus-visible`.
- Other `<div>` attributes, all but `title`, and the `ref` reach the container.
- axe-core finds no violations in any state, with actions, open details and dismiss.

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | Moves through the actions, the details toggle and dismiss. The text takes no focus. |
| `enter` / `space` | On the details toggle: shows or hides the details. On dismiss: calls `onDismiss`. On your actions: whatever they do. |

Today:

- Dismissing removes the button that had focus, so move focus yourself in `onDismiss`.
- With two strips on a page, set `detailsLabel` and `dismissLabel` so they can be told apart.
