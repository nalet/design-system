# tooltip

a small square label with a short, optional detail about what is under the pointer or the keyboard focus. it is css only: the bubble shows while its trigger is hovered or has keyboard focus.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Playground} />

## when to use

- to give the detail behind a compact value or term: the window behind `142 ms`, the full time behind `3 min ago`, what an abbreviation stands for.
- for information people can do without. anything they need to finish a task belongs on the screen.

## when not to use

- **to name an icon-only control.** an [icon button](?path=/docs/components-actions-icon-button--docs) takes a `label`, which becomes its accessible name and the browser's own tooltip (`title`).
- **for anything people need**: an error, a warning, the consequence of an action. there is no hover on touch screens, so people there never see the bubble. put it on the page — a [field](?path=/docs/components-inputs-field--docs) hint or error, a [message strip](?path=/docs/components-feedback-status-message-strip--docs).
- **for more than a short phrase.** the bubble doesn't wrap. write it on the page, or in a [modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs) when it needs a decision.
- **for links, buttons or anything else to act on.** the bubble can't be hovered or focused, and it disappears when the pointer leaves the trigger. offer the actions on the page, or behind an [action menu](?path=/docs/components-actions-action-menu--docs).

## anatomy

1. **wrapper** — an inline-flex `<span>` around your trigger. it is focusable (`tabindex="0"`) and points at the bubble with `aria-describedby`.
2. **trigger** — your `children`: a value or a term.
3. **bubble** — `role="tooltip"`. `--surface-2` fill, 1px `--border-2`, square, 4px × 8px padding; jetbrains mono 12px / 1.4 in `--fg`; one line, at most 240px wide. it sits 6px from the trigger, centred on it, at `z-index: 50`, with no arrow.

## variants and states

`side` puts the bubble `top` (the default), `bottom`, `left` or `right` of the trigger, centred along it. nothing flips or shifts it near the edge of the viewport, and an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` cuts it off: choose the side with room.

the bubble fades in over 100ms when the trigger is hovered or gets keyboard focus, and hides at once when both end. there is no delay before it shows.

## behaviour and keyboard

there is no script: css shows the bubble while the wrapper is hovered or has keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`).

| key | does |
| --- | --- |
| `tab` / `shift+tab` | moves focus to the wrapper, which shows the bubble. when your trigger is itself focusable, the next `tab` moves into it and the bubble hides. |
| `esc` | nothing. today the bubble can't be dismissed from the keyboard; it hides when focus leaves the wrapper. |

- pointer: hovering the wrapper shows the bubble, leaving it hides it. the bubble ignores the pointer (`pointer-events: none`), so it can't be hovered, selected or clicked.

## accessibility

- the bubble has `role="tooltip"`, and the wrapper refers to it with `aria-describedby`. the description belongs to the wrapper, not to your trigger.
- the wrapper always adds a tab stop. on a value or a term, that is what makes the detail reachable by keyboard. on a button it means two tab stops for one control, and the button itself gets no description.

today:

- the focused wrapper draws no focus ring (`outline: none`); the bubble appearing is the only sign of focus.
- `esc` doesn't hide the bubble, and the pointer can't rest on it: two of the three things wcag 1.4.13 asks of content that appears on hover or focus. keep only optional detail in it.
- text longer than 240px doesn't wrap; it runs out of the bubble.

## content

- one short phrase, lowercase, no full stop: `p95 over the last 5 minutes`, `built 2026-06-15 09:12`.
- add to the trigger; don't repeat it. a tooltip that says what the trigger already says is noise.
- plain text only: no links, no buttons, nothing that has to be read in a particular order.

  <Do caption="an optional detail behind a compact value.">

  </Dont>
</DoDont>

  <Do caption="an icon button names itself: its label is the accessible name and the native tooltip.">
    <IconButton label="restart worker-2"><Icon icon={RotateCw} /></IconButton>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="wrapped in a tooltip, it shows two tooltips (the native one and the bubble) and puts an extra tab stop in front of the button.">
    <Tooltip content="restart worker-2"><IconButton label="restart worker-2"><Icon icon={RotateCw} /></IconButton></Tooltip>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="one short line.">
    <Tooltip content="3 of 3 replicas ready"><Term>worker-2</Term></Tooltip>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="a sentence doesn't wrap: past 240px it runs out of the bubble. if it needs a sentence, it belongs on the page.">

  </Dont>
</DoDont>
