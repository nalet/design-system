# motion

Movement on our screens is small, quick, and only there to say something: that a control heard you, that a state changed, or that work is under way. Nothing bounces, slides in from far away or plays for show.

## what moves

| motion | where | what it says | with reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Colour change | Buttons, icon buttons, text fields, selects, text areas, checkboxes, the switch's track, tabs, filter tabs, the multi select's button, a dialog's close button | The control heard you: hover, press, focus, on | Unchanged: it is colour, not movement |
| Tile hover | A tile's surface and border change, and the brand chevron fades in | This tile is a way in | At once |
| Tooltip | Fades in, and hides at once | Here is the detail | Unchanged |
| Switch thumb | Slides across the track | The setting changed | Moves at once, without sliding |
| <Term id="disclosure">Disclosure</Term> arrow | Turns, in a message strip's details, a multi select and a data table group | Open, or closed | At once |
| Toast | Rises a few pixels as it fades in, and leaves at once | Something just happened | Appears at once |
| Spinner | The gap in its still square frame runs round at a steady pace, without end | Working on it | Stands still; its label carries the state |
| Busy line | On a data table that is loading, a short blue bar runs along the top edge, again and again | This list is being refreshed | A still line across the full width |
| Lockup cursor | The cursor block blinks: on for half of each beat, off for the other half | The brand's one cursor | Keeps blinking; see [accessibility](#accessibility) |

## what doesn't move

- Dialogs, action menus and the multi select's panel open and close at once. Only the multi select's arrow turns.
- Toasts and tooltips leave at once; only their arrival is animated.
- Sorting, selecting a row, columns popping in, the filter bar folding up and actions moving into the overflow menu all happen at once. Nothing animates a width, a height or the position of a row.
- The current tab's underline changes colour in place; it doesn't slide from tab to tab.
- The keyboard focus outline appears at once. A text field's blue focus border comes in with the usual colour change.
- Nothing scrolls smoothly: when the focus moves, the page jumps to it.

## durations and easing

Most changes take about a tenth of a second: fast enough to feel immediate, and slow enough to be seen. Each has its own <Term id="easing">easing</Term>, the way it speeds up and slows down.

- **80 <Term id="millisecond">ms</Term>, at an even speed:** the tile, a surface people sweep the pointer across.
- **100 ms, gently:** the tooltip's fade.
- **120 ms, gently:** every other change of state, colours and arrows.
- **140 ms, gently:** the switch's thumb, the one part of a control that travels.
- **160 ms, slowing down as it lands:** the toast, the one thing that arrives.
- **Loops:** the spinner, one lap in 4 seconds; the busy line, one run in 1.1 seconds; and the lockup's cursor, one blink in 1.05 seconds. They repeat for as long as they show.

Today: the durations and easings aren't shared values. Each component writes its own, so an app can't read them from the library; match the values above.

## how to apply it in your app

- Answer a person within about 120 ms, and never take longer than 160 ms. A reaction that takes longer feels like lag, not polish.
- Fade, or move a few pixels, as the toast does. Don't slide panels across the screen, or make things grow on hover.
- Run a loop only while something is busy, and stop it the moment the work is done. What to show while something loads is in [loading and progress](/design-system/patterns/loading-and-progress/).
- Don't animate the layout. Rows, columns and panels change at once, as the data table's do.
- Make every movement and every loop respect <Term id="reduced-motion">reduced motion</Term>: stop it, or show its end state, as the toast and the busy line do.
- To see the reduced versions, turn on reduced motion in your system's settings, or have the browser's developer tools pretend it is on.

## accessibility

When people turn on reduced motion:

- **These stop:** the toast's arrival, the arrows, the tile's hover and its chevron, the switch's thumb, the busy line (it becomes a still line), and the spinner (its frame stands still, three quarters lit, and its label carries the state).
- **These stay the same:** the colour changes and the tooltip's fade, which don't move anything.
- **This doesn't stop:** the lockup's cursor. The style meant to hold it still doesn't apply to this kind of animation, so it keeps blinking.

And:

- The lockup's cursor blinks for as long as the page is open. Anything that moves for more than five seconds next to other content needs a way to stop it (WCAG 2.2.2), so wherever the lockup stays in view, turn its cursor off; see [brand](/design-system/styles/brand/). On this site, the examples that keep moving have a pause button.
- Nothing flashes. The only thing that turns on and off is the cursor, less than once a second, far below the limit of three flashes a second.
- Motion never says something on its own. A loading data table also tells <Term id="assistive-technology">assistive technology</Term> that it is busy, a spinner is a status named “loading”, a toast is text that is read out, and a switch announces on or off.

## specs

| motion | duration, easing | moves | reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Colour change | 120ms `ease` | Colours on hover, press, focus and on | Unchanged |
| Tile hover | 80ms `linear` | Surface, border; the chevron fades in | Instant |
| Tooltip | 100ms `ease` | Fades in; hides at once | Unchanged |
| Switch thumb | 140ms `ease` | Slides 16px (14px at `sm`) | `transition: none` |
| Disclosure caret | 120ms `ease` | Turns 90° (message strip details, data table group), 180° (multi select) | Instant |
| Toast | 160ms `ease-out` | Rises 6px as it fades in; leaves at once | Appears at once |
| Spinner | 4s a lap, `linear` | The gap runs round the still frame | Stands still, three quarters lit |
| Busy rule | 1.1s a run, `ease-in-out` | A 2px `--cloud-blue` bar, 30% wide, along the top edge | A still 2px rule, full width |
| Lockup cursor | 1.05s a cycle, without end | On for half the cycle, off for the other half | Keeps blinking |

- **Reduced motion.** Every movement and loop goes behind `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
- **Lockup cursor.** Under reduced motion the stylesheet hides the cursor's `<animate>` element with `display: none`, but `display` doesn't apply to SVG animation elements, so the blink carries on. Pass `showCursor={false}` wherever the lockup stays in view.
- **Focus.** `--focus-ring` outlines appear at once; a field's `--cloud-blue` focus border comes in with its 120ms colour change.
- **Announced, not only moved.** A loading data table sets `aria-busy`; a spinner is a `status` named “loading”; a toast is text in a live region; a switch announces on or off.
- **Today:** no motion tokens. Each component writes its durations and easings in its own CSS.
