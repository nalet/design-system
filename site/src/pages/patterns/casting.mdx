# casting

<Term id="casting">Casting</Term> sends a title from the phone to a TV or a speaker on the same network. The TV plays it, and the phone becomes the remote. People cast from many apps, so they expect the same button and the same controls everywhere. We use Google's: the Cast icon, the bar while casting and the full controls, in our colours.

## the cast button

- It appears only when a device that can play is on the network. Without one, there is nothing to cast to, and the button would only raise a question.
- It sits in the top bar of the player and of a title's page, on the right.
- Its look is Google's icon: outlined when nothing is connected, moving while it connects, filled while casting. People know it from other apps, so we don't redraw it.

## while casting

<MiniController />

- **The bar.** Above the bottom navigation, never over it: the title, “playing on Living Room TV” with the device's own name, and play or pause. Tapping the bar opens the full controls.
- **The full controls.** The artwork, the title, the position with a seek bar, play or pause, back and forward 10 seconds, subtitles and audio when the TV offers them, and “stop casting”.
- **Outside the app.** The phone's notification and its lock screen show the title, the device, play or pause, and stop.
- **Handing over.** When casting starts, the title continues on the TV from where it was on the phone, and the phone's player closes. When people stop casting, the phone keeps the TV's position, so playing again continues from there.

## when the TV can't play a title

Some TVs can't play every kind of file. The app sends a version the TV can play whenever there is one. When there isn't, it says so in one line, with the device's name:

<Banner state="warning" live="off" details="the TV reported: unsupported media">Living Room TV can't play this title.</Banner>

Casting stops, and the title stays where it was on the phone, ready to play there.

## words

- The device's name comes from the network, as people named it: “Living Room TV”. Never a product name the device didn't give itself.
- Our words are “playing on …” and “stop casting”. The device chooser's own words are Google's.

## specs

- **Widgets.** Google's Cast library draws the button, the device chooser, the bar, the full controls and the notification. Its design checklist covers how they behave.
- **Colours.** The full controls on `--bg`, the bar on `--surface`, text in `--fg` and `--fg-muted`, the seek bar and the active controls in `--cloud-blue`. Labels and icons on a blue fill are dark, as everywhere.
- **Layout.** The bar is part of the screen's layout, above the bottom navigation, so nothing scrolls under it unseen.
- **On the TV.** The default receiver shows Google's own screen while it loads. There is no page of ours on the TV.
